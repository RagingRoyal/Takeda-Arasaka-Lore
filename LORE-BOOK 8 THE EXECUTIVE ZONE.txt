# LORE-BOOK: THE EXECUTIVE ZONE

# NORTH OAK, THE ESTATES, PRIVATE GOVERNMENT AND NIGHT CITY'S ULTRA-ELITE

## OVERVIEW

The Executive Zone is where Night City's ruling class goes when it no longer wishes to experience Night City.

It is not simply a wealthy neighborhood.

It is a deliberately insulated social environment built to separate the city's most powerful people from:

Crime.

Poverty.

Gangs.

Traffic.

Pollution.

Uncontrolled pedestrians.

Uninvited visitors.

Ordinary public services.

And, whenever possible, ordinary citizens.

During the Time of the Red, the Executive Zone existed as a formally recognized gated district on the hills east of Night City.

It was effectively an enormous fortified homeowners' community inhabited by the richest executives, celebrities, corporate leaders and power brokers in the city.

By 2077, the geography has changed.

The modern city map calls the surrounding luxury sub-district North Oak, part of Westbrook.

North Oak represents the expanded successor to the old Executive Zone system.

For roleplay purposes, the distinction should be understood as:

NORTH OAK:
The formal modern sub-district.

THE EXECUTIVE ZONE:
The ultra-exclusive estate community and private-governance culture that grew into modern North Oak.

Someone in 2077 might therefore say they live in North Oak geographically while referring socially to their gated estate community as the Executive Zone.

The terms overlap historically and culturally, but they are not technically identical on the 2077 municipal map.

Trees.

Dedicated security.

Domestic staff.

In one of the densest cities on Earth, empty space is one of the greatest possible displays of wealth.

Headquarters.

# THE EXECUTIVE ESTATES BEFORE THE FOURTH CORPORATE WAR

The Executive Zone did not appear from nothing.

Before the Fourth Corporate War, Westbrook already contained an extremely exclusive neighborhood known as the Executive Estates.

The Executive Estates catered primarily to senior corporate personnel.

It contained:

Multimillion-eurodollar mansions.

Large gardens.

Parks.

Greenbelts.

Golf courses.

Private AV landing areas.

Real vegetation.

Extensive private security.

An Arasaka-sponsored maglev line passed near the area.

Even wealthy passengers could not simply disembark there.

Access required an Executive Estates pass.

The idea was already established:

The elite required transportation infrastructure and residential territory ordinary Night Citizens could not freely enter.

# AFTER THE 2023 DISASTER

The destruction of central Night City during the Fourth Corporate War created enormous refugee populations.

People fled radioactive and devastated areas.

Temporary settlements appeared around surviving wealthy neighborhoods.

Refugees constructed:

Tent cities.

Shanties.

Improvised housing.

Temporary camps.

The contrast became extreme.

Corporate survivors lived behind protected walls.

Displaced citizens lived immediately outside them.

# THE CREATION OF THE EXECUTIVE ZONE

The wealthy residents of the old Executive Estates responded by increasing separation.

Large sections of the surrounding hills were enclosed.

Security walls were constructed.

Access was restricted.

Private protection expanded.

The result became the Executive Zone.

It was less a normal city district than a fortified residential enclave.

# THE TIME OF THE RED EXECUTIVE ZONE

By 2045, the Executive Zone had become one of the safest locations in Night City.

Night City's threat classification for the area was effectively unique:

EXECUTIVE.

The label communicates more than low crime.

It means local security conditions are determined by the people wealthy enough to rewrite normal expectations.

Approximately twenty major estates existed inside the district.

Fewer than one hundred people were considered true permanent residents.

Despite this tiny population, the district possessed enormous political influence.

# LESS THAN ONE HUNDRED PEOPLE

The Executive Zone had the smallest permanent residential population of any major Night City district.

This is essential to understanding it.

A Combat Zone can contain tens or hundreds of thousands of desperate residents.

The Executive Zone might contain fewer people than an ordinary apartment building.

Yet its residents include people controlling:

Corporations.

Infrastructure.

Media.

Energy.

Construction.

Technology.

Space transportation.

Private armies.

Political influence.

The Executive Zone's population is tiny.

Its combined power is enormous.

# WHO COUNTS AS A RESIDENT?

Being physically inside an estate does not necessarily make someone socially recognized as an Executive Zone resident.

True residents are generally:

Their immediate families.

Everyone else exists in a lower category.

# PROPERTY OWNERSHIP

During the Time of the Red, Executive Zone estates were not normal rental properties.

People did not casually rent a house there for six months.

To live there required either:

Enough money to purchase a place.

Or enough influence to force open a door money alone might not open.

Both were respected.

Being rich enough to purchase an estate demonstrated financial power.

Being connected enough to obtain one despite opposition demonstrated another kind of power.

# PROPERTY AS POLITICAL MEMBERSHIP

An Executive Zone estate is more than a residence.

Ownership provides entrance into a political community.

Your neighbors might include:

A megacorporate regional director.

The CEO of a technology company.

A legendary musician.

A corporate heiress.

Night City's most important fixer.

A weapons designer.

An orbital executive.

Social interactions between neighbors can therefore affect entire corporations.

# THE HOMEOWNERS ASSOCIATION

During 2045, the Executive Zone was governed by its Home Owners Association.

The HOA was not merely a suburban committee complaining about hedges.

It effectively functioned as the district government.

# HOA PRESIDENT

The elected president of the HOA served as the Executive Zone's City Manager.

This gave the HOA president a formal position inside Night City's municipal political structure.

The president therefore occupied two roles simultaneously:

Leader of the neighborhood association.

Political representative and administrator of the district.

# THE ABSURDITY OF REPRESENTATION

The Executive Zone could contain fewer than one hundred permanent residents.

Other Night City districts contained hundreds of thousands.

Yet the Executive Zone possessed representation within the city government's district system.

This means a neighborhood populated by the microscopic elite could exercise political influence wildly disproportionate to population.

Nobody in the Executive Zone considered this a problem.

# KAREN DAVIES

During 2045, Doctor Karen Davies served as President of the Executive Zone HOA and therefore City Manager.

Corporate position:

Regional Director of Pol-Bud.

Pol-Bud is a construction-materials corporation possessing significant industrial interests outside Night City.

Davies won her presidency by only one vote.

The election required multiple recounts.

That tiny margin demonstrates how powerful individual estate owners can become.

One neighbor changing sides may decide who controls an entire district.

# DAVIES' POLITICAL STYLE

Karen Davies represents the regulatory establishment.

She is intensely associated with:

Rules.

Standards.

Property management.

Institutional control.

Her own estate famously complied with HOA requirements down to the smallest details.

Her authority nevertheless exists inside a community filled with people powerful enough to resist her.

The president can control the HOA.

The president cannot casually order a megacorporate CEO around.

# HEATHER STEIN

Doctor Heather Stein was the previous HOA president.

Corporate affiliation:

Biotechnica.

Highly paid scientist and executive-level employee in Biotechnica's Home & Garden operations.

# THE REALGRASS WAR

The Executive Zone's famous green lawns were maintained using Biotechnica technology.

Because Heather Stein was closely associated with Realgrass, her political enemies discovered an absurdly Cyberpunk method of damaging her reputation.

Netrunners could interfere with the NET Architectures controlling:

Irrigation.

Fertilization.

Landscaping.

Grass health.

The objective was not destroying a military network.

It was making people's lawns turn yellow.

Why?

Because unhappy wealthy residents might blame Heather Stein.

The lawn becomes a campaign weapon.

That is Executive Zone politics.

# THE BRENTWOOD-TEALOV FEUD

One excellent example involves two neighboring corporate executives.

William Joseph "Billy Joe" Brentwood represented Petrochem's Night City leadership.

Jami "Sharkeater" Tealov represented SovOil's Night City operations.

Their estates bordered one another.

Tealov planted European white birch trees between the properties.

Brentwood hated them.

The HOA had approved the trees.

A normal homeowner might complain.

An executive with enormous money can begin looking for less official solutions.

Corporate energy rivalry had been reduced to landscaping.

The landscaping could still require professionals willing to break laws.

# PUDDLEFORGE VERSUS THE HOA

Another political dispute involved Puddleforge Games.

The Puddleforge family became extremely wealthy after selling rights to one of its properties.

They purchased an Executive Zone estate.

Then they did something horrifying by Executive Zone standards.

They worked from home.

The mansion became:

Family residence.

Corporate office.

Printing facility.

Warehouse.

Distribution center.

Employees were present.

Deliveries occurred.

It looked suspiciously like commerce.

The HOA repeatedly attempted to create rules banning such business operations from private estates.

Those attempts failed by narrow votes.

This demonstrates another core principle:

The HOA has substantial authority.

Its authority is political.

If enough residents vote against the board, the board loses.

# THE HOA NETWORK

During 2045, Ziggurat was contracted to build sophisticated NET infrastructure for HOA records.

# DOES THE HOA STILL RULE IN 2077?

Canon explicitly describes the HOA government in the 2045 period.

2077 sources instead refer to the modern district as North Oak and emphasize:

Private estates.

Individual security.

Corporate elites.

Celebrities.

Restricted access.

They do not explicitly identify the 2077 HOA president.

Therefore:

The continued existence of an HOA-style organization in 2077 is extremely plausible.

Its exact leadership and legal authority are not directly established in current canon.

For RP, the safest interpretation is:

North Oak retains a powerful private residents' association descended from the old Executive Zone HOA.

It handles:

Estate standards.

Common services.

Private-access rules.

Neighborhood security contracts.

Shared amenities.

Infrastructure coordination.

Political lobbying.

But do not assume Karen Davies is still president in 2077 unless your RP chooses that continuity.

Thirty-two years have passed since 2045.

# CITY HALL AND THE EXECUTIVE ZONE

The Executive Zone has never existed completely independently from Night City government.

Instead, wealthy residents manipulate municipal government.

During the expansion of elite Westbrook, corporate and wealthy interests secured city backing for their plans.

The political process legalized redevelopment.

Mercenaries then removed people occupying desirable land.

The wealthy did not merely violate the law.

They changed the circumstances so the law supported what they wanted.

# THE GENTRIFICATION OF NORTH OAK

The history of modern North Oak is brutal.

After the 2023 disaster, thousands of displaced Night Citizens settled in the northeastern hills.

Temporary settlements became semi-permanent communities.

For decades, poor residents lived on land that would eventually become immensely valuable.

Then corporations and municipal authorities decided the area required "cleaning up."

# THE WAR AGAINST CRIME

In the 2050s, corporate-backed Night City authorities launched what was publicly presented as a campaign against crime.

The reality involved clearing the impoverished settlements occupying North Oak.

Residents were forcibly removed.

Structures were destroyed.

Developers moved in.

The territory was transformed into luxury real estate.

# HISTORY GETS BURIED

After redevelopment, media narratives increasingly minimized what had happened.

Property values mattered.

Wealthy buyers did not want to associate their new estates with:

Refugees.

Forced clearances.

Shantytowns.

Corporate violence.

So the old history faded from fashionable conversation.

By 2077, North Oak is marketed as Night City's paradise.

The people removed to create it are mostly absent from the sales brochure.

# SECURITY

The defining feature of Executive Zone life is not the mansion.

It is the security surrounding it.

# THE 2045 SECURITY PROVIDER

During 2045, essentially all common law enforcement and security inside the Executive Zone were contracted to Lazarus.

Lazarus was one of the world's leading private military organizations.

The Executive Zone was its most important Night City security contract.

# LAZARUS GROUP

Lazarus is not a neighborhood security company.

It is an international military contractor capable of supplying:

Individual solos.

Bodyguards.

Special operations teams.

Infantry.

Military advisers.

Combat units.

Armored formations.

The Executive Zone hires personnel from an organization capable of fighting wars.

The HOA has effectively contracted a private military corporation to make sure nobody steals the neighbors' garden sculptures.

# WHY LAZARUS?

The Executive Zone requires something ordinary police cannot provide.

Discretion.

The residents possess secrets.

An NCPD officer discovering something embarrassing creates risk.

A Lazarus professional is contractually obligated to forget what they saw.

# 2077 PRIVATE SECURITY

By 2077, North Oak is described as an enclave where each super-rich estate maintains its own 24-hour security.

This is an important evolution from the 2045 model.

Rather than treating Lazarus as unquestionably policing every inch in 2077, a more lore-faithful approach is:

COMMON / LEGACY ZONE SECURITY:
Private contractors and neighborhood security.

ESTATE SECURITY:
Chosen directly by each estate owner.

CORPORATE ESTATES:
Protected by the owner's corporation.

This creates overlapping jurisdictions.

# 2077 RP DEFAULT: HOW THE EXECUTIVE ZONE SHOULD WORK

For a Cyberpunk 2077 RP, use the following model unless the story establishes otherwise.

North Oak is the legal city sub-district.

Within it exists the socially recognized Executive Zone:

The heavily gated concentration of premier estates descended from the old Executive Zone.

A residents' association descended from the old HOA manages:

Common landscaping.

Property restrictions.

Shared security contracts.

Community amenities.

Contractors.

Guest procedures.

Infrastructure.

Political lobbying.

The exact 2077 HOA president is not established by canon.

Lazarus remains historically prestigious and can plausibly retain major neighborhood contracts, but it should not automatically override estate-specific security.

Every major estate maintains independent protection.

The Arasaka Estate is protected by Arasaka.

Celebrity estates may use private security companies or robots.

Residents primarily travel by AV or secure private vehicles.

Unknown visitors are intercepted.

Invited guests are screened.

Employees and contractors are vetted.

The neighborhood is peaceful because the consequences of causing trouble are enormous.

# FOR AN ARASAKA CHARACTER

An Arasaka resident should experience the Executive Zone as one of the few places in Night City where they are genuinely at home among social equals.

In Corporate Plaza, they are surrounded by employees.

In Watson, they are surrounded by people who know the Arasaka logo.

In North Oak, their neighbor may control an energy corporation.

The person across the road may be an international celebrity.

Another resident may command a private intelligence company.

This changes behavior.

The Executive Zone is not a place where your Arasaka character gets to impress everyone merely by being wealthy.

Everyone is wealthy.

Prestige depends upon:

Family.

Position.

Influence.

Reputation.

Property.

Connections.

Power.

# IF YOUR CHARACTER IS AN ARASAKA FAMILY MEMBER

The hierarchy changes again.

Ordinary Executive Zone residents may be billionaires.

An HOA president can argue with them.

# IF YOUR CHARACTER IS A SELF-MADE BILLIONAIRE

Corporate sponsors.

# FINAL RP PRINCIPLE

The Executive Zone represents Night City's ultimate contradiction.

Night City is overcrowded.

The Executive Zone has empty lawns.

Night City is polluted.

The Executive Zone grows forests.

Night City is violent.

The Executive Zone hires armies to preserve quiet.

Night City struggles with unreliable public services.

The Executive Zone guards its utility substations with military contractors.

Night City families crowd into megabuildings.

One Executive Zone family can occupy an estate larger than an entire city block.

Night City is ruled officially from City Hall.

Some of the people who truly determine its future argue over neighborhood bylaws behind fortified gates.

That is what makes the Executive Zone important.

It is not merely where the rich live.

It is where power removes its tie, steps out of the boardroom, pours itself a drink and discovers that the CEO next door planted the wrong fucking tree.
