# LORE-BOOK: TAKEDA'S RETURN

# THE RETURN OF THE BLACK DEATH

## OVERVIEW

In 2077, Takeda Arasaka returned to active service.

Seven years had passed since the assassination attempt that should have killed him.

For most of that time, Takeda had existed inside the most secret medical program Arasaka could provide. His body had been reconstructed through experimental medicine, cybernetics, genetic treatment, cellular restoration and procedures available to almost nobody outside the highest reaches of the corporation.

The recovery was slow.

Arasaka could rebuild bone.

It could reconstruct organs.

It could restore damaged neural tissue.

It could replace cybernetics.

The more difficult question was whether the man who eventually opened his eyes would still be Takeda Arasaka.

By 2077, the answer was finally clear.

The Black Death had returned.

His body was stable.

His mind was intact.

His combat systems were operational.

His physicians formally cleared him for active duty.

Saburo Arasaka did not give him long to enjoy his resurrection.

---

# THE MEAL WITH SABURO

Shortly after Takeda was declared fully recovered, Saburo Arasaka met with him personally.

They shared a private meal.

For Takeda, the encounter carried more weight than an ordinary meeting with Arasaka's CEO.

Saburo was his creator, political father, commander and the architect of Project STEEL. He was also the man who had kept Takeda's survival hidden while Arasaka spent seven years putting him back together.

The meal represented something close to a second commissioning.

Takeda had returned.

Now Saburo intended to use him.

His primary order was enormous in scope:

Ensure Arasaka's dominance in North America.

Saburo did not define that objective as one battle, one acquisition or one diplomatic agreement.

Takeda was expected to examine the balance of power across Arasaka's North American operations and strengthen the corporation wherever necessary.

Militech influence was to be reduced.

Arasaka's military position was to be strengthened.

Its political relationships were to be expanded.

Its security contracts were to become increasingly indispensable.

Night City, already one of Arasaka's greatest centers of power outside Japan, would become the natural center of this campaign.

Saburo also gave Takeda two recovery objectives.

One concerned the experimental Relic program and a stolen prototype connected to Johnny Silverhand.

The second concerned another piece of highly valuable Arasaka cyberware that had disappeared into the North American criminal market.

Takeda was additionally expected to consider the future development of Secure Your Soul, particularly the identification of suitable personnel, experiments and strategic applications for Arasaka's increasingly sophisticated work with consciousness and engrams.

The mission was broad.

That suited Takeda.

Saburo had not rebuilt the Supreme-Commander so he could retire quietly.

---

# NIGHT CITY

Takeda knew Hanako was in Night City.

That alone made the city the obvious place to begin.

He travelled from Japan aboard his personal spaceplane with the Shirogane Guard.

The journey was not concealed in the manner of a covert deployment, but neither was it treated as a public spectacle. Takeda's return remained an extraordinarily sensitive matter.

After reaching Night City airspace, he transferred to an Arasaka AV and travelled to the fortified Arasaka estate in North Oak.

The estate stood within the wealthy hills that formed Night City's Executive Zone.

North Oak was already a landscape of mansions, private roads, corporate estates, celebrities and extreme wealth. Within Takeda's Night City, the old Executive Zone traditions had never truly disappeared. They had evolved into an even more exclusive culture embedded within modern North Oak.

Private estates possessed their own security.

Private AV access was normal.

Corporate leaders negotiated with one another away from ordinary Night City.

The wealthiest residents did not merely purchase expensive apartments.

They purchased distance from everyone else.

The Arasaka estate belonged naturally within that world.

---

# ARRIVAL AT THE ARASAKA ESTATE

The Shirogane Guard prepared the estate before Takeda's arrival.

Advance security inspected the grounds, approaches and landing area.

Counter-sniper teams watched the surrounding high ground.

Electronic protection covered the local network and airspace.

Colonel Daichi Kurosawa, Captain of the Shirogane Guard, maintained overall protective command.

Takeda's arrival brought two separate protection systems together.

The Shirogane remained responsible for him.

Sandayu Oda remained responsible for Hanako.

The two forces coordinated without difficulty.

Neither required Yorinobu's permission.

Waiting at the estate were:

Hanako Arasaka.

Yorinobu Arasaka.

Sandayu Oda.

Adam Smasher.

For Hanako, the reunion was deeply personal.

Seven years had passed since she had truly possessed her husband.

She had known that he was alive.

She had known physicians were working on him.

She had received progress reports.

None of that was the same as seeing him step from an AV.

Takeda had lost none of his physical presence.

At seven feet six inches, wearing formal Arasaka command attire and carrying the weight of decades of war, he seemed almost too large for the refined architecture around him.

Hanako's attention was not on his rank.

It was on him.

Takeda greeted her simply.

"Hanako, it's good to see you again."

She studied him closely, particularly the scar he had deliberately retained, and told him that he looked as stubborn as she remembered.

Adam Smasher responded to the reunion with considerably less refinement.

The old war machine had known Takeda before most of Night City understood what the name Black Death meant. Their familiarity came from battle rather than ceremony, and Smasher had little interest in pretending not to notice the reunion between husband and wife.

Yorinobu was more careful.

He made restrained remarks about Tokyo, Saburo and the success of the physicians who had reconstructed Takeda.

There was suspicion beneath the politeness.

Precisely how much Yorinobu understood about Takeda's disappearance remained uncertain.

Takeda's survival had never been public knowledge in the ordinary sense.

The complete truth of the assassination and reconstruction remained closely guarded.

Hanako had no interest in allowing Yorinobu to turn the reunion into an interrogation.

She brought Takeda inside.

---

# WHAT HAPPENED WHILE TAKEDA WAS GONE

Takeda had lost seven years.

Night City had not waited for him.

One of the events he asked about involved a young edgerunner named David Martinez.

Adam Smasher had been directly involved in the final confrontation and gave Takeda his version of events.

David had become involved with an Arasaka experimental cyberskeleton and pushed his cybernetic capacity far beyond anything an ordinary human should have survived.

His crew had assaulted Arasaka forces.

Lucy attempted to interfere through the Net.

Rebecca fought beside them.

David continued fighting even as the technology consuming his body pushed him toward destruction.

Smasher ultimately killed him.

To Smasher, the incident was not a war.

It was the extermination of another group of ambitious edgerunners who had climbed too high and discovered what waited above them.

Takeda nevertheless recognized the seriousness of the underlying failure.

A prototype had escaped Arasaka control.

Faraday had been able to manipulate corporate interests.

A young mercenary crew had reached the point where Arasaka's most dangerous enforcer became necessary.

The incident had ended in Arasaka's favor.

That did not make the failures preceding it acceptable.

Takeda praised Smasher for destroying Arasaka's enemies.

He still regarded him as an old battle-brother.

Smasher, predictably, treated Takeda's seven-year absence with the delicacy of a demolition charge.

At one point he observed that dying had apparently failed to improve Takeda's attitude.

The remark was noticed.

It was not discussed.

---

# HUSBAND AND WIFE

Yorinobu eventually departed for business at Arasaka Tower.

Before leaving, he suggested that he and Takeda should speak privately at some point about how Arasaka, and Saburo, had changed during the seven years Takeda had been absent.

Smasher went outside.

Oda withdrew from Hanako's immediate presence at her instruction.

For the first time since Takeda's arrival, husband and wife were alone.

Hanako told him that she had imagined the reunion many times.

None of those imaginings had been adequate.

During Takeda's reconstruction she had visited him in Tokyo more often than Saburo realized.

She had spoken to him even when physicians considered the practice pointless.

Takeda could not answer.

For years Hanako had possessed a husband whose heart could beat, whose body could heal and whose brain could be repaired, but who could not look at her.

Her greatest fear had not simply been that Takeda would die.

It had been that Arasaka would successfully restore his body while failing to restore the man she loved.

She had imagined him awakening and looking at her as though Hanako Arasaka were merely an entry in someone else's memory.

Instead he had stepped from the AV and greeted her as his wife.

That was enough.

Takeda told her that Saburo had already given him a mission.

Hanako was unsurprised.

Saburo Arasaka had not spent seven years reconstructing the Supreme-Commander so that Takeda could admire the gardens.

---

# THE NORTH AMERICAN MISSION

Takeda explained the broad scope of his assignment.

Arasaka's influence across North America was to increase.

Militech's was to decrease.

Night City would become the first major battlefield, although much of the fighting would initially occur through contracts, procurement, intelligence and political influence rather than artillery.

Hanako understood immediately.

One of the obvious opportunities involved the NCPD.

Militech supplied enormous quantities of weapons, equipment, maintenance and related services throughout North America.

Simply offering Night City cheaper Arasaka weapons would not be enough.

Hanako advised replacing the entire relationship.

Arasaka could provide:

Weapons.

Maintenance.

Training.

Financing.

Replacement guarantees.

Technical integration.

Security support.

Political assistance.

Long-term contracts.

The objective was not merely to convince a police department to purchase an Arasaka rifle.

The objective was to make continuing the Militech relationship economically irrational.

Night City's border security offered another opportunity.

Arasaka already possessed enormous military credibility within the city following the Unification War.

A legitimate municipal request for greater Arasaka involvement would be far more useful than simply moving forces into position and handing the NUSA a propaganda victory.

Pacifica presented a different problem.

Hanako understood that military occupation alone would not create lasting control there.

Anyone with sufficient force could occupy a ruined neighborhood.

Holding it was another matter.

If Arasaka ever intended to transform Pacifica into something valuable, military force would need to be followed by reconstruction.

Jobs.

Utilities.

Food.

Medical access.

Security.

Infrastructure.

Investment.

People obey soldiers because soldiers are armed.

People become dependent upon whoever keeps the lights on.

That distinction would become important later.

---

# THE STOLEN TECHNOLOGY

Arasaka Counter-Intelligence had already begun examining the stolen hardware.

Shinrei and Susan Abernathy were consolidating the available intelligence.

The second missing cyberware asset appeared to have moved through criminal intermediaries, black clinics, Nomad freight routes and technical brokers operating beyond ordinary corporate distribution systems.

At this stage, Takeda did not yet know where it had gone.

Neither did he fully understand what would happen with the stolen Relic.

Both trails would soon converge on Night City's underworld.

For one night, however, Hanako refused to let them consume the reunion.

Night City could survive without Takeda Arasaka for several more hours.

She had already survived without her husband for seven years.

---

# ONE NIGHT IN THE EXECUTIVE ZONE

Takeda decided they would spend the evening together at the Executive Zone Casino and Country Club.

Golf.

Drinks.

And, after some persuasion from Hanako, gambling.

Takeda changed out of his command uniform.

Hanako prepared for the evening.

Their protective details remained present but deliberately unobtrusive.

Kurosawa and Oda coordinated the visible security around the couple while the wider Shirogane network secured approaches, airspace and electronic systems.

The objective was not to make Takeda and Hanako appear unprotected.

That would have been absurd.

The objective was to create enough distance that they could pretend, for several hours, that security was not the most important thing in the world.

The club served the highest levels of Night City's ruling class.

Corporate executives.

Financiers.

Celebrities.

Political figures.

Foreign business leaders.

People wealthy enough that an ordinary Night Citizen might spend a lifetime working and never enter the building.

Takeda attracted attention immediately.

He always did.

The response was not screaming panic.

These were people accustomed to powerful individuals.

Instead conversations became slightly quieter.

Executives assessed him.

Corporate representatives watched who approached him.

People who recognized the Black Death understood exactly who had entered.

Politeness became unusually precise.

---

# THE LINKS

The Executive Zone club possessed one of its more extraordinary attractions:

An eighteen-floor indoor golf course.

Each floor contained one hole.

Elevators moved players vertically through the course.

Real turf, bunkers, water hazards, climate control and sophisticated landscaping created an artificial golfing environment inside one of Night City's monuments to elite excess.

Takeda understood golf perfectly well.

What he needed explained was why anyone had decided eighteen floors of a building should become a golf course.

Once shown the engineering involved, he appreciated the logic.

Japan still possessed outdoor courses worth playing.

Much of North America's countryside had spent decades enduring drought, war, pollution, abandoned infrastructure and environmental damage.

If Night City's elite wanted perfect grass, naturally someone would construct it inside a building and charge them for the privilege.

The club prepared equipment suitable for Takeda's unusual proportions and strength.

At one point Takeda joked that during a previous game he had struck a ball so far that nobody had been able to find it.

Staff initially interpreted the remark as a genuine engineering warning.

Takeda corrected them.

He knew how to control his strength.

He had been engineered to control it.

The Black Death was not a clumsy giant constantly breaking furniture and accidentally launching sporting equipment through walls.

His entire existence was built around precision.

The round went well.

Hanako became competitive.

Takeda enjoyed himself.

Nothing exploded.

No evacuation occurred.

No war was declared.

For Night City, this qualified as a remarkably successful evening.

---

# TAKEDA ARASAKA DISCOVERS GAMBLING

After golf and drinks, Hanako reminded Takeda that he had agreed to gamble.

Takeda regarded gambling with open contempt.

He preferred guarantees.

War involved enough uncertainty without voluntarily paying for more.

Hanako persuaded him to try baccarat.

Takeda placed one eurodollar on Banker.

A club accustomed to the spending habits of Night City's elite found itself waiving its normal minimum so the Supreme-Commander of Arasaka could risk approximately the price of a cheap vending-machine meal.

He won.

His profit after commission was ninety-five cents.

Takeda then wagered one hundred eurodollars.

He lost.

That ended his gambling career for the evening.

Hanako found the entire performance considerably more entertaining than Takeda did.

The most powerful military officer in Arasaka had entered one of Night City's most exclusive casinos, forced the house to accommodate a one-eurodollar wager, and departed approximately ninety-nine eurodollars poorer.

Takeda considered this evidence supporting his original opinion of gambling.

---

# TO BEING TOGETHER AGAIN

The couple returned to the bar for another drink.

For Hanako, the evening mattered precisely because almost nothing important had happened.

No board member had required her.

No general had required Takeda.

No assassin had appeared.

No one had asked the Supreme-Commander to decide the fate of a corporation, an army or a political relationship.

For several hours he had simply been her husband.

Takeda raised his glass.

"To being together again."

Hanako answered:

"And remaining so."

They returned to the Night City estate that night.

Neither regarded it as home.

Home remained Japan.

But for the first time in seven years, they could imagine returning there together.

---

# THE FIRST MORNING OF THE MISSION

The following morning Takeda travelled to Arasaka Tower.

The reunion was over.

The mission began.

Shinrei briefed him on the stolen cyberware investigation.

An intermediary network had split the missing hardware into separate movements.

One important name emerged:

Marcel Venn.

Venn was an independent technical broker operating around Watson and Northside.

His network touched scavengers, black clinics, Nomad freight contacts and illegal technology markets.

One of the relevant lots had moved toward Pacifica.

The movement was recent.

Adam Smasher had also produced a surviving captive for Counter-Intelligence.

The individual was alive only barely, which was about as restrained as anyone expected Smasher to be.

Takeda immediately began preparing for the possibility of an Arasaka operation in Pacifica.

---

# THE PACIFICA SECURITY PLAN

Takeda divided responsibility clearly.

Arasaka Security would control the outside.

Arasaka Military would conduct the fighting inside.

Colonel Masato Kuroda prepared the corporate security perimeter.

The plan included:

Road control.

Checkpoints.

Restricted air movement.

Waterfront observation.

Screening points.

Traffic management.

Containment of unauthorized movement.

Brigadier General Kenzo Mori commanded the Night City ground forces assigned to the actual assault.

Military infantry, special operations personnel, combat netrunners and tactical AV support would enter the target area once Counter-Intelligence identified it.

The Shirogane Guard would remain what it was designed to be:

Takeda's protective formation.

It would not be substituted for an army simply because its members were elite.

Shinrei returned to Counter-Intelligence and divided the investigation among specialists.

Human contacts.

Financial movements.

Electronic trails.

Transport changes.

Clinic activity.

Power consumption.

Scans.

Communications.

The goal was to identify what had changed when the stolen device entered Pacifica.

Nobody was to alert the holder before the target could be isolated.

---

# REIKO NISHIMURA

Takeda quickly encountered another problem.

He was Supreme-Commander of Arasaka's military forces.

He was not interested in personally arranging press conferences, procurement meetings, executive schedules, transportation requests and bureaucratic appointments.

He went to Finance and requested someone capable of doing that work for him.

Naoki Watanabe proposed establishing a senior executive aide with sufficient authority to coordinate across Finance, Legal, Government Relations, Public Relations, procurement and regional administration.

The aide would speak with delegated administrative authority.

They would not issue military orders.

They would not interpret Takeda's battlefield commands.

They would not command the Shirogane Guard.

Reiko Nishimura was selected.

She had spent fifteen years inside Arasaka, possessed extensive experience supporting senior executives and corporate delegations, understood government liaison work and had developed a reputation for discretion.

She was transferred immediately into the Office of the Supreme-Commander.

Reiko became the administrative bridge between Takeda's authority and the enormous machinery required to turn his decisions into meetings, contracts, negotiations and public appearances.

One of her earliest responsibilities involved preparing the political and commercial campaign to displace Militech from the NCPD supply relationship.

No agreement had yet been signed.

The machinery was beginning to move.

---

# THE KONPEKI PLAZA HEIST

While Takeda was beginning his North American campaign, another operation was already underway.

V.

Jackie Welles.

T-Bug.

Dexter DeShawn.

Evelyn Parker.

They were planning to steal the Relic.

Yorinobu Arasaka had taken the prototype from Saburo.

Evelyn had obtained access to Yorinobu's suite at Konpeki Plaza and provided the intelligence necessary for the theft.

V and Jackie entered the hotel disguised as members of a Militech arms delegation.

T-Bug handled the digital intrusion.

A Flathead robot was used to compromise the hotel's netrunner.

The plan depended upon avoiding direct confrontation.

It almost worked.

V and Jackie reached Yorinobu's penthouse.

They obtained the Relic.

Then Saburo Arasaka arrived.

And he brought Takeda with him.

---

# SABURO ENTERS KONPEKI

Takemura entered ahead of Saburo.

Adam Smasher was present during the broader arrival but did not remain inside the most private part of the confrontation.

Takeda stayed with Saburo.

Yorinobu returned to find both of them waiting.

The situation transformed instantly.

Saburo demanded accountability for the Relic.

Yorinobu insisted that his position within the Arasaka family entitled him to control it.

His anger quickly focused on Takeda.

To Yorinobu, Takeda represented one of Saburo's greatest insults.

When Saburo's biological son refused to become the heir his father desired, Saburo had created another.

Not through birth.

Through Project STEEL.

Takeda had been engineered, trained, elevated, married into the family and eventually named heir.

Yorinobu saw a manufactured replacement standing beside his father.

Saburo saw something simpler.

The Relic belonged to Arasaka.

Yorinobu had removed it without authorization.

His surname did not place him above the corporation.

Hidden nearby, V and Jackie listened.

T-Bug remained connected remotely.

Then Arasaka detected her.

---

# THE DEATH OF T-BUG

Naoko Hoshino identified unauthorized remote access inside Konpeki's security architecture.

The hostile netrunner possessed privileged access to cameras, doors and routing.

Arasaka responded lethally.

T-Bug screamed.

Then the connection disappeared.

She died inside the Net.

The event immediately changed the character of the situation.

This was no longer merely a dispute between Saburo and Yorinobu.

Someone had penetrated Konpeki Plaza's systems while the CEO of Arasaka and the Supreme-Commander were physically inside the building.

Takeda assumed physical accomplices were present.

He was correct.

---

# KONPEKI LOCKDOWN

Takeda ordered the hotel sealed.

No guest was to leave.

No employee was to leave.

Civilian AV traffic was grounded.

Parking areas were closed.

Stairwells and service routes were secured.

Guests and staff were identified.

Rooms were searched.

Yorinobu opened the Relic safe.

It was empty.

The hotel logs showed no successful departure following the lockdown.

Therefore the thieves were still inside.

Takeda ordered the penthouse searched completely.

The Shirogane found the concealed space containing V and Jackie.

Both were forced out.

They were disarmed.

The Relic case was recovered.

The heist was over.

Or so Arasaka initially believed.

---

# THE PRISONERS

Takeda questioned the mercenaries.

Jackie identified himself.

V identified herself.

Under the circumstances, preserving client confidentiality quickly became less attractive than survival.

They named Dexter DeShawn as the fixer.

They named Evelyn Parker as the client behind him.

Yorinobu recognized Evelyn.

He had allowed her into the penthouse.

The implications were obvious.

Someone admitted to his private suite had recorded enough information for mercenaries to identify the safe, security systems, room layout and access routes.

Takeda initially wanted Evelyn killed.

Saburo was more patient.

She would be taken alive.

Arasaka needed to know who she had spoken to.

Who had hired her.

Who knew about the Relic.

Who ultimately expected to receive it.

Then Takeda proposed something better.

Do not arrest the entire chain.

Feed it.

---

# THE BAIT RELIC

The people behind the heist did not know V and Jackie had been captured.

Takeda proposed giving the mercenaries a fabricated Relic and allowing them to complete their delivery.

Arasaka would follow the device through the criminal network.

Dex would lead them to Evelyn.

Evelyn would lead them to whoever stood behind her.

Saburo approved.

V and Jackie were informed that they would complete the job.

They were not volunteers.

Takeda made the consequences of betrayal clear.

Arasaka arranged passive surveillance around them.

Direct neural intervention remained available if required.

The fabricated Relic was designed to survive superficial inspection while failing under serious technical analysis.

Konpeki's lockdown was publicly explained as an automated security malfunction.

Logs were altered.

The confirmed intrusion disappeared into corrupted diagnostic records.

T-Bug did not return.

V and Jackie left Konpeki alive.

That alone should have told them something was wrong.

---

# V'S DECEPTION

There was one problem.

The Relic recovered from V and Jackie was already fake.

During the heist, V had substituted another device and inserted the authentic Relic into her own neural interface.

Arasaka did not realize this immediately.

The corporation seized V's counterfeit.

Then it supplied V and Jackie with a second counterfeit to deliver onward.

Only after deeper authentication did Naoko discover the truth.

The device seized from the mercenaries possessed reproduced casing and simulated thermal behavior.

It did not possess valid secure keys.

It contained no genuine engram architecture.

Reprocessed scans from the prisoners produced the answer.

The authentic Relic had interfaced with V.

Johnny Silverhand was inside her.

Takeda did not recall the couriers.

The situation had become more valuable.

---

# JOHNNY SILVERHAND RETURNS

The Relic was already altering V's neural tissue.

Simply removing the hardware would not reverse what had begun.

Takeda understood the strategic opportunity.

V was no longer merely a thief.

She was an unwilling human experiment.

Johnny Silverhand, dead since 2023, was gradually being written into a living brain.

Takeda despised him.

He considered Johnny a terrorist responsible for Arasaka deaths and one of the great symbols of anti-corporate violence.

He nevertheless recognized his courage.

Johnny had knowingly opposed an enemy vastly stronger than himself.

Takeda could respect courage without forgiving the cause it served.

He proposed an eventual fate far worse than simply killing Silverhand again.

If the Relic successfully transferred Johnny into V's body, Arasaka would capture him alive.

Conscious.

Contained.

Preserved.

A living historical enemy turned into corporate property.

Saburo approved preparations for containment.

Anders Hellman was ordered to prepare for the possibility.

No extraction was to be attempted while the integration continued.

V was watched.

Johnny was allowed to grow.

---

# YORINOBU'S TREASON

With the immediate heist contained, Takeda returned to Yorinobu.

He told him plainly that he had respected him largely because Hanako loved her brother.

That respect had limits.

The theft of the Relic crossed them.

Yorinobu did not deny defying Saburo.

He did not accept the sacred view of Arasaka held by Takeda.

His earlier rebellion, the Steel Dragons and his eventual return to the family all came from the same belief:

Arasaka was something that needed to be opposed.

Fighting it from outside had failed.

Yorinobu had returned because destroying it from within offered a better chance.

Saburo treated the unauthorized removal and intended transfer of the Relic as treason.

Takeda proposed removing Yorinobu's corporate powers and placing him under detention.

He also raised the possibility of forced retirement.

Saburo rejected that option immediately.

Retirement would give Yorinobu freedom.

He would retain his name.

His money.

His relationships.

His followers.

His resentment.

Saburo preferred a controlled son to a free enemy.

Yorinobu's executive authorities were suspended.

His access to classified databases, command systems, financial instruments, communications and sensitive facilities was revoked.

Unapproved private meetings were prohibited.

His staff were placed under review.

Takemura was ordered to maintain custody.

Yorinobu was transferred to comfortable guarded quarters at the Arasaka estate.

He called it a luxurious cage.

He was correct.

---

# THE COUNTERFEIT MOVES THROUGH NIGHT CITY

V and Jackie returned to the Afterlife.

Dexter DeShawn accepted their story about the security failure at Konpeki and T-Bug's disappearance.

Evelyn Parker inspected the counterfeit Relic and took possession.

She did not reveal the final recipient.

Arasaka did not need her to.

They followed her.

Shortly afterward V collapsed.

Jackie brought her to Viktor Vektor.

Viktor discovered the prototype inside her nervous system.

The Relic was physically restructuring her neural tissue.

Johnny Silverhand's engram was beginning to overwrite her.

Removal would not simply restore V to normal.

The process had already gone too far.

V survived.

Jackie remained beside her.

Arasaka continued watching.

---

# THE VOODOO BOYS

Evelyn carried the false Relic to Pacifica.

The trail reached the Voodoo Boys.

Placide attempted to receive the device.

Evelyn insisted that Brigitte had hired her and that Brigitte should receive it.

The truth began emerging.

Evelyn had originally been given a narrower role.

Instead she had expanded the operation herself.

She had hired a fixer.

The fixer hired mercenaries.

An independent netrunner became involved.

A direct theft from the Arasaka family had grown from a reconnaissance operation.

Brigitte subjected the Relic to isolated analysis.

The device contained no Johnny Silverhand engram.

It was an extraordinarily convincing empty shell.

Brigitte suspected deception.

Arasaka had what it needed.

The network had exposed itself.

---

# THE PACIFICA OPERATION

Takeda ordered the containment operation activated.

Arasaka Security sealed the relevant areas of Pacifica.

Kuroda controlled the approaches.

Ground forces under Brigadier General Kenzo Mori moved inside.

Combat netrunners supported the advance.

AVs controlled the air.

Screening points processed civilians and suspects.

The operation encountered resistance.

Armed Voodoo Boys fought Arasaka troops.

Hostile netrunners attacked optics, cyberware, communications and local systems.

Arasaka's hardened networks and combat netrunners responded.

Mori specifically prohibited troops from firing indiscriminately into mixed civilian groups.

Takeda wanted the area controlled.

He did not need useless civilian corpses interfering with the objective.

The fighting was real.

Arasaka soldiers died.

Corporal Kenta Fujimori, twenty-nine years old, was killed during the initial penetration of the central market.

Sergeant Masaaki Endo, forty-one years old and a fourteen-year Arasaka veteran, died while covering his squad near the underground approaches.

Nine additional soldiers were wounded.

One was listed in critical condition.

Takeda ordered both dead given honorable burial.

Their families were to be notified in person.

Compensation was to reflect combat deaths rather than disappearing into ordinary corporate accounting.

To Takeda, soldiers were assets only in the strategic sense.

They were still soldiers.

They had names.

Their families would know Arasaka remembered them.

---

# PLACIDE

Placide resisted heavily.

He wounded several Arasaka personnel before a coordinated assault overwhelmed him.

He was captured alive.

Arasaka sedated and medically stabilized him before moving him to a hardened interrogation site.

Brigitte proved more difficult.

She withdrew with netrunner support and attempted to bury her movements among false signatures, automated network activity and decoys.

Counter-Intelligence avoided immediately attacking every possible signal.

Destroying the wrong network would merely warn the real target.

Arasaka tightened the physical perimeter while its netrunners followed the trail.

Hundreds of civilians and unknown persons passed through screening.

Most were eventually released.

Those linked to the Voodoo Boys, hostile activity or the Relic investigation remained in custody.

---

# DEXTER DESHAWN

Dexter DeShawn was seized separately.

Arasaka did not stage a polite arrest.

A corporate security team entered the Afterlife, reached him, physically subdued him, restrained him and removed him.

Dex protested.

Rogue did not start a war over him.

She understood the difference between protecting the reputation of the Afterlife and getting everyone in the building killed for Dexter DeShawn.

Dex was taken alive.

Oleg Darkevich was later held separately.

Counter-Intelligence began dismantling Dex's network through testimony, financial records, devices and communications.

The investigation revealed older anti-Arasaka activity passing through the fixer economy.

The important conclusion went beyond Dex himself.

The problem was not simply one fixer.

Night City contained an entire market capable of connecting a client who wanted Arasaka harmed with mercenaries willing to attempt it.

Kill Dex and another fixer could eventually take the contract.

Takeda increasingly disliked the concept of Arasaka relying upon that same marketplace for its own deniable violence.

He preferred enemies to know whose flag had come for them.

---

# THE SECOND CYBERWARE TRAIL

During the Pacifica fighting, Takeda noticed something on recorded surveillance.

He ordered the footage rewound.

A man connected to the other stolen cyberware investigation was using the chaos of the Pacifica operation to disappear.

Takeda recognized the opportunity.

He ordered no immediate arrest.

Counter-Intelligence followed him instead.

The suspect eventually travelled toward a Maelstrom industrial location in Northside.

A technician there authenticated the stolen Arasaka military hardware.

The seller demanded payment.

Before the transaction completed, another buyer contacted an additional party.

Takeda ordered continued observation.

If Arasaka attacked immediately, it would recover one stolen device.

If it waited, the investigation might reveal the entire market surrounding it.

The final disposition of this second stolen hardware operation was not recorded during the events immediately surrounding Takeda's return.

The investigation remained active.

---

# TAKEDA GOES TO THE AFTERLIFE

Takeda eventually decided Counter-Intelligence reports were not enough.

He wanted to see the institution that had enabled the Relic operation.

He ordered Amaterasu prepared.

The Shirogane formation deployed with him.

Advance security reached Little China first.

Counter-sniper coverage took elevated positions.

Counter-assault forces watched the approaches.

Electronic warfare systems covered the area.

Then Amaterasu arrived.

Night City knew what that formation meant.

The Silver Guard was present.

Therefore the Black Death was present.

Takeda entered the Afterlife.

He asked who was in charge.

Rogue Amendiares answered.

---

# ROGUE AND TAKEDA

Rogue was one of the few people in Night City with enough history, influence and nerve to speak to Takeda without collapsing into obedience.

Takeda challenged her directly.

The Afterlife had allowed Dexter DeShawn to assemble a crew capable of entering Konpeki Plaza and attempting to steal from the Arasaka family.

What would happen the next time the target was Hanako?

Saburo?

Takeda himself?

Rogue denied knowing about Dex's Relic operation.

She also rejected the idea that every fixer and mercenary passing through the Afterlife was personally under her control.

The Afterlife provided connections.

It created opportunities.

That did not make Rogue responsible for every secret held by every client.

Takeda asked her for a reason not to destroy the place.

Rogue gave him one.

Because it would be stupid.

Destroy the Afterlife and the market would not disappear.

It would fragment.

Fixers would move elsewhere.

Jobs would become harder to observe.

Clients would operate through smaller invisible networks.

Arasaka would destroy one of Night City's best intelligence collection points because of one fixer who was already in custody.

Takeda disliked her tone.

He respected the argument.

The Afterlife survived.

---

# THE AFTERLIFE COMPACT

Takeda imposed a new obligation.

Rogue would report anti-Arasaka jobs that came through her floor, her contacts or the broader fixer network when she became aware of them.

Names.

Clients.

Targets.

Crews.

Fixers.

Whatever could be identified.

Rogue agreed, with one limitation.

She could not report information she never received.

Takeda accepted that reality.

Rogue passed the instruction to her people.

The Afterlife remained independent.

It did not become Arasaka property.

Rogue remained in control.

But from that point onward, openly planning serious action against Arasaka through the Afterlife became considerably more dangerous.

Takeda also supplied information concerning the second stolen cyberware target.

If Rogue encountered the seller, the hardware or a buyer knowingly involved in the theft, she was expected to report it.

---

# THE DRINKS

Before leaving, Takeda noticed two drinks on the Afterlife menu.

Johnny Silverhand.

David Martinez.

Both men had died fighting Arasaka.

Takeda asked whether the Afterlife considered honoring Arasaka's enemies evidence of an anti-Arasaka bias.

Claire explained that Afterlife drinks commemorated legends.

They did not necessarily endorse every belief or action attached to those legends.

Rogue was less evasive.

She had known Johnny.

She understood his flaws.

She understood his hatred of Arasaka.

She also understood that Night City did not ask corporations for permission before deciding who became a legend.

David Martinez had risen from nothing, become famous and died fighting Adam Smasher.

That was enough for Night City.

Takeda did not order the drinks removed.

He simply made his opinion clear.

Johnny and David were fortunate Smasher had killed them quickly.

Had Takeda judged them dishonorable enemies and taken them alive, their deaths might have been considerably less merciful.

Then he left.

---

# ELIAS MERCER

Outside the Afterlife, Takeda noticed a homeless man asking for food.

He diverted from the prepared route.

The Shirogane adjusted immediately.

The man was Elias Mercer.

He was a former NUSA soldier.

Thirty-four years of service.

Mechanized infantry.

A veteran of the Unification War.

Mercer recognized Takeda.

He had never fought the Black Death face to face, but he remembered the radio warnings.

Shadow Wings.

Black Death sightings.

Veterans suddenly sounding afraid.

Mercer had survived the war.

Peace had treated him less generously.

His leg prosthesis was failing.

The interface produced burning sensations, electrical shocks and false movement.

An actuator locked in cold weather.

Old fragments remained near his spine.

His left hearing had been damaged by an explosion.

An obsolete communications implant near his mastoid caused severe migraines.

He had lost his housing eleven days earlier.

He needed food.

Takeda was furious.

Not at Mercer.

At the system that had used a soldier for thirty-four years and then allowed him to rot on a pavement.

Takeda had fought the NUSA.

That did not mean he hated NUSA soldiers merely for wearing the opposing uniform.

A soldier who served with courage had still served.

The fact that former superiors could live comfortably while one of their veterans begged for food offended Takeda's conception of military honor.

He ordered immediate medical assistance.

Trauma Team Platinum.

All expenses personally guaranteed by Takeda Arasaka.

Not charged to an Arasaka operating budget.

Not treated as charity paperwork.

Takeda would pay.

---

# A JOB AFTER RECOVERY

Takeda also promised Mercer employment once he was medically cleared.

Not a ceremonial position.

Useful work.

Mercer had led approximately forty personnel at platoon level and understood maintenance, rotations, convoy planning, ammunition accountability, evacuation and military logistics.

Those skills had value.

Takeda told him Arasaka could use them.

Training.

Logistics.

Security planning.

Operational support.

The final assignment would be decided after recovery and assessment.

Mercer was instructed to learn Japanese customs and prepare for working inside a Japanese corporation, although Takeda acknowledged that Arasaka's North American branches were culturally less severe than its Japanese heart.

Trauma Team arrived under Dr. Lena Vasquez.

Mercer was taken for treatment.

Before leaving, he saluted Takeda.

Takeda did not expect to see him again.

That did not make the promise less binding.

---

# THE BLACK DEATH GOES PUBLIC

Someone recorded the encounter.

Then someone else did.

Then hundreds did.

For the first time since his return, Takeda Arasaka appeared publicly in Night City in a way that could not be contained within private estates, corporate buildings or exclusive clubs.

Footage spread rapidly.

The Black Death was alive.

He was in Night City.

And one of the first things the public watched him do was stop beside a homeless NUSA veteran and personally purchase medical care for him.

Network 54 covered the story.

WNS emphasized the return of the Samurai.

Max Jones and the Night City Inquirer questioned the politics behind the act.

Was it charity?

Recruitment?

Propaganda?

What kind of society required one of the richest and most powerful men alive to personally intervene before a veteran could receive medical treatment?

Public reaction divided immediately.

Some admired Takeda.

Some distrusted him.

Veterans noticed.

Militech noticed.

NUSA observers noticed.

Arasaka employees certainly noticed.

Takeda had not planned a publicity event.

The event became publicity anyway.

---

# THE COST OF PACIFICA

When Takeda returned to Arasaka Tower, the operational picture had developed further.

Pacifica's principal above-ground objectives were secured.

Placide was in custody.

Captured netrunners, armed Voodoo Boys and logistical personnel were being interrogated.

The majority of civilians detained during the operation were processed and released.

Arasaka's confirmed casualties remained:

Two dead.

Nine wounded.

One critically.

Hostile losses were substantially greater.

Takeda focused first on his own dead.

Corporal Kenta Fujimori.

Sergeant Masaaki Endo.

Their families would be compensated.

They would receive military honors.

Their deaths would not be hidden behind language designed to make a combat casualty sound like a damaged company vehicle.

Takeda then gave the darker order.

Once captured Voodoo Boys had yielded everything useful, those responsible for the operation against Arasaka would be executed.

Counter-Intelligence would decide when each prisoner had ceased to possess intelligence value.

Then Takeda's judgment would be carried out.

---

# THE WEEKS AFTER KONPEKI

The investigations continued for several weeks.

Arasaka dismantled the principal captured portion of the Konpeki conspiracy.

Dexter DeShawn was interrogated and killed.

Placide was interrogated and killed.

Captured Voodoo Boys connected to the operation were exploited for intelligence and eventually executed.

Other conspirators and intermediaries met similar ends once Arasaka determined they had nothing left to provide.

Three important figures remained outside that purge.

V.

Jackie Welles.

Evelyn Parker.

V remained alive because the Relic had made her far more valuable as an experiment than as a corpse.

Jackie remained alive alongside her.

Evelyn disappeared.

Arasaka could no longer locate her reliably.

Her attempt to rise beyond her place in Night City's hierarchy had destroyed what little security she possessed.

She eventually returned to the doll world, stripped of the leverage and ambitions that had led her toward Konpeki.

The precise final fate of every Voodoo Boy involved in the wider conspiracy was not preserved in Takeda's immediate operational record.

The essential result was clear.

The organization that had attempted to manipulate the Relic operation had been broken apart where Arasaka could reach it.

---

# V REMAINS ALIVE

Takeda never considered the Relic incident truly finished.

Johnny Silverhand was still inside V.

The transformation continued.

V became both fugitive and experiment.

Arasaka did not need to place her in a laboratory to study her.

Night City itself became the laboratory.

Every neurological change, behavioral development and manifestation of Johnny provided information.

Takeda's long-term objective remained intact:

If Silverhand eventually achieved full control of the body, take him alive.

For Takeda, killing Johnny Silverhand would merely repeat history.

Owning him would be more appropriate.

---

# YORINOBU IN THE CAGE

Yorinobu remained at the Arasaka estate.

He was not imprisoned in a cell.

He lived surrounded by luxury.

Good food.

Fine clothing.

Comfortable rooms.

Gardens.

Servants.

Security.

Every material convenience.

And almost no meaningful freedom.

His corporate authorities remained suspended.

His movements were controlled.

His communications were monitored.

Saburo had not decided when the punishment would end.

Yorinobu remained furious.

Takeda had one additional burden.

Hanako deserved to know what her brother had done.

Takeda intended to tell her himself.

The surviving record of these weeks does not preserve that private conversation.

---

# THE POOLSIDE NIGHT

After weeks of operations, investigations, interrogations, political preparation and bloodshed, Takeda finally returned to the Night City estate and spent another quiet evening with Hanako.

They sat beside the pool near the AV pad.

No aircraft was arriving.

No command staff waited nearby.

The Shirogane remained present at a respectful distance.

Hanako wore a dark silk evening kimono.

For once nobody required an immediate decision.

Takeda told her he wanted to return to Tokyo.

Hanako agreed.

Night City was important.

Tokyo was home.

Night City meant other people's chaos.

Tokyo meant familiarity, order and the life that had been interrupted seven years earlier.

Hanako wanted her husband back in Japan.

Takeda wanted the same thing.

Saburo's mission was not finished.

But home no longer felt impossibly distant.

---

# THE FAMILY LUNCH

The following day, Saburo gathered the family for lunch before his departure from Night City.

Present were:

Saburo Arasaka.

Takeda Arasaka.

Hanako Arasaka.

Yorinobu Arasaka.

Michiko Arasaka.

Marc Sanderson.

Saburo sat at the head.

Yorinobu's presence did not indicate forgiveness.

His executive authorities remained suspended.

He was there as Saburo's son and Hanako's brother.

Nothing more.

The meal contained the peculiar atmosphere common to Arasaka family gatherings.

Extreme wealth.

Perfect service.

Polite conversation.

And enough political tension to destabilize a corporation.

Michiko and Marc occasionally used humor to relieve the pressure.

Yorinobu endured his suspension with barely concealed hostility.

Hanako remained composed.

Takeda sat beside her.

Saburo needed only a few words at a time to remind everyone who still ruled the family.

---

# SABURO'S DEPARTURE

Takeda asked whether Tokyo was already calling Saburo home.

Saburo confirmed that his immediate purpose in Night City was complete.

His departure was scheduled for 14:30.

Takeda and Hanako were not required to leave with him.

Takeda still had work to complete.

Saburo reaffirmed the North American objective.

Arasaka should become Night City's preferred choice.

Not merely through intimidation.

Through profit.

Security.

Dependability.

Prestige.

Desirability.

The city should increasingly choose Arasaka because refusing Arasaka became economically and strategically foolish.

Saburo wanted dominance without giving the NUSA an easy narrative of occupation.

Takeda understood.

He promised to continue reducing Militech influence and strengthening Arasaka's position.

"Of course, Arasaka-sama."

---

# THE TOAST

Yorinobu offered a formally correct toast to the Supreme-Commander.

The hostility beneath it was obvious.

Takeda thanked Saburo.

Michiko needled Yorinobu about his suspension.

Marc attempted to disguise his amusement as a cough.

Saburo ended the exchange with one word.

"Enough."

The table obeyed.

Conversation moved toward Tokyo, residences, corporate schedules and Michiko's work with Danger Gal.

A current Danger Gal investigation had apparently irritated two executives considerably, although the details were not discussed.

Saburo instructed Hanako to return to Tokyo when Takeda's duties permitted.

Yorinobu received different instructions.

He was to remain in Night City.

He was to reflect.

His suspension would end when Saburo decided it would end.

Not before.

Beneath the table, Hanako rested her hand against Takeda's.

Then Saburo addressed him.

He asked Takeda to walk with him after lunch.

The meal continued.

What Saburo intended to discuss during that walk remained unknown.

That is where this chapter of Takeda Arasaka's return ends.

---

# THE STATE OF NIGHT CITY AT THE END OF TAKEDA'S RETURN

Takeda Arasaka had entered Night City as a man resurrected from seven years of secrecy.

Within weeks:

His survival had become public knowledge.

The Black Death had returned to Arasaka's active command.

The Konpeki Plaza heist had failed.

T-Bug was dead.

Dexter DeShawn was dead.

Placide and captured Voodoo Boys involved in the operation had been interrogated and executed.

V remained alive with Johnny Silverhand's engram rewriting her mind.

Jackie Welles remained alive.

Evelyn Parker remained missing from Arasaka custody.

Yorinobu had been exposed as the thief who removed the Relic from Arasaka.

His executive powers had been suspended.

He remained under guarded confinement at the Arasaka Estate.

Arasaka had conducted a major military and security operation in Pacifica.

Two Arasaka soldiers had been killed and nine wounded.

The corporation had begun examining how to replace Militech's influence over important Night City institutions.

Reiko Nishimura had joined Takeda as his principal executive aide.

The Afterlife remained independent but now operated under an understanding with Takeda concerning serious anti-Arasaka contracts.

The second stolen cyberware investigation remained active.

Elias Mercer had received medical treatment and the promise of Arasaka employment.

And Saburo Arasaka was preparing to return to Tokyo.

The mission he had given Takeda was only beginning.

Ensure Arasaka's dominance in North America.

Takeda had spent his first weeks back remembering how to be a husband.

A commander.

An heir.

A weapon.

Night City had now remembered what he was.

The rest of North America would learn soon enough.
