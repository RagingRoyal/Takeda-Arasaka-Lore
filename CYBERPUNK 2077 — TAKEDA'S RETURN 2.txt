2. Takeda meets his son
Takeda entered the maternity suite and was reunited with Hanako, who was exhausted but recovering well.

Takeda and Hanako decided to contact Saburo Arasaka at his residence in Tokyo.

Through a secure neural holographic connection, Takeda announced:
"We have had a son Arasaka-Sama. I've named him Ishida."

Saburo reacted with genuine happiness.

As the patriarch of the Arasaka dynasty, he considered the birth extremely important. Ishida represented a continuation of the family through his beloved daughter and the man he had personally selected as his heir.

Saburo also approved of the baby's name and its connection to Takeda's original heritage.

During the conversation, Saburo asked Takeda what kind of father he intended to become.

Takeda replied:
"I will teach him the way of the Japanese samurai, the way of Arasaka. To be a great man."

Saburo strongly approved, emphasizing discipline, responsibility, family honour and loyalty to Arasaka.

Saburo invited Takeda, Hanako and Ishida to return to Tokyo once Hanako and their son were healthy enough to travel.

He also encouraged Takeda to set aside his military duties for the evening and remain with his wife and son.

The call ended peacefully.

The family intends to visit Japan, but no departure date has been set.

# PART ONE — THE ACQUISITION OF PACIFICA

## 1. ARASAKA ACQUIRES THE LAND

**Date: 19 June 2077**  
**Location: Pacifica, Night City**

The Pacifica campaign did not begin with Takeda simply arriving and declaring that the district belonged to him.

Arasaka had secured the property deeds, legal rights and ownership interests needed to bring Pacifica under corporate ownership.

The process was ruthless.

Arasaka used its financial resources, political influence, intelligence networks and legal apparatus to acquire the district's property.

The acquisitions were not ordinary fair-market transactions conducted entirely through willing sellers.

The campaign included blackmail, assassinations, intimidation, coercive negotiations and other illegal or morally compromised methods used to obtain control of the relevant land and property rights.

Some owners sold because they had been pressured.

Other interests were removed through corporate intelligence operations.

Legal documentation and property transfers were then used to establish Arasaka's ownership claims.

The result was that Pacifica became **Arasaka Corporate Property** within the RP's established legal and corporate framework.

This fact is fundamental.

Takeda was not claiming land merely because Arasaka possessed an army.

The corporation had already acquired the deeds and rights, even though those acquisitions involved criminal and coercive methods.

Arasaka could therefore present the coming military campaign as an enforcement operation against unlawful occupants and armed trespassers.

That was the corporation's legal justification.

It did not mean that every resident accepted the legitimacy of those acquisitions or that Night City's government necessarily accepted every extension of Arasaka's authority.

Arasaka nevertheless intended to enforce its property rights.

## 2. WHY PACIFICA WAS CHOSEN

Pacifica had originally been intended as an upscale tourist and leisure district.

Its developers envisioned beachfront hotels, luxury accommodation, shopping centres and entertainment complexes.

Investment collapsed, leaving unfinished buildings and abandoned commercial developments.

Over the following years, gangs and criminal organizations established themselves throughout the district.

The major organizations included:

- The Voodoo Boys, particularly in Coastview.
- The Animals, including their presence at the Grand Imperial Mall.
- Scavenger crews occupying abandoned buildings.
- BARGHEST, Kurt Hansen's heavily armed paramilitary organization controlling Dogtown.
- Smaller criminal groups and independent armed operators.

Takeda's proposed redevelopment aimed to reverse the district's decline.

Arasaka intended to exploit the coastline, abandoned commercial property and existing resort infrastructure.

Its planned developments included luxury hotels, private estates, entertainment facilities, new shopping districts and residential accommodation.

The corporation would provide security and administrative services.

Residents could receive employment and housing under Arasaka's new arrangements.

However, these opportunities would be tied to corporate control.

Pacifica was not being liberated into independent local government.

It was being transformed into an Arasaka-controlled commercial territory.

# PART TWO — ARASAKA'S INITIAL MILITARY ACTION

## 3. THE GRAND IMPERIAL MALL ASSAULT

**Date: 19 June 2077**  
**Location: Grand Imperial Mall, Coastview**

Arasaka identified the Grand Imperial Mall as an early priority.

The enormous former shopping centre had become an Animals stronghold.

The building was valuable to Takeda's redevelopment project and could also function as a military and civilian support facility.

The order was to capture it while preserving the structure as much as possible.

Japanese Arasaka infantry, supported by armored units and specialized military personnel, attacked.

The Animals resisted.

Their physical augmentations, combat stimulants and aggressive fighting style made them dangerous in close quarters.

However, the Animals were fighting a professional corporate military rather than another street gang.

Arasaka possessed superior coordination, equipment, intelligence and support.

The soldiers advanced through the building's retail sections and defended corridors.

The fighting was violent.

The Animals inflicted casualties before the Japanese forces established control.

### Recorded outcome

Arasaka fatalities: **5**.

Arasaka wounded: **14**.

Animals detained: **47**.

Additional Animals escaped.

Matilda Rose, known as Sasquatch, was not confirmed captured or killed in this particular operation.

The mall's principal structure remained standing.

The interior sustained battle damage.

The five Arasaka fatalities were not insignificant to Takeda, who takes the deaths of his soldiers seriously.

The Animals were defeated as an organized force inside the mall.

## 4. THE MALL BECOMES A CIVILIAN SAFE ZONE

Following the assault, Arasaka converted the Grand Imperial Mall into a protected civilian reception facility.

The corporation established:

- Armed security checkpoints.
- Registration and identification processing.
- Medical stations.
- Emergency sleeping accommodation.
- Drinking-water distribution.
- Food supplies.
- Areas for families and children.
- Temporary sanitation facilities.
- Administrative facilities for future residency arrangements.

The mall became the designated destination for residents wishing to avoid the coming military operations.

This was not a charity operation independent of Arasaka's interests.

The shelter helped the corporation remove civilians from combat zones, control population movement, identify residents, create future employment records and demonstrate that Arasaka could provide services that Pacifica's previous authorities had failed to deliver.

Japanese military police and corporate security personnel maintained strict control over entry and movement.

People were searched and screened.

The atmosphere was intimidating.

Arasaka was capable of providing food and shelter while simultaneously exercising coercive authority over everyone entering its property.

# PART THREE — THE DOGTOWN WARNING

## 5. OFFICIAL ARASAKA MILITARY ANNOUNCEMENT

**Broadcast period: Before the invasion of 21 June 2077**  
**Area: Dogtown, Pacifica**

This announcement was transmitted repeatedly across Dogtown through public-address speakers.

It was an official Arasaka Corporation military warning.

**Takeda did not personally speak this announcement.**

The announcement was recorded in the following exact wording:

### VERBATIM ANNOUNCEMENT

**ARASAKA CORPORATION — MILITARY ANNOUNCEMENT**

"On the 21st of June 2077 at 8.00am, Arasaka Military forces from Japan will be conducting an operation into Pacifica which includes the civilian named area Dogtown.

Use of deadly force will be deployed.

You're all trespassing on Arasaka Corporate Property.

Failure to disperse or reach the designated safe zone of the Imperial Grand Imperial Mall will result in an arrest and may result in death, injury, or assets being seized by Arasaka.

By orders of Saburo and Takeda Arasaka you're in violation of Corporate Property Law.

Repeat—"

### END OF VERBATIM ANNOUNCEMENT

The final word, "Repeat—", marked the beginning of the next cycle.

The warning continued playing.

### Why the announcement mattered

This announcement established the legalistic and threatening character of Arasaka's occupation.

It did not simply request that residents evacuate.

It declared them trespassers.

It invoked Corporate Property Law.

It warned of arrest.

It warned of injury and death.

It warned that assets could be seized.

It identified Saburo and Takeda Arasaka as the authorities behind the operation.

And it explicitly stated that Japanese military forces were coming.

The wording was cold and impersonal.

No attempt was made to present the operation as a friendly police visit.

This was a corporation publicly announcing its intention to enforce property ownership with an army.

### What Dogtown residents experienced

The repeated warning became part of everyday life during the buildup.

People heard it from speakers while walking through commercial areas.

Vendors heard it from their stalls.

BARGHEST soldiers heard it at checkpoints.

Residents heard it while watching Japanese military activity around Pacifica.

The message was particularly threatening because many residents had occupied buildings for years and had never considered themselves trespassers.

Arasaka's legal position changed that overnight.

Some residents attempted to leave.

Others refused to abandon their homes.

Many were uncertain whether Arasaka would actually carry out the threat.

The repeated announcement removed any ambiguity about the deadline.

**21 June 2077. 08:00.**

### BARGHEST reaction

BARGHEST had its own territorial control over Dogtown.

Its checkpoints restricted movement.

The Arasaka warning directed civilians toward the Grand Imperial Mall, but leaving Dogtown was not always straightforward.

Hansen's military maintained control over the district's principal entrances.

This meant that civilians who wanted to comply with Arasaka's instructions could still find themselves trapped behind BARGHEST security measures.

The warning therefore created tension between the residents and Hansen's soldiers.

### Future continuity

Characters who lived in Dogtown during the buildup can remember the announcement.

They can remember the phrases "Use of deadly force will be deployed" and "You're all trespassing on Arasaka Corporate Property."

They can remember hearing the names Saburo and Takeda Arasaka.

They can remember the warning repeating while Japanese aircraft passed over Dogtown.

This announcement is now a permanent event in the campaign's history.

# PART THIRTY-THREE — CORRECTED ARASAKA CHARACTERIZATION

This section is essential for future RP continuity.

Earlier narration sometimes portrayed Arasaka personnel as excessively friendly and humanitarian.

That presentation did not adequately reflect the corporation's established culture.

The correction applies to future characterization while preserving the events that actually occurred.

Arasaka remains:

Authoritarian.

Hierarchical.

Militaristic.

Ruthless.

Highly disciplined.

Technologically advanced.

Corporate-nationalist.

Obsessed with control, prestige, security and profitability.

Its soldiers serve the corporation and the Arasaka family.

Its officers enforce corporate property and military orders.

Its intelligence services use blackmail, assassinations, surveillance and coercion.

Its executives may discuss reconstruction while ignoring the suffering caused by the acquisitions that made their projects possible.

Its military police do not regard every detainee as a guest.

### Military superiority

Arasaka's expeditionary troops are a professional military formation.

They possess superior equipment and command systems.

Their tanks are not repeatedly destroyed by ordinary militia weapons for dramatic balance.

Their aircraft have sophisticated defensive systems.

Their netrunners can cause lethal damage through vulnerable cyberware.

Their infantry coordinate with vehicles, drones and support personnel.

BARGHEST is dangerous but outmatched.

### Treatment of civilians

Arasaka's aid is conditional upon its authority.

The Grand Imperial Mall provides real safety, food and medical care.

But the same corporation acquired Pacifica through intimidation and assassinations.

Residents can be helped by Arasaka while being frightened of it.

The two facts are not contradictory.

### Treatment of enemies

Armed resistance can be met with lethal force.

Surrendering personnel are detained under Takeda's explicit orders.

Their protection does not imply that Arasaka prisons are comfortable or that the corporation has suddenly adopted humanitarian principles.

Takeda promised that Hansen would survive and that surrendering prisoners would not be harmed.

Those promises are binding orders within the RP.

### Takeda's distinction

Takeda is not personally identical to the corporation's bureaucracy.

He genuinely respects military service and sacrifice.

He wants Pacifica rebuilt.

He wants civilians to benefit from employment and housing.

He also believes in Arasaka's absolute authority, is loyal to Saburo, and will use overwhelming violence against resistance.

He can be generous toward soldiers and merciless toward enemies.

This contradiction is essential to his character.

# FINAL STATE OF THE ROLEPLAY

**Date:** 21 June 2077.

**Time:** Approximately 11:35.

**Campaign:** Arasaka Pacifica.

Pacifica's land and property interests have been acquired by Arasaka through an aggressive campaign involving purchases, intimidation, blackmail, assassinations and legal transfers.

Arasaka has used the resulting deeds and rights as the foundation for its corporate-property enforcement operation.

On 19 June, Japanese Arasaka forces captured the Grand Imperial Mall and turned it into a civilian safe zone.

On 20 June, major Japanese military reinforcements arrived in Night City.

Arasaka's repeated military announcement warned Dogtown residents of the coming invasion and explicitly threatened deadly force, arrest and seizure of assets.

Takeda personally addressed Night City, promising to transform Pacifica through employment, housing, tourism and corporate investment.

On 21 June at 08:00, Takeda authorized the full-scale invasion.

Japanese mechanized formations, combat aircraft, netrunners and special military personnel attacked BARGHEST.

General Hinata's Shadow Wings independently rescued President Rosalind Myers, Solomon Reed and V from Dogtown.

Arasaka subsequently captured the Scraper and Heavy Hearts.

Takeda personally delivered an ultimatum to Kurt Hansen from atop a battle tank.

Hansen refused surrender.

Japanese infantry breached EBM Petrochem Stadium while attempting to preserve its structure.

At Takeda's order, Adam Smasher captured Hansen alive.

Chester Bennett surrendered.

Takeda then ordered the clearance of all remaining criminal organizations throughout Pacifica.

He formally described the territory as Arasaka Pacifica.

The military was instructed to maintain temporary authority until corporate civilian departments could assume responsibility.

At Arasaka Tower, Takeda ordered the establishment of a Resorts & Leisure Division.

Human Resources began recruiting personnel from Japan, Night City and Pacifica itself.

The Grand Imperial Mall became an employment registration centre.

Takeda retained personal responsibility for negotiating with outside investors and companies interested in establishing hotels and estates.

**At the latest recorded moment, Arasaka possesses overwhelming military control over Pacifica's principal strategic assets. The remaining security operations are continuing, and the first administrative preparations for reconstruction are underway.**

The occupation is not yet over.

The hotels have not yet been built.

The stadium has not yet reopened.

The investment agreements have not yet been secured.

Pacifica remains a heavily militarized district under the authority of the world's most powerful Japanese security megacorporation.

For some residents, Arasaka represents employment, protection and the possibility of a better future.

For others, it represents armed checkpoints, confiscated property, corporate surveillance and the threat of detention.

Both experiences are part of the same corporate order.
