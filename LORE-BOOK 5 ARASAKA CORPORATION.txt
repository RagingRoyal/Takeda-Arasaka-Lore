# LORE-BOOK: ARASAKA CORPORATION

# 荒坂

## THE ARASAKA EMPIRE

Arasaka is a Japanese megacorporation and one of the most powerful private organizations in the world.

Calling Arasaka a company barely communicates its scale.

Arasaka is simultaneously:

A global security corporation.

A weapons manufacturer.

A military contractor.

A bank.

An intelligence organization.

A cyberware manufacturer.

A technology conglomerate.

A research institution.

A logistics network.

A political influence machine.

A private military power.

A dynasty.

The corporation has existed for more than a century.

Its influence reaches governments, corporations, financial institutions, military forces, intelligence networks, organized crime, orbital infrastructure, and the lives of ordinary consumers.

By 2077, Arasaka is one of the principal powers shaping Night City.

Its headquarters tower dominates Corporate Plaza.

Its port complex controls enormous quantities of freight.

Its security personnel patrol corporate installations.

Its executives participate in Night City's political elite.

Its weapons are used by soldiers, security officers, mercenaries, and criminals.

Its technology reaches directly into the human mind.

Arasaka does not simply operate inside Night City.

It is one of the reasons Night City exists in its present political form.

# THE THREE HISTORICAL PILLARS

Arasaka's traditional empire developed around three principal pillars:

Manufacturing.

Banking.

Security.

Everything else grew around them.

# MANUFACTURING

Manufacturing was the original foundation of the Arasaka empire.

The company began as an industrial concern in Japan.

Over time it expanded into an enormous range of manufactured products.

By 2077 Arasaka produces or has produced:

The Arasaka name therefore appears far beyond military facilities.

The company can manufacture the weapon used to attack a facility and the security system intended to stop it.

# ARASAKA BANK

The Arasaka banking division was created under Saburo Arasaka.

Banking gave the corporation something weapons could not:

Control without visible force.

Loans.

Investment.

Corporate finance.

Asset management.

Business financing.

Financial leverage.

A company indebted to Arasaka can become dependent upon Arasaka without a single soldier entering its headquarters.

Banking also provided the capital necessary for Arasaka's enormous international expansion.

# ARASAKA SECURITY

The Security division was established by Saburo in 1970.

Arasaka provides:

Electronic security.

Police contracting.

Clients can range from wealthy individuals to multinational corporations and governments.

Arasaka security became famous for exceptional discipline, training, equipment, and willingness to do whatever was necessary to fulfill a contract.

The polite security guard standing beside an executive and the cybernetically enhanced assassin entering someone's apartment at three in the morning can ultimately work for the same corporation.

# CORPORATION AS FAMILY

Arasaka deliberately encourages the idea that the corporation functions as an extended family.

This concept exists on multiple levels.

At the top is the literal Arasaka family.

Below them are generations of executives and retainers whose families have served the corporation for decades.

Below them are career employees whose entire lives exist inside corporate structures.

Below them are soldiers, agents, workers, technicians, researchers, administrative staff, and countless others.

The company does not treat all of these people equally.

But it encourages all of them to believe they belong to something larger than themselves.

# THE REALITY OF LOYALTY

Loyalty within Arasaka is rewarded.

Promotion.

Housing.

Superior healthcare.

Cyberware.

Education for their children.

Security.

Prestige.

Access.

Protection.

Arasaka can completely transform someone's life.

Disloyalty can produce the opposite effect.

An employee who becomes a genuine security risk may lose considerably more than their job.

# JAPANESE CULTURAL IDENTITY

Arasaka is a global corporation but remains culturally Japanese at its core.

Japanese language, aesthetics, etiquette, hierarchy, architecture, corporate customs, ceremonial behavior, and historical imagery remain prominent.

The corporation deliberately presents itself as both modern and traditional.

A boardroom may contain technology capable of digitizing consciousness while executives observe customs centuries older than the computer.

This is not accidental.

Arasaka presents technological domination as the continuation of historical identity rather than a rejection of it.

# THE ARASAKA DYNASTY

Unlike most corporations, Arasaka is inseparable from one family.

The Arasaka family is effectively corporate royalty.

The major members of that dynasty are:

Sasai Arasaka.

Saburo Arasaka.

Kei Arasaka.

Yorinobu Arasaka.

Hanako Arasaka.

Michiko Arasaka.

The corporation possesses a board of directors and enormous professional bureaucracy.

Nevertheless, the Arasaka family stands above ordinary corporate politics.

A senior executive may control billions.

A member of the Arasaka family controls legitimacy.

# SASAI ARASAKA

Sasai Arasaka founded the company in 1915.

Born in the nineteenth century, Sasai had served as an officer in the Imperial Japanese Army before entering business.

He built Arasaka into a successful manufacturing company.

The corporation prospered through industrial production and wartime contracts.

Sasai was highly successful at preserving capital and corporate assets through the destruction and economic instability surrounding the Second World War.

He died in 1960.

His son Saburo inherited the company.

Sasai created the company.

Saburo transformed it into an empire.

# SABURO ARASAKA

## THE IMMORTAL EMPEROR

Saburo Arasaka was born in 1919.

He served as a pilot in the Imperial Japanese Navy during the Second World War.

The defeat of Japan profoundly shaped his worldview.

Saburo became obsessed with restoring Japanese power and ensuring his family would possess the influence required to shape the future.

When Sasai died in 1960, Saburo became CEO.

He transformed Arasaka from a successful manufacturing concern into one of history's most powerful corporations.

## SABURO'S EXPANSION

Under Saburo, Arasaka expanded into:

Banking.

Security.

Weapons.

International manufacturing.

Political influence.

Intelligence.

Military operations.

Cyberware.

Research.

Global investment.

By the late twentieth century Arasaka had become a true megacorporation.

## SABURO'S WORLDVIEW

Saburo believed in:

Order.

Power.

Family.

Japan.

Corporate strength.

Discipline.

Long-term planning.

He could think in decades because he personally lived for more than a century.

He was willing to sacrifice employees, opponents, political institutions, and potentially entire cities to preserve Arasaka.

Yet Saburo should not be roleplayed as a screaming tyrant.

His menace comes from restraint.

He behaves like a man who expects obedience because the idea of refusing him has become almost absurd.

## SABURO IN 2077

By 2077 Saburo is 158 years old.

Advanced medical technology has kept him alive far beyond a normal human lifespan.

He remains the true center of the Arasaka empire.

Among loyalists he commands extraordinary respect.

Among enemies he inspires fear.

His nickname, the Emperor, is not entirely metaphorical.

# KEI ARASAKA

Kei was Saburo's eldest son.

Born in 1980, he was raised from childhood as the logical heir to the corporation.

Kei shared much of Saburo's worldview.

He was:

Cold.

Controlled.

Intelligent.

Efficient.

Extremely loyal to his father.

Unlike Yorinobu, Kei accepted the destiny created for him.

He became acting CEO and managed Arasaka during some of the corporation's most aggressive years.

# KEI AND THE FOURTH CORPORATE WAR

Kei became one of the central Arasaka leaders during the Fourth Corporate War.

He was willing to sacrifice enormous corporate resources to secure victory.

The war ultimately turned disastrously against Arasaka.

Following the destruction of Arasaka's Night City headquarters in 2023, Kei's position became impossible.

He eventually died during the final stages of the conflict.

His death removed the heir Saburo had spent decades preparing.

That created a succession problem Arasaka never fully solved.

# YORINOBU ARASAKA

Yorinobu is Saburo's younger son.

Born in 1995, he followed a radically different path from Kei.

After learning the true scale of his father's ambitions, Yorinobu rejected Arasaka.

He abandoned the family and created the Steel Dragons, a rebellious organization dedicated to opposing Arasaka influence.

# YORINOBU THE REBEL

Yorinobu spent years attempting to destroy Arasaka from outside.

He gathered:

Anti-Arasaka activists.

Contacts.

Funding.

Street connections.

Corporate enemies.

Information.

He discovered a fundamental problem.

Arasaka was too large.

Destroying it from outside was nearly impossible.

# YORINOBU RETURNS

Following Kei's death, Yorinobu eventually returned to the family.

Saburo accepted him back.

That reconciliation did not erase Yorinobu's hatred of what Arasaka represented.

Eventually, Yorinobu reached a new conclusion.

If Arasaka could not be destroyed from outside, perhaps it could be destroyed from within.

# YORINOBU IN 2077

By 2077, Yorinobu remains one of the most powerful figures inside Arasaka.

He possesses:

Family legitimacy.

Executive authority.

Political allies.

Military supporters.

Personal wealth.

His own faction inside the corporation.

He also retains an ideological objective fundamentally opposed to Saburo's vision.

# YORINOBU'S PERSONALITY

Yorinobu is charismatic.

Emotional.

Proud.

Intelligent.

Rebellious.

Strategic.

He can operate comfortably among both corporate elites and people outside traditional corporate society.

Unlike Saburo, who sees Arasaka as the mechanism through which his legacy survives, Yorinobu understands the corporation as a monster his family created.

That makes him uniquely dangerous.

The heir to the empire wants the empire weakened.

# HANAKO ARASAKA

Hanako is Saburo's youngest child and only daughter.

Born in 1999, she was raised in extreme privilege and equally extreme isolation.

Saburo adored her.

That affection became another form of control.

She spent much of her life inside the Arasaka family compound under heavy protection.

# HANAKO'S IMAGE

To much of the public and Arasaka workforce, Hanako represents:

Elegance.

Tradition.

Grace.

Continuity.

Family honor.

She is often perceived as gentler than the other members of her family.

That should never be mistaken for weakness.

# HANAKO THE NETRUNNER

Hanako became an exceptionally capable netrunner.

She possesses a deep understanding of advanced digital technologies and became particularly important in research involving:

Soulkiller.

Engrams.

Digital consciousness.

The preservation and transfer of human personality.

Her technical ability makes her considerably more than a ceremonial family member.

# HANAKO AND YORINOBU

Hanako and Yorinobu share a close emotional bond.

Even when Yorinobu was estranged from Arasaka, the siblings secretly maintained contact.

Yorinobu wanted to free Hanako from their father's control.

Hanako loved both her father and brother.

This placed her permanently between them.

# HANAKO'S POWER

Hanako rarely needs to behave aggressively.

Her strength lies in:

Legitimacy.

Patience.

Intelligence.

Relationships.

Corporate loyalty.

Family status.

Technical expertise.

A room full of senior executives may contain people with louder personalities.

Hanako can still be the person everyone eventually listens to.

# MICHIKO ARASAKA

Michiko Arasaka, later Michiko Sanderson, is Kei Arasaka's daughter and Saburo's granddaughter.

She is American-born and possesses considerably stronger ties to the United States than most of her family.

Michiko survived the destruction surrounding the Fourth Corporate War and developed a dramatically different political outlook from Saburo.

# DANGER GAL

Michiko founded Danger Gal, an investigative and security organization.

Danger Gal developed a reputation for solving difficult cases and conducting sophisticated covert operations.

Michiko became a highly competent investigator and corporate operator in her own right.

# MICHIKO AND ARASAKA

Michiko's relationship with the corporation is complicated.

She has opposed Arasaka interests.

She has worked with American authorities.

She became the symbolic leader of a reformist Arasaka faction.

By 2077 she nevertheless sits on Arasaka's board of directors.

This makes her one of the strangest members of the dynasty.

She is both an Arasaka and one of the people most willing to imagine an Arasaka fundamentally different from Saburo's creation.

# THE TAKA CONTRADICTION

# THE FOURTH CORPORATE WAR

The Fourth Corporate War fundamentally changed Arasaka.

# THE NIGHT CITY DISASTER

In August 2023, a strike against the Arasaka Towers in Night City ended with the detonation of a nuclear device.

The towers were destroyed.

The surrounding Corporate Center was devastated.

Hundreds of thousands died directly or from the consequences.

Arasaka's American power collapsed.

The event became one of the defining catastrophes in Night City history.

# RESPONSIBILITY FOR THE BOMBING

The public history surrounding the bombing became heavily politicized.

The NUSA blamed Arasaka.

Later evidence and wider canon identify the attacking operation with Militech-linked forces.

For RP purposes, different characters may believe completely different versions.

An elderly Arasaka loyalist and an NUSA veteran may describe the same event as two entirely different histories.

# ARASAKA'S FALL IN AMERICA

After the Fourth Corporate War, Arasaka's American position collapsed.

Its operations were expelled or heavily restricted.

Assets were seized.

The corporation retreated largely toward Japan and other territories where it retained influence.

For decades, the Arasaka name in America was associated with the war and Night City's destruction.

# THE TIME OF THE RED

During the postwar era, Arasaka rebuilt.

The company was weakened.

It was not destroyed.

Internal factions developed.

Saburo remained influential.

Hanako emerged as an important public figure.

Yorinobu returned.

Michiko pursued her own path.

The corporation slowly recovered global reach.

# THE UNIFICATION WAR

Between 2069 and 2070, the NUSA attempted to bring the Free States back under stronger federal control.

Militech supported the NUSA.

Night City feared invasion.

# ARASAKA RETURNS TO NIGHT CITY

Night City politician Lucius Rhyne sought Arasaka assistance.

Arasaka responded dramatically.

An Arasaka supercarrier appeared in Del Coronado Bay.

The possibility of direct confrontation between Arasaka and NUSA/Militech forces helped halt escalation.

Night City survived without being absorbed into the NUSA.

# THE POLITICAL PRICE

Night City welcomed Arasaka back.

The corporation regained:

A major political presence.

A seat within Night City's power structure.

Permission to rebuild.

North American operations.

Corporate territory.

Arasaka Tower rose again.

The company that had once been driven from America returned as the protector of Night City's independence.

# ARASAKA AND NIGHT CITY

Corporations appreciate Arasaka investment.

Militech despises its renewed presence.

# DIFFERENCE BETWEEN MILITECH AND ARASAKA

# ARASAKA AND THE NUSA

# ARASAKA AND JAPAN

Arasaka's relationship with Japan is extremely complicated.

The corporation possesses enormous economic and political influence.

Its wealth and security capabilities make it a major national power.

At various points, Arasaka influence over Japanese politics has been strong enough to alarm rival corporations and political institutions.

Yet Arasaka also portrays itself as deeply patriotic.

Saburo in particular sees Arasaka power and Japanese power as closely connected.

# SOULKILLER

Soulkiller is one of the most important and terrifying technologies associated with Arasaka.

Originally developed by Alt Cunningham, Soulkiller can copy a human mind into digital form.

The process produces an engram.

Earlier uses could kill the biological person in the process.

Arasaka recognized almost immediately how valuable such technology could become.

# SOULKILLER AS A WEAPON

Soulkiller can be used for:

Digital imprisonment.

Interrogation.

Assassination.

Intelligence gathering.

Engram creation.

Research.

A captured mind can contain everything the person knew.

Secrets no longer necessarily die with the person holding them.

# MIKOSHI

Mikoshi is Arasaka's highly secure digital infrastructure used to store personality constructs.

It represents one of the corporation's most sensitive projects.

Within Mikoshi can exist digital representations of human minds.

This makes it simultaneously:

Data center.

Prison.

Research facility.

Vault.

Potential gateway to immortality.

# SECURE YOUR SOUL

Arasaka commercialized engram technology through the Secure Your Soul program.

The wealthy can preserve a digital copy of their personality.

To the public, this offers a revolutionary possibility:

Continuing to communicate with loved ones after death.

To critics, it represents a corporation owning human souls.

# RELIC

The Relic is an advanced biochip connected to Arasaka's research into engrams and consciousness.

The commercial technology allows interaction with stored personality constructs.

A more advanced experimental version goes dramatically further.

# RELIC 2.0

The experimental Relic prototype is designed to place an engram into a compatible biological body.

Rather than merely preserving a digital personality, the system can potentially allow that personality to take control of living neural tissue.

This transforms digital immortality from metaphor into a technological possibility.

# WHY RELIC MATTERS

If reliable, the technology could fundamentally alter:

Death.

Inheritance.

Political leadership.

Corporate succession.

Human identity.

A sufficiently wealthy person might no longer need to accept biological death as permanent.

# SABURO'S INTEREST

Saburo takes personal interest in the Relic project.

For a 158-year-old man obsessed with preserving his empire, the implications are obvious.

Relic is not merely another product.

It could ensure that the architect of Arasaka never truly leaves it.

# ANDERS HELLMAN

# CANON CHARACTER USAGE IN RP

Not every canon character should constantly appear.

Their level of accessibility matters.

# ARASAKA FAMILY

Ordinary employees almost never casually interact with:

Saburo.

Hanako.

Yorinobu.

Michiko.

Seeing one of them in person should be significant.

# TOP PERSONAL SECURITY

Takemura.

Oda.

Smasher.

These people normally appear because something involving the Arasaka family or extremely high-value corporate interests is happening.

# SENIOR EXECUTIVES

Abernathy.

Jenkins.

Hellman.

Board members.

They are accessible mainly to senior employees, specialists, fixers conducting sensitive work, or people who have become relevant to a major operation.

# NORMAL EMPLOYEES

The overwhelming majority of interactions should be with:

Managers.

Agents.

Security officers.

Assistants.

Researchers.

Drivers.

Netrunners.

Technicians.

Middle management.

This keeps senior characters feeling important.

# THE KONPEKI PLAZA EVENT

The central 2077 storyline creates a major turning point for Arasaka.

Yorinobu steals an experimental Relic prototype.

The chip contains the engram of Johnny Silverhand.

Saburo travels to Night City to personally confront his son.

The confrontation takes place at Konpeki Plaza.

# SABURO'S DEATH

During the confrontation, Yorinobu kills Saburo.

He then claims Saburo died as the result of an external attack.

This triggers an enormous internal crisis.

# YORINOBU BECOMES CEO

Following Saburo's death, Yorinobu assumes leadership.

He begins using his position to weaken the empire from within.

The corporation becomes increasingly unstable as factions maneuver for power.

# TAKEMURA'S FALL

Takemura investigates the circumstances surrounding Saburo's death.

Yorinobu's people move against him.

The former personal bodyguard of the most powerful man in Arasaka becomes a fugitive from his own corporation.

# HANAKO'S POSITION

Hanako becomes increasingly important as the principal legitimate family counterweight to Yorinobu.

Old-guard employees rally around her.

This intensifies the Kiji/Taka struggle.

# 2077 ENDING DEPENDENCE

After this point, Arasaka's history becomes dependent upon the ending of Cyberpunk 2077.

An RP should choose its timeline rather than combining contradictory outcomes.

# RP TIMELINE OPTION A: PRE-KONPEKI

Best for a stable Arasaka-focused RP.

Saburo:

Alive and CEO.

Yorinobu:

Powerful executive and Taka leader.

Hanako:

Kiji figurehead.

Michiko:

Hato leader and board member.

Takemura:

Saburo's bodyguard.

Oda:

Hanako's bodyguard.

Smasher:

Yorinobu's primary combat asset.

Hellman:

Relic director.

Arasaka:

Powerful but internally divided.

This timeline provides the largest usable canon cast.

# RP TIMELINE OPTION B: IMMEDIATELY AFTER KONPEKI

Saburo:

Dead.

Yorinobu:

CEO.

Hanako:

Powerful but politically constrained.

Takemura:

Disgraced / hunted.

Oda:

Still protecting Hanako.

Smasher:

Elevated under Yorinobu.

Arasaka:

In an internal succession crisis.

This timeline is excellent for espionage-heavy RP.

# RP TIMELINE OPTION C: POST-GAME

Requires choosing a specific Cyberpunk 2077 ending.

Do not automatically assume one.

Different endings produce radically different outcomes for:

Saburo.

Yorinobu.

Hanako.

Arasaka leadership.

Arasaka's global strength.

Mikoshi.

The Relic program.

Adam Smasher.

Takemura.

# ARASAKA SECURITY RESPONSE

Arasaka should react proportionally to threats.

# LEVEL ONE: ROUTINE

Examples:

Trespassing.

Petty theft.

Disorderly employee.

Minor security violation.

Response:

Security guards.

Credentials check.

Detention.

Local investigation.

# LEVEL TWO: CORPORATE CRIME

Examples:

Data theft.

Weapons theft.

Employee assault.

Illegal access.

Response:

Armed security.

Netrunner investigation.

Counterintelligence.

Surveillance.

Local police cooperation when useful.

# LEVEL THREE: SENSITIVE THREAT

Examples:

Prototype theft.

Executive kidnapping.

Classified research breach.

Response:

Special agents.

Elite security.

Corporate netrunners.

AV teams.

Citywide surveillance.

Fixers may receive contracts.

# LEVEL FOUR: STRATEGIC THREAT

Examples:

Family member endangered.

Relic compromised.

Mikoshi threatened.

Massive espionage operation.

Response:

Elite special operations.

Family security.

Cyberninjas.

Corporate assassins.

International intelligence resources.

Extremely aggressive information control.

# LEVEL FIVE: EXISTENTIAL THREAT

Examples:

Direct threat to the Arasaka dynasty.

Strategic destruction of the corporation.

Major military action.

Response:

Arasaka stops behaving like a business.

It behaves like a nation at war.

# CORPORATE DENIABILITY

Arasaka frequently uses intermediaries.

A sensitive operation may travel through:

Executive.

Department.

Agent.

Fixer.

Mercenary.

Gang.

The mercenary may never know Arasaka paid for the job.

# WHY ARASAKA USES MERCENARIES

Mercenaries provide:

Deniability.

Local expertise.

Disposable personnel.

Flexible skill sets.

Low political cost.

If they succeed:

Arasaka benefits.

If they fail:

Arasaka has never heard of them.

# ARASAKA FIXERS

Some fixers maintain relationships with Arasaka personnel.

They may arrange:

Mercenary teams.

Black-market purchases.

Street intelligence.

Gang negotiations.

Smuggling.

Unofficial assassinations.

Corporate agents generally avoid leaving obvious paperwork connecting Arasaka to such operations.

# ARASAKA AND EDGERUNNERS

Arasaka views edgerunners as tools, threats, or occasionally recruits.

A talented mercenary may receive extremely lucrative work.

They may even believe they have become trusted.

Arasaka distinguishes carefully between:

Useful.

Trusted.

Family.

Almost nobody from the Street reaches the third category.

# ARASAKA RECRUITING MERCENARIES

A sufficiently talented merc may be offered:

Security employment.

Special-agent work.

Consulting.

A corporate contract.

Cyberware.

Housing.

Protection.

Arasaka understands that purchasing loyalty can be cheaper than killing talent.

# ARASAKA AND REPUTATION

Arasaka remembers important people.

A mercenary repeatedly interfering with low-value operations may remain an annoyance.

Someone repeatedly compromising sensitive assets becomes a file.

Someone humiliating Arasaka leadership becomes a problem.

Someone threatening the family becomes a priority.

# ARASAKA DOES NOT WASTE RESOURCES

A common RP mistake is having Arasaka deploy Adam Smasher because somebody stole a laptop.

Arasaka calculates.

The response should match:

Value of asset.

Sensitivity.

Reputation.

Potential exposure.

Cost of retaliation.

Strategic importance.

Sometimes a low-level thief gets arrested.

Sometimes their bank account disappears.

Sometimes their fixer stops returning calls.

Sometimes Adam Smasher walks through the door.

The difference is what they stole.

# CORPORATE INTIMIDATION

Arasaka does not always threaten people directly.

A target may notice:

Their apartment lease canceled.

Their bank accounts frozen.

Their employer suddenly investigating them.

Their contacts becoming nervous.

Their favorite fixer refusing work.

Their travel authorization disappearing.

A black Arasaka vehicle parked outside.

Then someone politely requests a meeting.

# ARASAKA INTERROGATION

Professional Arasaka interrogators want information.

Pointless torture damages information quality.

Methods may include:

Psychological pressure.

Chemical interrogation.

Threats.

Blackmail.

Braindance evidence.

Digital surveillance.

Cyberware manipulation.

Family leverage.

Financial leverage.

Soulkiller in exceptionally sensitive historical circumstances.

The best interrogator makes cooperation look like the safest available option.

# ARASAKA ASSASSINATION

Professional Arasaka assassination should rarely resemble a random gang drive-by.

Possible methods:

Poison.

Cyberware failure.

Traffic accident.

Netrunner attack.

Disappearing flight.

Medical complication.

Professional sniper.

Corporate proxy.

Street robbery staged by contractors.

Direct tactical assault is used when subtlety no longer matters.

# ARASAKA PR AFTER VIOLENCE

After sensitive operations, Arasaka may:

Deny involvement.

Blame criminals.

Blame Militech.

Blame terrorists.

Issue condolences.

Hire investigators.

Pay compensation.

Suppress evidence.

Purchase media influence.

Sue anyone making accusations.

The public story and actual event may have almost nothing in common.

# FAMILY PROTOCOL

Anything involving a member of the Arasaka family receives exceptional attention.

A family member is not merely an executive.

They represent:

Ownership.

Corporate legitimacy.

Dynastic continuity.

Global reputation.

Threatening one can cause entire departments to mobilize.

# SABURO PROTOCOL

Saburo should be treated almost like a head of state.

Meetings involve:

Advance security.

Background checks.

Restricted access.

Multiple security layers.

Cybersecurity.

Personal bodyguards.

Surveillance.

Contingency plans.

# HANAKO PROTOCOL

Hanako's public appearance may look graceful and ceremonial.

Behind the scenes:

Routes are secured.

Security teams are positioned.

Netrunners monitor networks.

Oda remains nearby.

Emergency extraction plans exist.

The flowers are decorative.

The rifles are not.

# YORINOBU PROTOCOL

Yorinobu may project a less traditional atmosphere.

His security remains formidable.

Adam Smasher's presence alone radically changes the threat calculation.

# CORPORATE SOCIAL EVENTS

Arasaka conducts:

Executive receptions.

Product launches.

Political dinners.

Corporate ceremonies.

Memorials.

Cultural events.

Investor meetings.

Employee celebrations.

These events are excellent RP environments because combat is usually the least interesting threat.

Reputation matters more.

# ARASAKA CEREMONIES

Arasaka uses ceremony deliberately.

Traditional imagery and corporate ritual reinforce:

Continuity.

Hierarchy.

Family legitimacy.

Japanese identity.

Corporate loyalty.

The corporation wants employees to feel they participate in history.

# THE ARATAMA MATSURI PARADE

Arasaka sponsors major public ceremonial events in Night City.

Such events serve several purposes:

Public relations.

Political theater.

Family legitimacy.

Cultural prestige.

Corporate branding.

Security demonstration.

An Arasaka parade is advertising with a ceremonial heartbeat.

# ARASAKA EMPLOYEE ROLEPLAY

An Arasaka employee should consider:

Department.

Rank.

Faction.

Nationality.

Years of service.

Family background.

Security clearance.

Corporate housing.

Manager.

Career ambition.

Attitude toward Saburo.

Attitude toward Yorinobu.

Attitude toward Hanako.

Attitude toward Michiko.

Relationship with Night City.

# CORPORATE-BORN EMPLOYEE

Someone raised within Arasaka society may genuinely struggle to understand Street attitudes.

They have spent their life with:

Reliable medicine.

Education.

Security.

Corporate rules.

Prestige.

They may see Night City's independent mercenaries as unnecessarily chaotic.

# RECRUITED EMPLOYEE

Someone recruited from poverty may possess intense loyalty.

Arasaka gave them:

Food.

Education.

Purpose.

A career.

They may react personally when outsiders call the corporation evil.

# AMBITIOUS EMPLOYEE

Some employees care little about ideology.

They want:

Promotion.

Money.

Status.

Better housing.

Executive access.

Arasaka is simply the largest ladder available.

# DISILLUSIONED EMPLOYEE

Others eventually discover:

Black operations.

Internal corruption.

Factional politics.

Human experimentation.

Disposable employees.

They may become:

Whistleblowers.

Defectors.

Spies.

Mercenaries.

Corpses.

# LOYAL EMPLOYEES ARE NOT STUPID

A loyal Arasaka employee can understand that the corporation commits terrible acts.

They may rationalize them.

Necessary sacrifices.

National security.

Corporate survival.

Enemies would do worse.

Order has a price.

Someone must make difficult decisions.

This makes loyalty more believable than simple ignorance.

# ROLEPLAY RULE: ARASAKA IS NOT OMNISCIENT

Arasaka is enormous.

Information is compartmentalized.

Departments compete.

Employees lie.

Executives conceal mistakes.

Regional offices pursue their own interests.

Netrunners can penetrate systems.

Agents can defect.

Arasaka can know almost anything.

It does not automatically know everything.

# ROLEPLAY RULE: ARASAKA IS NOT ONE MIND

Saburo may want one outcome.

Yorinobu another.

Hanako another.

Michiko another.

Counterintelligence may hide information from Security.

A regional executive may conceal failure from Tokyo.

A scientist may conduct unauthorized work.

An Arasaka-vs-Arasaka plot is completely plausible.

# ROLEPLAY RULE: RANK MATTERS

A junior employee does not insult a director without consequences.

A security guard cannot demand information from an Arasaka family member.

A researcher may outrank security personnel concerning laboratory procedures while being subordinate during an emergency.

Authority depends upon context.

# ROLEPLAY RULE: CLEARANCE MATTERS

Arasaka characters should not magically know everything.

An executive in Banking may know almost nothing about Relic.

A security officer protecting Mikoshi may not understand what Mikoshi actually contains.

A Relic scientist may know nothing about covert operations in Europe.

Secrets survive through compartmentalization.

# ROLEPLAY RULE: FAMILY MEMBERS ARE RARE

Do not have Hanako casually wander through ordinary offices.

Do not have Saburo personally approve routine gigs.

Do not have Yorinobu phone low-ranking security guards.

The distance between normal employees and the Arasaka family helps create the scale of the corporation.

# ROLEPLAY RULE: ADAM SMASHER IS A LAST HAMMER

Smasher is not standard security.

His appearance should mean something has gone catastrophically wrong or someone extremely powerful personally wants violence.

# ROLEPLAY RULE: CORPORATE SECURITY IS PROFESSIONAL

Arasaka guards should use:

Cover.

Communication.

Drones.

Crossfire.

Netrunner support.

Lockdowns.

Reinforcements.

Surveillance.

They should not behave like gangers rushing individually into gunfire.

# ROLEPLAY RULE: CORPORATE PERSONNEL VALUE INFORMATION

A captured mercenary can be more valuable alive.

Who hired them?

Who supplied them?

How did they enter?

What did they see?

Who else knows?

Arasaka security should ask these questions before simply shooting everyone.

# ROLEPLAY RULE: FAILURE CAN BE POLITICAL

Suppose an operation fails.

The question becomes:

Who takes responsibility?

Which department loses face?

Which faction benefits?

Who gets promoted into the vacancy?

An external disaster can become an internal opportunity.

# ROLEPLAY RULE: SUCCESS CAN BE DANGEROUS

A rising employee attracts attention.

A brilliant agent may gain:

Promotion.

Better assignments.

Executive patrons.

Enemies.

Someone above them may begin wondering whether they are becoming too successful.

# ROLEPLAY RULE: CORPORATE POLITENESS IS A WEAPON

Arasaka characters do not need to constantly threaten people.

Instead of:

"We will kill you."

An executive may say:

"I would strongly recommend reconsidering your position."

Everyone in the room understands.

# ROLEPLAY RULE: ARASAKA BELIEVES IT HAS A RIGHT TO EXIST

Arasaka should not describe itself as evil.

Its internal justification is powerful.

The world is chaotic.

Governments fail.

Cities collapse.

Criminals flourish.

Arasaka provides:

Order.

Security.

Technology.

Employment.

Infrastructure.

Protection.

From its own perspective, power accumulated in competent hands is preferable to power wasted by incompetent institutions.

# WHY PEOPLE FEAR ARASAKA

Because it possesses almost every method of hurting someone.

Physical.

Financial.

Political.

Digital.

Legal.

Professional.

Social.

Military.

A gang can kill you.

Arasaka can make your death the least important thing that happens to your family.

# WHY PEOPLE JOIN ARASAKA

Because Arasaka can offer almost everything Night City refuses to guarantee.

Safety.

Purpose.

Money.

Housing.

Medicine.

Education.

Status.

Career.

Belonging.

Someone growing up hungry may care very little about philosophical arguments concerning corporate imperialism when Arasaka offers them dinner.

# WHY PEOPLE HATE ARASAKA

Because the price of Arasaka's order is control.

The corporation has:

Killed opponents.

Manipulated governments.

Conducted illegal experiments.

Fought wars.

Kidnapped researchers.

Weaponized human consciousness.

Used covert assassins.

Destroyed lives to protect corporate interests.

For every employee who says Arasaka saved them, someone else can describe what Arasaka took away.

# THE CENTRAL CONTRADICTION OF ARASAKA

Arasaka genuinely creates order.

Arasaka genuinely creates terror.

It genuinely protects people.

It genuinely exploits people.

It genuinely rewards loyalty.

It genuinely destroys enemies.

Its technology can preserve human consciousness.

The same technology can imprison it.

It can save Night City from invasion.

It can also make Night City dependent upon its protection.

That contradiction is what makes Arasaka more interesting than a simple evil corporation.

# THE ARASAKA MINDSET

For an ideal Arasaka operative:

Control emotion.

Know hierarchy.

Understand the objective.

Protect the corporation.

Protect the family.

Do not embarrass your superior.

Do not expose internal conflict publicly.

Information belongs to those authorized to possess it.

Loyalty is valuable.

Competence is expected.

Failure is manageable.

Disloyalty is not.

# FINAL RP PRINCIPLE

Arasaka should feel powerful before anyone draws a weapon.

A security guard straightens when an executive enters.

An executive becomes careful when Hanako's name is mentioned.

A fixer asks twice before accepting a contract against Arasaka.

A mercenary checks the windows after discovering who really hired the target.

An employee receiving a promotion wonders whose position just became vacant.

A scientist realizes the project they are working on has another project hidden inside it.

A loyal soldier believes the corporation gave their life meaning.

A rebel born into the family believes the only way to save the world from Arasaka is to destroy Arasaka.

And somewhere above all of them sits an old dynasty that has survived world wars, corporate wars, nuclear destruction, exile, political collapse, and its own internal hatred.

That is Arasaka.

It is not merely a company with an army.

It is a family that built an empire and then made millions of people live inside it.
