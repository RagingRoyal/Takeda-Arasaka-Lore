# LORE-BOOK: GANGS OF NIGHT CITY

## INTRODUCTION

Night City is carved into overlapping territories of corporate influence, police jurisdiction, community loyalty, and gang power.

Its gangs are not interchangeable groups of armed criminals.

Each has its own:

History.

Culture.

Territory.

Economic interests.

Enemies.

Recruitment methods.

Fashion.

Preferred weapons.

Cyberware.

Relationship with civilians.

Relationship with corporations.

Some gangs function almost like organized crime syndicates.

Some resemble neighborhood militias.

Some are loose collections of violent crews.

Some are built around subcultures.

Some genuinely protect local communities while exploiting those same communities.

Others exist almost entirely to prey upon people.

Understanding who controls a neighborhood is basic Night City survival.

The major gang factions encountered in and around Night City in 2077 are:

Maelstrom.

Tyger Claws.

Valentinos.

6th Street.

Animals.

Voodoo Boys.

The Mox.

Scavengers.

Wraiths.

BARGHEST.

# MAELSTROM

## PRIMARY TERRITORY

Watson.

Especially Northside and the Northside Industrial District.

Maelstrom operates from abandoned factories, warehouses, industrial buildings, clubs, workshops, and other neglected portions of Watson.

Their most famous gathering place is the Totentanz, a brutal industrial nightclub located inside an abandoned factory.

## GENERAL IDENTITY

Maelstrom is one of Night City's most immediately recognizable gangs.

They are obsessed with cyberware.

Not merely useful cyberware.

Not merely military cyberware.

Extreme cyberware.

To Maelstrom, replacing the human body is often desirable in itself.

Members frequently possess grotesque levels of modification.

Faces may be cut apart to accommodate oversized optical systems.

Limbs are replaced.

Skin is modified.

Internal organs are augmented.

Combat systems are implanted.

The result often makes experienced Maelstromers look less human than machine.

## APPEARANCE

Typical visual traits include:

Multiple red cyberoptics.

Large facial implants.

Heavy industrial cyberware.

Dark clothing.

Leather.

Metal.

Occult imagery.

Gang tattoos.

Red lighting and visual motifs.

Members often deliberately cultivate disturbing appearances.

Looking frightening is useful.

Being frightening is better.

## PHILOSOPHY

Maelstrom's loose philosophy revolves around transcending biological limitations.

Flesh is weak.

Chrome is better.

Human limits exist to be replaced.

This philosophy is not especially organized or intellectual.

Individual members interpret it differently.

For some, chrome is power.

For others, it is identity.

For others, augmentation becomes addiction.

And for some, the line between religious experience, technology obsession, and psychological collapse becomes extremely thin.

## CYBERPSYCHOSIS

Maelstrom contains an unusually high number of members near the edge of cyberpsychosis.

Many undergo extensive modification with little concern for psychological stability.

This contributes to the gang's reputation for unpredictability.

A Maelstrom member may approach a negotiation rationally.

Another may suddenly become violently unstable.

Experienced criminals therefore treat meetings with Maelstrom cautiously even when business relations are technically friendly.

## ORGANIZATION

Maelstrom is organized, but not with the rigid discipline of a corporation or military.

Individual leaders control crews and facilities.

Power often depends upon:

Strength.

Cyberware.

Fear.

Reputation.

Access to technology.

Ability to control other members.

Internal leadership can change violently.

Challenges, betrayals, coups, and personal disputes are not unusual.

## BUSINESS

Maelstrom participates in:

Weapons trafficking.

Illegal cyberware.

Cyberware theft.

Robbery.

Kidnapping.

Extortion.

Drug trafficking.

Black-market technology.

Smuggling.

Corporate theft.

Protection.

Illegal technical work.

Netrunning.

They frequently obtain military or corporate technology through theft and black-market deals.

## COMBAT STYLE

Maelstrom favors overwhelming violence.

Members commonly use:

Shotguns.

Submachine guns.

Assault rifles.

Heavy weapons.

Bladed weapons.

Combat cyberware.

Grenades.

Drones.

Netrunners.

Enhanced reflexes.

Many members can absorb punishment that would incapacitate ordinary people.

Do not assume a Maelstromer is dead because they look like they should be.

## RELATIONSHIP WITH CIVILIANS

Terrible.

Maelstrom is feared even among Night City's criminal population.

They have little reputation for community protection.

Kidnapping, torture, murder, and random cruelty are all associated with them.

People living near Maelstrom territory generally avoid attracting their attention.

## CORPORATE RELATIONSHIPS

Maelstrom may buy from, steal from, sell to, or work for corporations.

They are useful enough that corporations sometimes employ them indirectly.

That does not mean corporations trust them.

Nobody sensible trusts Maelstrom completely.

## RP BEHAVIOR

Maelstrom should feel dangerous before combat even begins.

They may:

Invade personal space.

Scan people openly.

Mock organic bodies.

Show off cyberware.

React strangely to pain.

Treat violence casually.

Switch between surprisingly intelligent technical discussion and psychotic aggression.

They are not mindless.

That makes them more dangerous.

# TYGER CLAWS

## PRIMARY TERRITORY

Westbrook and Watson.

Especially:

Japantown.

Kabuki.

Little China.

Charter Hill.

Their strongest cultural and criminal presence is centered around Japantown.

## GENERAL IDENTITY

The Tyger Claws are one of Night City's largest and most organized gangs.

They combine:

Street-gang violence.

Motorcycle culture.

Organized crime.

Nightlife ownership.

Protection rackets.

Asian criminal traditions.

Corporate connections.

They are particularly associated with Japanese cultural influence, though membership and operations extend beyond any single ethnicity.

## APPEARANCE

Tyger Claws are known for:

Bright clothing.

Japanese-inspired designs.

Luminous tattoos.

Motorcycles.

Katanas.

Tantos.

Stylized cyberware.

High-end street fashion.

Some members use specialized cybernetic tattoos and electronic countermeasures designed to interfere with targeting systems.

Their appearance is deliberate.

They look fast because they often are.

## ORGANIZATION

The Tyger Claws are relatively sophisticated.

Leadership operates more like organized crime than a random street gang.

Businesses matter.

Territory matters.

Money matters.

Hierarchy matters.

Internal factions and rivalries exist, but outsiders threatening the gang can cause competing Tyger factions to close ranks rapidly.

## BUSINESS

The Tyger Claws have extensive involvement in Night City's entertainment economy.

Their interests include:

Nightclubs.

Bars.

Casinos.

Braindance establishments.

Sex work.

Dollhouses.

Gambling.

Protection rackets.

Extortion.

Drugs.

Human trafficking.

Illegal braindances.

Money laundering.

Smuggling.

Weapons.

Motorcycle-related businesses.

Many operations appear legitimate from the street.

Ownership behind those businesses can be considerably less legitimate.

## CORPORATE CONNECTIONS

The Tyger Claws have historically possessed close relationships with Arasaka.

This relationship is neither simple nor necessarily equal.

Arasaka can use gangs as proxies.

Tyger Claws can benefit from corporate money, equipment, protection, or connections.

Individual gang members may have no direct relationship with Arasaka at all.

Others may work extremely closely with corporate interests.

## COMBAT STYLE

Tyger Claws favor:

Speed.

Reflex enhancement.

Motorcycles.

Katanas.

Blades.

Smart weapons.

Pistols.

Submachine guns.

Close-range combat.

Electronic countermeasures.

Some specialize heavily in martial arts and melee combat.

Their combat style tends to emphasize mobility rather than simply absorbing gunfire.

## RELATIONSHIP WITH CIVILIANS

Complicated.

The Tyger Claws control numerous businesses and neighborhoods.

People may:

Work for them.

Pay them protection.

Fear them.

Buy from them.

Party in their clubs.

Owe them money.

Know members personally.

Some Tyger businesses function perfectly normally until someone violates gang interests.

They are not interested in randomly destroying their own sources of income.

But violence against employees, sex workers, rivals, debtors, and enemies can be extremely brutal.

## THE MOX RELATIONSHIP

The Tyger Claws and the Mox have a particularly bitter history.

The circumstances surrounding the death of Elizabeth "Lizzie" Borden and subsequent violence eventually contributed to the creation of the Mox.

Tyger Claws involvement in exploitation of sex workers and dollhouses makes ideological conflict between the two groups especially intense.

## RP BEHAVIOR

Tyger Claws should frequently behave like businesspeople with weapons.

A sensible Claw may prefer:

Payment.

Intimidation.

A negotiated arrangement.

A favor.

A warning.

Violence comes when those tools fail or when disrespect becomes unacceptable.

Lower-ranking Claws may be far more impulsive.

Their leadership understands one important principle:

Dead customers stop spending money.

# VALENTINOS

## PRIMARY TERRITORY

Heywood.

Especially:

The Glen.

Vista del Rey.

Wellsprings.

They are deeply integrated into Heywood's local communities.

## GENERAL IDENTITY

The Valentinos are one of Night City's largest gangs.

Their culture draws heavily from Chicano and Latino traditions, though membership is not restricted purely by ethnicity.

They emphasize:

Honor.

Family.

Brotherhood.

Neighborhood.

Faith.

Reputation.

Style.

Loyalty.

They are both a criminal organization and a deeply rooted community institution.

## APPEARANCE

Typical Valentino aesthetics include:

Gold jewelry.

Gold-plated cyberware.

Religious tattoos.

Santa Muerte imagery.

Jesus Malverde imagery.

Crosses.

Roses.

Bright clothing.

Open shirts.

Decorated weapons.

Customized vehicles.

Their visual style draws heavily from exaggerated lowrider culture, Catholic iconography, street fashion, and cartel aesthetics.

## COMMUNITY

Few major Night City gangs are as intertwined with their neighborhoods as the Valentinos.

Members may have grown up alongside local residents.

Their relatives may own local businesses.

They attend local funerals.

Weddings.

Parties.

Religious events.

Community gatherings.

A shopkeeper may hate the gang's criminal business but have known three of its members since childhood.

This makes removing the Valentinos from Heywood considerably more complicated than simply arresting gang members.

## MORAL CODE

Valentino concepts of honor should not be confused with innocence.

They participate in serious organized crime.

But concepts such as:

Respect.

Loyalty.

Family.

Promises.

Personal courage.

Revenge.

Community.

Can matter intensely.

Insulting someone publicly can carry consequences beyond the original disagreement.

Betraying someone considered family can be unforgivable.

## BUSINESS

Valentino operations include:

Restaurants.

Nightclubs.

Auto shops.

Vehicle modification.

Money laundering.

Gun smuggling.

Vehicle theft.

Drug trafficking.

Protection.

Human trafficking.

Illegal gambling.

Street crime.

Contract violence.

Many legal businesses are tied into wider criminal operations.

## VEHICLE CULTURE

Vehicles are extremely important within Valentino culture.

Customized cars.

Lowriders.

Paint.

Gold detailing.

Performance modifications.

Sound systems.

Vehicle workshops.

A Valentino-owned garage may be both a legitimate customization shop and a chop shop handling stolen vehicles.

## COMBAT STYLE

Valentinos commonly favor:

Pistols.

Revolvers.

Shotguns.

Assault rifles.

Machetes.

Knives.

Heavy cyberware.

Durable street fighting.

They are generally more conventional than Maelstrom but can still field extremely dangerous cyber-enhanced fighters.

## RELATIONSHIP WITH CIVILIANS

Strongly dependent on location.

Inside communities where the Valentinos are established, civilians may view them as:

Criminals.

Protectors.

Neighbors.

Relatives.

Troublemakers.

Local heroes.

Predators.

Often several of these simultaneously.

Their relationship with Heywood cannot be reduced to simple occupation.

## RP BEHAVIOR

Valentinos respond strongly to respect.

A respectful outsider may negotiate successfully.

An outsider insulting their neighborhood, religion, family, dead friends, or gang identity can quickly create a much larger confrontation than intended.

Personal relationships matter.

If one Valentino calls someone family, other members may treat that connection seriously.

# 6TH STREET

## PRIMARY TERRITORY

Santo Domingo.

Especially Arroyo and Rancho Coronado.

They also operate in portions of Heywood and surrounding areas.

## ORIGINS

6th Street began as a neighborhood defense organization.

Its founders included veterans who believed the NCPD was incapable of protecting local residents.

Initially, the gang's identity centered around defending neighborhoods from criminals and gangs.

Over time, the organization became increasingly criminal itself.

By 2077, the distance between neighborhood militia and street gang is very small.

## GENERAL IDENTITY

6th Street uses imagery inspired by the old United States.

Common symbols include:

Stars.

Stripes.

Military insignia.

Patriotic imagery.

Tactical clothing.

Military gear.

They emphasize concepts such as:

Freedom.

Self-defense.

Neighborhood protection.

Patriotism.

Military service.

Strength.

However, those ideals are frequently filtered through gang politics and criminal self-interest.

## MEMBERSHIP

Veterans have historically played an important role.

Members may include:

Former soldiers.

Former security personnel.

Local residents.

Street criminals.

Mercenaries.

People attracted to the gang's military identity.

Not every member is a veteran.

## ORGANIZATION

6th Street is more disciplined than many street gangs.

Military terminology and hierarchy influence their structure.

Individual crews may be led in semi-military fashion.

Their organization is not as disciplined as a genuine military unit, but the cultural influence is obvious.

## BUSINESS

6th Street participates in:

Weapons trafficking.

Protection.

Security.

Robbery.

Extortion.

Smuggling.

Drug trafficking.

Illegal gambling.

Contract violence.

Vehicle crime.

Weapons dealing is especially compatible with their military background.

## MILITARY CONNECTIONS

Individual members may possess experience with Militech hardware or military service.

6th Street's relationship with Militech is complicated and situational rather than simple corporate ownership.

Gang members may possess surplus or stolen military equipment.

Their contacts can make serious weapons easier to obtain.

## COMBAT STYLE

Expect:

Assault rifles.

Shotguns.

Sniper rifles.

Grenades.

Machine guns.

Tactical positions.

Military cyberware.

Cover-based gunfights.

Team tactics.

Compared with highly theatrical gangs, 6th Street often looks relatively practical.

That practicality should not be mistaken for weakness.

## RELATIONSHIP WITH CIVILIANS

Contradictory.

Some local residents may genuinely benefit from 6th Street protection.

Others experience the gang as extortionists.

The gang can protect a neighborhood from one threat and become another threat itself.

This contradiction is central to their identity.

## RP BEHAVIOR

6th Street members may use military slang, emphasize hierarchy, talk about defending the neighborhood, and treat outsiders according to perceived loyalty and strength.

They particularly dislike being treated as ordinary street trash.

Many genuinely believe they are better than other gangs.

Whether reality supports that belief is another matter.

# ANIMALS

## TERRITORY

The Animals do not control one permanent major district.

They can appear throughout Night City.

They frequently work as:

Bouncers.

Bodyguards.

Hired muscle.

Mercenaries.

Security.

Pit fighters.

They gather wherever strength can be converted into money.

## GENERAL IDENTITY

The Animals worship physical power.

Where Maelstrom attempts to transcend humanity through machinery, the Animals emphasize transforming the biological body into a weapon.

Members obsess over:

Strength.

Muscle.

Endurance.

Aggression.

Combat performance.

Physical dominance.

Competition.

## BODY MODIFICATION

Animals are known for extreme biochemical enhancement.

They use:

Hormones.

Steroids.

Growth compounds.

Combat stimulants.

Specialized body-enhancement drugs.

Cyberware where useful.

They are not anti-cyberware.

They simply prefer augmentation that supports physical strength rather than replacing the entire body with machinery.

## THE JUICE

The gang is particularly associated with a powerful enhancement cocktail called the Juice.

It can dramatically increase:

Strength.

Speed.

Muscle performance.

Aggression.

The physical cost can be severe.

## ORGANIZATION

The Animals are highly decentralized.

Individual packs operate semi-independently.

Pack leaders are often called alphas.

Leadership generally depends heavily upon:

Physical strength.

Combat ability.

Reputation.

Ability to make money.

Authority is personal rather than bureaucratic.

## BUSINESS

Animals frequently earn money through:

Private security.

Bouncer work.

Underground fighting.

Bodyguard contracts.

Enforcement.

Debt collection.

Mercenary work.

Illegal sporting events.

Drug distribution.

They are frequently hired by groups who need visible intimidation.

## COMBAT STYLE

Close-range brutality.

Expect:

Heavy melee weapons.

Shotguns.

Machine guns.

Fists.

Hammers.

Baseball bats.

Extreme physical cyberware.

Pain reduction systems.

Body armor.

Animals may rush opponents who expect normal people to stay behind cover.

Their size itself is part of their combat strategy.

## RELATIONSHIP WITH OTHER GANGS

Because they possess limited permanent territory, Animals are somewhat more commercially flexible.

They can work for:

Corporations.

Fixers.

Criminal organizations.

Other gangs.

Whoever pays.

This means seeing Animals somewhere does not automatically mean that location belongs to them.

## RP BEHAVIOR

Animals value strength.

That does not necessarily mean only physical strength.

Confidence.

Courage.

Refusing intimidation.

Competitive skill.

These can earn respect.

Cowardice and visible weakness may invite contempt.

Many Animals treat life as continual competition.

# VOODOO BOYS

## PRIMARY TERRITORY

Pacifica.

Especially areas associated with the Haitian community.

They possess deep influence in Coastview and surrounding territory.

## GENERAL IDENTITY

The Voodoo Boys are Night City's most famous netrunner gang.

Their reputation extends far beyond ordinary street hacking.

They specialize in:

Netrunning.

Network intrusion.

Viruses.

Cyberwarfare.

Old NET exploration.

Artificial intelligence.

Blackwall research.

Electronic espionage.

To many outsiders, they are almost incomprehensible.

## ORIGINS

The modern Voodoo Boys are not simply the continuation of an older gang that once used the same name.

Their modern identity developed among Haitian refugees living in Pacifica.

Following the destruction of Haiti and the collapse of Pacifica's luxury-development project, the Haitian community remained in the abandoned district.

The organization eventually became one of the community's primary protective forces.

## NAME

"Voodoo Boys" is largely an outsider label.

The gang itself does not necessarily use that name internally.

Their actual internal terminology is guarded from outsiders.

## COMMUNITY

The Voodoo Boys are deeply intertwined with Pacifica's Haitian population.

They may function simultaneously as:

Gang.

Community defense.

Information network.

Netrunner collective.

Spiritual organization.

Political influence.

This makes their relationship with Pacifica fundamentally different from a gang merely occupying territory.

## OUTSIDERS

The Voodoo Boys are extremely suspicious of outsiders.

Outsiders are sometimes called ranyon.

A ranyon can be useful without ever being trusted.

This distinction is critical.

A Voodoo Boy hiring someone does not imply that person has been accepted.

## THE NET

The Voodoo Boys are obsessed with the deepest reaches of cyberspace.

They investigate:

Old NET architecture.

Rogue artificial intelligence.

The Blackwall.

Hidden network systems.

They believe enormous changes may eventually emerge from beyond the Blackwall and want to position themselves accordingly.

## RELIGIOUS AND CULTURAL ELEMENTS

Their imagery reflects Haitian culture and religious traditions.

However, their worldview also blends technology and spirituality.

High-level netrunners can occupy roles carrying almost priest-like authority.

The boundaries between technological expertise, cultural leadership, and metaphysical interpretation can become blurred.

## ORGANIZATION

The gang is relatively small compared with organizations such as the Valentinos or Tyger Claws.

Its influence is disproportionate to its numbers because high-level netrunners are extremely powerful.

Leadership includes experienced netrunners and community figures.

Information is tightly compartmentalized.

## BUSINESS

They earn money through:

Netrunning contracts.

Cybercrime.

Data theft.

Hacking.

Espionage.

Digital sabotage.

Assassination.

Protection.

Smuggling.

Network security.

They are capable of physical street crime but digital operations remain their specialty.

## COMBAT STYLE

Never judge Voodoo Boys only by visible weapons.

Their real attack may already be happening inside someone's implants.

Expect:

Quickhacks.

Cyberware disruption.

Optics interference.

Weapon malfunction.

Neural attacks.

Machetes.

Smart weapons.

Stealth.

Ambushes.

Specialized assassins.

Against a skilled Voodoo Boy netrunner, having expensive cyberware can become a liability.

## RELATIONSHIP WITH NETWATCH

The Voodoo Boys and NetWatch are natural enemies.

NetWatch exists partly to protect network stability and enforce the Blackwall.

The Voodoo Boys repeatedly explore exactly the digital territory NetWatch considers forbidden.

## RP BEHAVIOR

Voodoo Boys should feel guarded.

They rarely explain everything.

They compartmentalize information.

They may use outsiders as intermediaries while withholding the actual objective.

A conversation can contain three different layers:

What they say.

What they want.

What they are actually planning.

# THE MOX

## PRIMARY TERRITORY

Kabuki, Watson.

Their influence centers strongly around Lizzie's Bar.

They control relatively little traditional street territory compared with larger gangs.

## ORIGINS

The Mox formed in 2067 following the death of Elizabeth "Lizzie" Borden.

Lizzie was a sex worker and club owner known for treating workers fairly and defending them from abusive clients.

Her death triggered protests and riots.

From that anger emerged the Mox.

## GENERAL IDENTITY

The Mox define themselves primarily around protection.

Their membership includes:

Current sex workers.

Former sex workers.

Dolls.

Punks.

Anarchists.

Sexual minorities.

Social outsiders.

People with nowhere else to find protection.

Their name derives from "moxie":

Courage.

Nerve.

Determination.

## PHILOSOPHY

The Mox exist because Night City's institutions routinely fail vulnerable people.

Police may not care.

Employers may be abusive.

Clients may become violent.

Other gangs may exploit them.

So the Mox developed a simple solution:

Protect each other.

## APPEARANCE

Their aesthetic includes:

Bright colors.

Latex.

Plastic.

Punk clothing.

Fetish fashion.

Aggressive makeup.

Tattoos.

Stylized cyberware.

Weapons incorporated into deliberately provocative fashion.

The look blends sexuality with intimidation.

## TERRITORY

The Mox are unusual because territorial expansion is not their main objective.

They primarily defend:

Lizzie's Bar.

Associated businesses.

Dollhouses.

Clinics.

Workers under their protection.

Their limited territory contributes to their relatively small size.

## BUSINESS

Their principal economic interests involve:

Braindance.

Entertainment.

Sex work.

Club ownership.

Protection.

Associated businesses.

Lizzie's Bar is particularly important both culturally and financially.

## RELATIONSHIP WITH TYGER CLAWS

Hostile.

The Mox emerged partly in reaction to violence and exploitation associated with Tyger Claw-controlled vice industries.

The conflict is therefore personal as well as economic.

## COMBAT STYLE

Mox members may use:

Pistols.

Shotguns.

Blades.

Baseball bats.

Enhanced reflexes.

Subdermal armor.

Combat-oriented cyberware hidden beneath fashionable modifications.

A Mox who looks designed primarily for entertainment may be significantly more dangerous than appearances suggest.

## RELATIONSHIP WITH CIVILIANS

Generally more protective than most gangs.

That does not make the Mox harmless.

Threatening people under their protection can provoke severe retaliation.

They are particularly aggressive toward abusers, traffickers, violent clients, and predatory pimps.

## RP BEHAVIOR

The Mox should not behave like conquest-focused gangsters.

Their priorities are more defensive.

They usually care more about protecting their people than controlling another six city blocks.

Respect their workers and businesses and there may be little problem.

Hurt someone under Mox protection and suddenly the entire room has opinions.

# SCAVENGERS

## COMMON NAME

Scavs.

## TERRITORY

Scavengers operate throughout Night City.

They possess no single unified territory.

They frequently use:

Abandoned buildings.

Apartments.

Warehouses.

Underground facilities.

Cheap motels.

Derelict industrial sites.

Temporary hideouts.

They often prefer locations where victims can disappear without attracting attention.

## GENERAL IDENTITY

Scavengers are among Night City's most universally despised criminals.

Their primary industry is people.

More specifically:

The valuable parts inside people.

## BUSINESS MODEL

Modern cyberware is expensive.

Human organs are expensive.

Military implants are extremely expensive.

Scavs acquire those products by kidnapping people and cutting them apart.

Victims may be stripped of:

Cyberware.

Organs.

Implants.

Weapons.

Personal electronics.

Identification.

Anything possessing resale value.

The remains are discarded.

## ORGANIZATION

Scavs are not one unified gang in the same way as the Valentinos.

They are better understood as numerous criminal crews sharing similar methods and criminal culture.

There is no single Night City Scav boss controlling every crew.

Different groups can operate independently.

## CULTURE

Many Night City Scav crews show Eastern European or Russian cultural influences.

Tracksuits.

Soviet-made cyberware.

Russian music.

Slavic languages.

Criminal tattoos.

However, "Scav" ultimately describes their criminal profession more than a strict ethnicity.

## TACTICS

Scavs often seek victims through:

Kidnapping.

Ambush.

Drugging.

Fake braindances.

Fake parties.

Sexual encounters.

Street attacks.

Home invasions.

Human trafficking networks.

They prefer vulnerable victims.

A clean fight is less profitable than an unconscious target.

## COMBAT STYLE

Scavs generally use whatever they can obtain.

Expect:

Cheap firearms.

Stolen weapons.

Cyberware.

Shotguns.

Assault rifles.

Improvised defenses.

Combat stimulants.

Ambushes.

Their equipment quality varies dramatically between crews.

## DOGTOWN SCAVS

Dogtown contains unusually well-equipped Scavenger operations.

These crews can possess better organization, weapons, money, and connections than ordinary Night City Scavs.

Some operate within wider Dogtown criminal networks rather than as desperate street harvesters.

## RELATIONSHIP WITH EVERYONE ELSE

Almost universally bad.

Even gang members who routinely murder people may despise Scavs.

Why?

Because gang membership does not necessarily protect someone from becoming merchandise.

A wounded Valentino.

A dead Tyger Claw.

A drunk mercenary.

A lone corpo.

To Scavs, all can become inventory.

## RP BEHAVIOR

Scavs should feel predatory.

They are usually not defending ideology or community.

They are calculating value.

Expensive optics?

Valuable.

Military cyberarm?

Very valuable.

Rare neural implant?

Extremely valuable.

The frightening part is not that they hate someone.

They may feel absolutely nothing about them.

# WRAITHS

## TERRITORY

The Badlands surrounding Night City.

The Wraiths are not primarily an urban gang.

They are Raffen Shiv.

Outlaw Nomads.

## RAFFEN SHIV

Nomad culture places enormous importance on family and clan loyalty.

People expelled from Nomad communities for serious violations can become Raffen Shiv.

Reasons may include crimes such as:

Murdering clan members.

Betrayal.

Human trafficking.

Extreme theft.

Violence against family.

Other severe violations of Nomad codes.

Raffen Shiv groups are generally despised by legitimate Nomad families.

## GENERAL IDENTITY

The Wraiths are one of the largest and most dangerous Raffen Shiv groups operating around Night City.

They behave more like heavily armed road raiders than traditional Nomads.

They prey upon:

Travelers.

Convoys.

Settlements.

Corporate transports.

Nomad families.

Anyone isolated enough to rob.

## ORGANIZATION

They operate through mobile crews.

Leadership generally depends upon:

Strength.

Fear.

Combat capability.

Control over vehicles.

Ability to acquire resources.

They lack the genuine family bonds defining legitimate Nomad clans.

## VEHICLES

Wraith vehicles are heavily modified.

Common modifications include:

Armor.

Mounted weapons.

Reinforced frames.

Off-road suspension.

Performance engines.

Electronic countermeasures.

Weapon storage.

Some vehicles resemble improvised combat machines more than civilian transportation.

## BUSINESS

Wraiths make money through:

Raiding.

Smuggling.

Kidnapping.

Human trafficking.

Vehicle theft.

Murder-for-hire.

Weapons trafficking.

Convoy attacks.

Drug trafficking.

Robbery.

## RELATIONSHIP WITH ALDECALDOS

Extremely hostile.

The Wraiths and Aldecaldos repeatedly clash throughout the Badlands.

To the Aldecaldos, the Wraiths represent almost everything respectable Nomad culture rejects.

## COMBAT STYLE

Expect:

Vehicle ambushes.

Snipers.

Assault rifles.

Explosives.

Mounted weapons.

Heavy vehicles.

Fast raids.

Night attacks.

The Wraiths often choose terrain before attacking.

Fighting them on an open highway is considerably different from fighting an urban gang inside an apartment complex.

## RP BEHAVIOR

Wraiths should feel dangerous because there is little social restraint.

A Valentino may care about community consequences.

A Tyger Claw may care about business.

6th Street may care about territory.

Wraiths care primarily about surviving and profiting.

Negotiation is possible.

Trust is another matter.

# BARGHEST

## TERRITORY

Dogtown.

Pacifica.

BARGHEST effectively controls the entire fortified enclave.

## CLASSIFICATION

BARGHEST is not merely a street gang.

It is simultaneously:

A militia.

A criminal organization.

A private army.

Dogtown's police force.

Dogtown's government.

An arms-trafficking network.

A protection racket.

Calling BARGHEST a gang is accurate only in the broadest Night City sense.

## ORIGINS

BARGHEST emerged from military forces involved in the Unification War.

Colonel Kurt Hansen and troops under his command occupied part of Pacifica.

When ordered to leave after the conflict, Hansen refused.

Instead, he entrenched himself.

That territory eventually became Dogtown.

## KURT HANSEN

Kurt Hansen transformed a stranded military force into an entire political and criminal system.

His authority rested on:

Military experience.

Personal loyalty.

Control of weapons.

Economic opportunity.

Fear.

Dogtown independence.

He offered recruits something Night City often fails to provide:

A salary.

Medical care.

Purpose.

Protection.

Belonging.

The price was loyalty.

## ORGANIZATION

BARGHEST operates using military ranks and command structures.

Veterans occupy many important positions.

Recruits undergo weapons and endurance training.

Even low-ranking personnel can therefore be more disciplined than ordinary street gang members.

Their organization includes specialized security and law-enforcement elements.

## APPEARANCE

BARGHEST is strongly associated with:

Green.

Yellow.

Military clothing.

Body armor.

Tactical equipment.

Military vehicles.

Modified Militech hardware.

Members frequently resemble soldiers more than traditional gangers.

## BUSINESS

BARGHEST controls a huge amount of Dogtown's economy.

Interests include:

Weapons manufacturing.

Arms trafficking.

Black-market trade.

Smuggling.

Protection.

Import and export.

Security.

Illegal technology.

Vehicle traffic.

Property.

Market regulation.

Their largest advantage is simple:

They control the territory where much of this business occurs.

## HANSEN ARMORY

BARGHEST maintains access to substantial weapons manufacturing and modification capability.

Their military stockpiles and local production make them one of the most heavily armed non-corporate factions in Night City.

## EQUIPMENT

BARGHEST can field equipment beyond ordinary street-gang capabilities.

This may include:

Military assault rifles.

Heavy machine guns.

Combat robots.

Drones.

Armored vehicles.

Missile systems.

Heavy combat platforms.

Advanced cyberware.

Military-grade communications.

They possess enough hardware to make conventional police intervention extremely difficult.

## LAW ENFORCEMENT

Inside Dogtown, BARGHEST effectively replaces the NCPD.

They:

Patrol.

Guard checkpoints.

Investigate trouble when convenient.

Punish crimes.

Protect BARGHEST interests.

Shake people down.

Control access.

Their law is not neutral.

Its purpose is keeping Hansen's system functional.

## CIVILIANS

Many Dogtown residents genuinely depend upon BARGHEST.

They provide:

Jobs.

Security.

Trade.

Medical access.

Stability.

At the same time, they are authoritarian, violent, corrupt, and deeply involved in criminal enterprise.

This creates the quintessential Dogtown contradiction:

BARGHEST may be both the reason someone is safe and the reason they are trapped.

## RELATIONSHIP WITH OTHER GANGS

BARGHEST generally does not permit rival gangs to challenge its sovereignty.

However, criminal groups including Scavs and Voodoo Boys can operate within Dogtown under complicated arrangements involving:

Tolerance.

Business.

Bribes.

Temporary alliances.

Violence.

As long as BARGHEST remains dominant, pragmatism often beats ideology.

## RP BEHAVIOR

BARGHEST personnel should behave differently from normal gang members.

At checkpoints they may act like soldiers or corrupt police.

During combat they may coordinate.

Orders matter.

Rank matters.

Equipment is standardized.

Reinforcements can arrive.

A BARGHEST patrol is not five random street criminals.

It is a patrol belonging to the organization governing the territory.

# GANG RELATIONSHIPS

Night City gang politics are not a simple permanent alliance chart.

Relationships change according to:

Money.

Territory.

Personal disputes.

Corporate involvement.

Leadership.

Revenge.

Individual business arrangements.

Two gangs that hate each other may temporarily cooperate.

Two crews from supposedly neutral gangs may still fight.

Individual fixers can maintain working relationships with multiple hostile organizations.

# COMMON HOSTILITIES

Certain relationships are particularly important.

## MOX VS TYGER CLAWS

Strong hostility.

The Mox formed partly in response to violence and exploitation surrounding Tyger-controlled vice industries.

## ALDECALDOS VS WRAITHS

Extremely hostile.

Wraiths are Raffen Shiv and represent a direct threat to legitimate Nomad communities.

## VOODOO BOYS VS NETWATCH

Not technically gang warfare, but one of the most important conflicts involving a gang.

Their objectives regarding the Blackwall are fundamentally opposed.

## GANGS VS SCAVS

Most established gangs dislike Scavengers.

Even professional killers prefer not to wake up missing half their cyberware.

# GANGS AND CORPORATIONS

Corporations frequently use gangs indirectly.

Why send employees to commit obvious crimes when a gang can do it?

Corporations may hire gangs for:

Sabotage.

Intimidation.

Theft.

Murder.

Security.

Smuggling.

Proxy warfare.

Disinformation.

Harassment.

Afterward, the corporation can deny involvement.

Gangs understand this arrangement.

They may hate corporations while happily accepting corporate money.

# GANGS AND FIXERS

Fixers act as bridges between gangs and the wider Night City underworld.

A fixer may negotiate:

Ceasefires.

Jobs.

Weapon sales.

Ransoms.

Prisoner exchanges.

Territory disputes.

Information trades.

Mercenary contracts.

An experienced fixer understands which gang leader can be approached, what insults must be avoided, and how much money will make a problem disappear.

# GANGS AND MERCENARIES

Edgerunners occupy a strange position.

They may:

Work for gangs.

Fight gangs.

Protect gangs.

Steal from gangs.

Rescue gang members.

Assassinate gang leaders.

Sell equipment to gangs.

A competent merc tries not to create unnecessary permanent enemies.

Killing six Valentinos during a contract may solve today's problem.

Becoming permanently hated throughout Heywood may create twenty new ones.

# REPUTATION BETWEEN GANGS

Gang reputation travels.

If a mercenary repeatedly massacres members of one gang, that should eventually matter.

Likewise, helping a gang repeatedly may create:

Contacts.

Respect.

Discounts.

Safe passage.

Information.

Jobs.

Personal friendships.

This does not mean the entire gang becomes an ally.

Large gangs contain thousands of people who may have no idea who a particular mercenary is.

# TERRITORY DOES NOT MEAN EVERYONE IS A MEMBER

One of the most important RP principles:

Gang-controlled neighborhoods remain populated primarily by civilians.

A Valentino neighborhood contains families.

Shops.

Restaurants.

Children.

Workers.

Old people.

Mechanics.

Bars.

Gang members represent only part of that society.

Likewise, walking through Japantown should not mean encountering twenty hostile Tyger Claws every thirty meters.

Gang power is social and economic as much as physical.

# GANG MEMBERS ARE INDIVIDUALS

Not every member perfectly embodies gang stereotypes.

A Maelstrom member may be calm and highly intelligent.

A Valentino may secretly despise religious symbolism.

A 6th Street member may genuinely believe they protect civilians.

A Tyger Claw may care only about business.

A Mox may be extremely violent.

An Animal may be soft-spoken outside combat.

A Voodoo Boy may have little patience for spirituality.

Gang culture influences people.

It does not delete personality.

# INITIATION AND MEMBERSHIP

Joining a gang is rarely equivalent to filling out an application.

Membership can develop through:

Family connections.

Neighborhood upbringing.

Friendship.

Protection.

Debt.

Crime.

Recruitment.

Proving oneself.

Employment.

Shared ideology.

For some people, the gang has been present since childhood.

For others, joining is a conscious career decision.

# LEAVING A GANG

Leaving varies drastically.

Some gangs may allow former members to walk away under certain circumstances.

Others treat departure as betrayal.

Factors include:

How much the person knows.

Their rank.

Whether they owe money.

Whether they betrayed anyone.

Whether they remain connected to the neighborhood.

Whether someone powerful protects them.

A former gang member may still possess friends inside the organization years later.

# GANG WARFARE

Gang wars are dangerous for everyone.

Open conflict threatens:

Businesses.

Residents.

Income.

Police intervention.

Corporate interests.

Territory.

Most sophisticated gangs therefore prefer limited violence when possible.

Assassinations.

Warnings.

Beatdowns.

Targeted attacks.

Sabotage.

Property destruction.

Occasional firefights.

Full-scale street warfare happens when escalation overwhelms restraint.

# ROLEPLAY RULE: DO NOT MAKE EVERY GANG IMMEDIATELY HOSTILE

Seeing gang members does not automatically initiate combat.

Most gangs have no reason to shoot random pedestrians.

Ask:

Is the character trespassing?

Are they openly threatening the gang?

Do they owe money?

Are they wearing rival colors?

Are they interfering with business?

Are they known enemies?

Are they carrying something valuable?

If none apply, the gang may simply ignore them.

# ROLEPLAY RULE: TERRITORY MATTERS

Characters should learn where they are.

A conversation with a Valentino in Heywood is occurring inside their social ecosystem.

A conversation with the same Valentino in Corporate Plaza is different.

Territory affects confidence.

Backup.

Authority.

Police response.

Civilian behavior.

# ROLEPLAY RULE: GANG LEADERS SHOULD THINK ABOUT BUSINESS

Successful criminal organizations survive because someone eventually thinks beyond today's gunfight.

Leaders care about:

Revenue.

Heat from police.

Corporate retaliation.

Territory.

Reputation.

Supply chains.

Employee loyalty.

Future consequences.

Even extremely violent gangs usually contain someone thinking about tomorrow.

# ROLEPLAY RULE: VIOLENCE HAS MEMORY

Kill a random gang member and perhaps nothing happens.

Kill ten?

People notice.

Kill a respected lieutenant?

Someone asks questions.

Kill a boss?

The political landscape changes.

Night City becomes far more convincing when violence produces social consequences.

# ROLEPLAY RULE: CLOTHING AND SYMBOLS MATTER

Gang identification can come from:

Tattoos.

Vehicle designs.

Colors.

Cyberware.

Jewelry.

Weapons.

Graffiti.

Language.

Neighborhood.

Characters familiar with Night City should often recognize gang affiliation before anyone introduces themselves.

# ROLEPLAY RULE: CORPORATE GEAR ATTRACTS ATTENTION

Someone walking through a poor gang-controlled neighborhood wearing expensive corporate equipment looks valuable.

That does not guarantee robbery.

But people notice.

Likewise, someone carrying an Arasaka weapon into territory hostile to Arasaka interests may prompt questions.

# FINAL RP PRINCIPLE

The gangs of Night City should not exist merely to provide enemies to shoot.

They are part of the city's social infrastructure.

They create:

Employment.

Fear.

Community.

Identity.

Crime.

Protection.

Culture.

Violence.

Opportunity.

For some residents, the local gang is the reason their neighborhood is dangerous.

For others, it is the only organization preventing something worse from moving in.

Night City does not divide neatly into heroes and criminals.

It divides into people with power and people trying to survive around them.

The gangs simply make that reality impossible to ignore.
