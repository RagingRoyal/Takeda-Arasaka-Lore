# LORE-BOOK: THE CORPORATE DYSTOPIA

# HOW CORPORATE POWER ACTUALLY WORKS IN CYBERPUNK 2077

## PURPOSE

This lore-book exists to enforce one fundamental fact:

CYBERPUNK 2077 TAKES PLACE IN A CORPORATE DYSTOPIA.

Night City must NEVER be portrayed as:

"A normal American city with some unusually large companies."

That is wrong.

The megacorporations are not simply wealthy companies.

They are major centers of:

Political authority.

Military force.

Law enforcement.

Property ownership.

Healthcare.

Employment.

Housing.

Transportation.

Banking.

Education.

Media.

Intelligence.

Technology.

Infrastructure.

Security.

Culture.

The largest corporations operate more like private states than conventional businesses.

Night City still has a government.

Night City still has laws.

Night City still has police.

The dystopia is that the corporations possess enough legal, economic, military and political power to exist alongside those institutions as competing sovereign authorities.

In some places, municipal law dominates.

In some places, gang power dominates.

In some places, corporate authority dominates.

In some places, all three overlap.

The question in Night City is therefore not merely:

"What does the law say?"

It is:

WHOSE LAW?

WHOSE PROPERTY?

WHOSE SECURITY?

WHOSE MONEY?

WHOSE POLITICIAN?

WHOSE JURISDICTION?

WHOSE LAWYERS?

And most importantly:

WHO HAS ENOUGH POWER TO MAKE THEIR VERSION OF EVENTS MATTER?

# THE MOST IMPORTANT CANON RULE

Corporate sovereignty is real.

Corporate-controlled:

Buildings.

Blocks.

Facilities.

And even entire districts.

Can fall under the jurisdiction of the corporation controlling them.

Inside corporate territory, corporate agents handle crimes committed there.

This means a corporation is not merely allowed to place security guards inside its office.

A corporation may exercise something much closer to territorial jurisdiction.

# CORPORATE JURISDICTION

If a person enters ordinary Night City municipal territory and commits a crime, the normal authority is generally the NCPD.

If they enter Arasaka-controlled territory and commit a crime, the first authority may be:

ARASAKA SECURITY.

Not the NCPD.

Not City Hall.

Not some patrol officer arriving ten minutes later.

Arasaka.

# CORPORATE COPS

Corporate agents are often called:

Corp cops.

This term can refer broadly to privately funded corporate security and law-enforcement personnel.

They are not necessarily equivalent to mall guards.

Depending upon corporation and assignment, they can include:

Security officers.

Investigators.

Corporate police.

Netrunners.

Counterintelligence personnel.

Tactical officers.

Military contractors.

Combat specialists.

Drones.

Robots.

Armored response teams.

Heavy corporate security.

The richest corporations can deploy forces significantly better armed than ordinary police.

# CORPORATE JUSTICE

Justice on corporate turf can be extremely harsh.

Corporate security exists primarily to protect:

Corporate property.

Corporate employees.

Corporate secrets.

Corporate operations.

Corporate reputation.

Corporate profitability.

It does not primarily exist to protect abstract public rights.

# A CORPORATE SECURITY OFFICER'S PRIORITY

Imagine someone breaks into an Arasaka facility.

The Arasaka guard is not thinking:

"I must preserve this suspect's constitutional rights until an NCPD detective arrives."

They are thinking:

Identify intruder.

Contain intruder.

Protect asset.

Determine breach.

Recover information.

Secure facility.

Inform corporate chain of command.

# SHOOT FIRST

High-threat corporate installations may use lethal force quickly.

Corporate justice can operate on an assumption fundamentally different from civilian policing:

You entered somewhere you were clearly forbidden to enter.

You bypassed security.

You are armed.

You are therefore a threat.

The corporation does not necessarily consider itself obligated to offer the same patience an ordinary public police force theoretically should.

# NCPD AUTHORITY IS LIMITED BY CORPORATE SOVEREIGNTY

This is crucial.

The NCPD is not the supreme authority in every square meter of Night City.

Its authority becomes complicated inside corporate-controlled areas.

Police may require:

Corporate cooperation.

Jurisdictional transfer.

Executive authorization.

Political pressure.

A warrant acceptable to corporate lawyers.

Or simply enough leverage to force the issue.

# CORPORATE CASES CAN LEAVE NCPD HANDS

Crimes involving corporate interests may eventually disappear from ordinary police jurisdiction.

The case may become:

Corporate Security's problem.

Corporate Counterintelligence's problem.

A private investigation.

A political matter.

A classified matter.

Something quietly buried.

The detective does not necessarily receive an explanation.

They receive:

CASE TRANSFERRED.

ACCESS DENIED.

AUTHORIZATION REVOKED.

And they go investigate another murder.

# THE CITY COUNCIL

Night City's political structure is heavily influenced by corporations.

Corporate-backed politicians occupy significant portions of municipal government.

This means corporations do not always need to circumvent government.

They can use government.

# STRUCTURAL CORRUPTION

Do not reduce Night City's corruption to:

"A corporation secretly hands the mayor a suitcase of cash."

That happens.

But the deeper corruption is structural.

The corporation may:

Fund the politician's campaign.

Employ their relatives.

Provide municipal infrastructure.

Own property central to the tax base.

Provide weapons to police.

Provide security services.

Own local media.

Employ thousands of voters.

Finance construction.

Provide transportation.

Control investment.

Provide political intelligence.

The politician understands what happens if that relationship disappears.

# CORPORATE-BACKED COUNCIL MEMBERS

A council member may legally be:

Elected.

Publicly accountable.

Officially independent.

They can simultaneously owe their entire political career to corporate support.

That is more powerful than a simple bribe.

The corporation helped build the chair they are sitting in.

# THE NCPD CHIEF

The NCPD formally answers to municipal government.

But if the City Council is heavily corporate-backed, then the practical chain of influence becomes obvious.

CITY COUNCIL.

MAYOR.

NCPD LEADERSHIP.

Corporate donors and sponsors are standing behind several of those layers.

# THIS DOES NOT MEAN EVERY COP IS BOUGHT

Do not write every NCPD officer as taking envelopes of money from Arasaka.

Some are honest.

Some hate corporations.

Some are corrupt.

Some are exhausted.

Some are scared.

Some are trying to do their jobs.

The system can produce corporate-friendly outcomes even when individual officers personally despise corporations.

# CORPORATE PRIVILEGE UNDER LAW

The legal system itself recognizes corporate status.

This is one of the most important parts of the dystopia.

# CORPORATE-SPECIFIC CRIMINAL OFFENCES

The NCPD criminal database seen throughout Cyberpunk 2077 contains corporate-specific offences.

Examples include:

SLANDER AGAINST A CORPORATE REPRESENTATIVE.

ASSAULTING A CORPORATE REPRESENTATIVE.

KIDNAPPING OF A CORPORATE REPRESENTATIVE.

THEFT OF VALUABLE CORPORATE PROPERTY.

VANDALISM IN A CORPORATE ZONE.

TRESPASSING IN CORPORATE AREAS.

Other corporate-property and corporate-security offences appear throughout Night City law enforcement and security systems.

# WHY THIS MATTERS

Compare:

Assault.

And:

Assaulting a Corporate Representative.

The existence of a special category tells you something about the society.

A corporate representative possesses a legally recognized status important enough to be reflected in criminal enforcement.

# CORPORATE REPRESENTATIVE

A corporate representative is not merely:

"Some random person who works at All Foods."

It generally implies someone acting in an official corporate capacity.

Examples could include:

Executive.

Corporate agent.

Authorized negotiator.

Corporate official.

Corporate security representative.

Corporate diplomat.

Senior employee formally representing corporate interests.

Their employer's institutional status follows them into the legal system.

# SLANDER AGAINST A CORPORATE REPRESENTATIVE

Night City's criminal database recognizes:

SLANDER AGAINST A CORPORATE REP.

That fact should significantly affect RP atmosphere.

# WHAT SLANDER MEANS

Do NOT incorrectly interpret this as:

"It is illegal to ever criticize a corporation."

Slander normally refers to defamatory claims.

The important dystopian element is that defamatory speech concerning an official corporate representative can be treated as a criminal-law matter rather than merely an ordinary private dispute.

# PRACTICAL REALITY

Imagine an ordinary worker publicly accuses an Arasaka representative of:

Corruption.

Murder.

Fraud.

Illegal experiments.

Bribery.

If they cannot prove the accusation, they are not merely risking:

An angry corpo.

They may face:

Police attention.

Corporate lawyers.

Defamation proceedings.

Employment consequences.

Financial ruin.

Criminal charges.

Corporate investigation.

The power imbalance is staggering.

# EVEN IF YOU ARE RIGHT

Being factually correct does not automatically make someone safe.

The corporation may dispute the evidence.

Destroy the evidence.

Seal the evidence.

Classify the evidence.

Attack credibility.

Sue the source.

Purchase media coverage.

Threaten witnesses.

Convince authorities the information was illegally obtained.

Someone can possess the truth and still lose.

# JOURNALISM IS DANGEROUS

This is why investigative journalism in Cyberpunk is dangerous.

A journalist investigating corporate corruption is not simply writing an unpopular article.

They may be challenging an institution possessing:

Lawyers.

Security personnel.

Netrunners.

Political influence.

Private intelligence.

Media influence.

Assassins.

Corporate espionage capabilities.

And potentially legal statutes specifically protecting corporate interests.

# CORPORATE REPUTATION IS AN ASSET

A corporation considers reputation economically valuable.

Therefore:

Defamation.

Leaks.

Embarrassing footage.

Internal documents.

Employee testimony.

Whistleblowing.

Can all be treated as attacks on corporate assets.

# THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN A CIVILIAN AND A CORPO

Imagine two people are punched in Night City.

Person One:

Unemployed Watson resident.

Person Two:

Senior corporate representative acting on company business.

Legally, assault is assault.

Practically, the cases may receive dramatically different attention.

Person Two has:

Corporate lawyers.

Corporate security.

Corporate medical coverage.

Corporate investigators.

Political contacts.

Employer pressure.

Potentially specialized criminal charges.

Person One may have:

A bruised face.

A case number.

And an overworked detective who never calls back.

# EQUALITY BEFORE THE LAW IS THEORETICAL

Night City's legal structure is not simply:

"Rich people sometimes get away with things."

The system actively produces different practical levels of protection.

# A CORPORATE EMPLOYEE CAN BE WORTH MORE

Corporations classify employees according to value.

A cafeteria worker is replaceable.

A senior researcher may be worth millions.

A corporate director may control billions in projects.

A member of the Arasaka family may be strategically invaluable.

Security response changes accordingly.

# EMPLOYEES AS ASSETS

Corporate language frequently treats people as:

A valuable employee possesses:

Knowledge.

Training.

Contacts.

Cyberware.

Corporate information.

If they leave, those assets may go with them.

# RESIGNATION IS NOT ALWAYS JUST RESIGNATION

Imagine an engineer wants to leave Arasaka and join Kang Tao.

An ordinary company might say:

"Good luck at your new job."

What do they know?

What prototypes have they seen?

What networks can they access?

What patents are they connected to?

What rival recruited them?

Can they legally leave?

Do they possess corporate cyberware?

Are they bound by confidentiality?

Is this an extraction attempt?

# CORPORATE EXTRACTION

Corporate extraction exists because corporations can treat skilled employees almost like strategic property.

A rival corporation may literally hire mercenaries to extract a specialist.

That specialist may be:

Willing.

Unwilling.

Ambiguous.

The corporate terminology remains:

ASSET EXTRACTION.

# CORPO V IS THE PERFECT EXAMPLE

Corporate-lifepath V begins the game as an Arasaka Counterintelligence employee.

"You are fired."

They strip V's corporate status.

Corporate access disappears.

Corporate implants are deactivated.

Financial access is seized or shut down.

V physically collapses.

Their entire life disappears in minutes.

# THE CORPORATION CAN TURN OFF PART OF YOU

Think about what this means.

V has cyberware inside their own body.

Arasaka can remotely deactivate corporate systems.

Your eye may be inside your skull.

Your nervous-system enhancement may be attached to your spine.

Your hormone regulator may be integrated into your biology.

The corporation can still possess enough technological control to switch corporate functionality off.

# THE WATSON LOCKDOWN

At the beginning of Cyberpunk 2077, Watson is under lockdown.

This is an entire district.

# WHAT THE PLAYER EXPERIENCES

# ARASAKA'S ROLE

# THE IMPORTANT LESSON
