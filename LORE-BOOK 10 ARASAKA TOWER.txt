# THE OLD ARASAKA TOWERS

The current Tower is not the first Arasaka headquarters built on the site.

Before 2023, Arasaka maintained a massive twin-tower complex in Corporate Plaza.

Those towers were among the largest corporate structures in Night City.

They contained:

Administration.

Executive offices.

Corporate apartments.

Security installations.

Research laboratories.

Data systems.

Military personnel.

Vehicle facilities.

Underground infrastructure.

They were destroyed during the 2023 Arasaka Tower assault and nuclear disaster.

# THE 2023 DESTRUCTION

On August 20, 2023, a major Militech-linked assault struck the Arasaka complex.

Participants included legendary mercenaries and operatives associated with the Fourth Corporate War.

A nuclear device detonated within the complex.

The original towers were destroyed.

Corporate Plaza was devastated.

Hundreds of thousands died during the disaster and its aftermath.

Arasaka was subsequently driven from North America.

For decades, the destroyed Arasaka headquarters existed as one of the defining scars of Night City's history.

# THE TIME OF THE RED

The area surrounding the former corporate center became part of the ruined Hot Zone.

The destruction was too extensive to immediately rebuild.

For decades, the old Corporate Center remained associated with:

Radiation.

Ruins.

Salvage.

War damage.

Mass death.

The disappearance of Arasaka from North America.

# ARASAKA RETURNS

The political situation changed during the Unification War.

Night City faced the possibility of NUSA occupation.

Arasaka intervened militarily on Night City's behalf.

The threat of another direct Arasaka-Militech confrontation helped force negotiations.

When the war ended in 2070, Arasaka was welcomed back into Night City.

# THE NEW TOWER

Arasaka received permission to establish a new regional headquarters.

It chose the most politically provocative location imaginable:

The site of its previous headquarters.

The ruins were cleared.

Construction began.

Within only a few years, a new Arasaka Tower rose over Corporate Plaza.

The new Tower stands approximately:

620 meters.

140 floors.

It is taller than every other building in Night City.

# WHY REBUILD ON THE SAME SITE?

Because Arasaka understands symbolism.

Building somewhere else would imply:

Retreat.

Defeat.

Instead Arasaka returned to the exact place where its former headquarters had been destroyed.

The new Tower announces:

YOU FAILED TO REMOVE US.
