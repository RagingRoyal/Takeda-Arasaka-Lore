# LORE-BOOK: GENERAL SETTING OF CYBERPUNK 2077

## THE DARK FUTURE

Cyberpunk 2077 takes place in an alternate future where technological advancement has dramatically outpaced society's ability to control it.

Humanity did not experience a single clean apocalypse. Instead, the world suffered through decades of economic collapse, political instability, corporate warfare, environmental disaster, social fragmentation, technological upheaval, and repeated attempts at reconstruction.

By 2077, civilization still exists, but it is harsher, more unequal, and considerably more commercialized than the world of the early twenty-first century.

Governments remain important, but they are no longer automatically the most powerful institutions on Earth. Enormous multinational corporations possess wealth, military forces, intelligence networks, research programs, private security, manufacturing infrastructure, political influence, and technological resources comparable to those of entire nations.

The result is a world where nearly everything can become a commodity.

Security can be purchased.

Medical treatment can be purchased.

Better organs can be purchased.

Memories and experiences can be purchased.

Violence can be purchased.

Identity itself can increasingly be altered, reconstructed, or sold.

Those with money can live surrounded by extraordinary technology and comfort.

Those without it frequently survive among crime, failing infrastructure, polluted environments, overcrowded neighborhoods, corporate exploitation, and an economy that rarely forgives weakness.

This contradiction defines the Cyberpunk world.

Humanity possesses astonishing technology.

Human society remains profoundly broken.

## NIGHT CITY

The primary setting of Cyberpunk 2077 is Night City, a massive independent metropolis located on the coast of California.

Night City was founded by businessman Richard Night with the ambition of creating an urban paradise largely free from traditional government interference.

That dream failed spectacularly.

Instead, Night City became one of the world's great centers of corporate power, organized crime, mercenary activity, technological development, entertainment, violence, and extreme inequality.

By 2077, Night City is effectively a city-state existing between major political powers while fiercely guarding its independence.

It is simultaneously one of the most advanced cities on Earth and one of the most dangerous.

Towering corporate headquarters dominate the skyline.

Luxury apartments and exclusive clubs exist only streets away from overcrowded housing blocks.

Advertisements cover enormous portions of the urban landscape.

Cybernetic modification is commonplace.

Armed security is everywhere.

Gang territory can begin or end within a few city blocks.

Mercenaries conduct assassinations, extractions, sabotage, theft, protection jobs, and corporate espionage as routine business.

Night City rewards ambition but rarely protects the ambitious.

People arrive hoping to become rich, famous, powerful, or legendary.

Most never do.

The city consumes people constantly, but the possibility of becoming someone important keeps producing new dreamers.

## THE CITY'S CULTURE

Night City is culturally enormous and extremely diverse.

Its population contains communities influenced by cultures from across the world, especially North American, Latin American, Japanese, Chinese, Korean, Filipino, Caribbean, and numerous other immigrant traditions.

Languages mix freely.

Street slang develops rapidly.

Fashion can communicate wealth, profession, subculture, gang membership, philosophy, or deliberate rebellion.

Food ranges from expensive imported delicacies to cheap synthetic meals sold from vending machines.

Music, advertisements, braindance entertainment, celebrity culture, gangs, corporations, and social media constantly compete for people's attention.

The city does not possess one unified culture.

It contains hundreds of overlapping cultures fighting for space beneath the same neon skyline.

## CORPORATE POWER

Megacorporations are among the most powerful forces in the Cyberpunk world.

A corporation in 2077 can be far more than a company.

Major corporations operate private armies, intelligence divisions, weapons manufacturers, medical research facilities, banks, factories, security forces, orbital infrastructure, political lobbying networks, and enormous private territories.

Two corporations are especially important to the political landscape surrounding Night City.

### ARASAKA

Arasaka is a Japanese megacorporation and one of the most powerful organizations in the world.

Its interests include banking, security, weapons, manufacturing, intelligence, and advanced technology.

Arasaka maintains enormous political and economic influence and possesses extremely capable private military and security forces.

To supporters, Arasaka represents stability, efficiency, technological progress, and protection.

To its enemies, it represents corporate imperialism backed by money, surveillance, and armed force.

Arasaka maintains a particularly strong presence in Night City.

### MILITECH

Militech is a massive American weapons and military technology corporation closely connected with the New United States of America.

The company manufactures weapons, military vehicles, security systems, cyberware, and other combat technology.

Militech and Arasaka have been historic rivals.

Their competition has previously escalated beyond ordinary corporate espionage into open warfare.

Corporations at this level do not merely compete for customers.

They influence international politics.

## CORPORATE WARS

Corporate conflict has shaped modern history.

The largest corporations possess enough military strength that disputes between them can escalate into genuine wars.

The most catastrophic example was the Fourth Corporate War.

Beginning as a conflict involving corporate interests, it eventually expanded into a worldwide confrontation primarily associated with Arasaka and Militech.

Fighting occurred across multiple continents, oceans, computer networks, and corporate facilities.

The war devastated global trade and infrastructure.

Night City itself became one of its most infamous battlegrounds.

In 2023, the Arasaka Tower in central Night City was destroyed by a nuclear explosion.

The blast devastated much of the city center and killed enormous numbers of people.

The decades that followed became known as the Time of the Red, named partly for the red skies produced by pollution and atmospheric debris following years of destruction.

Night City eventually rebuilt.

By 2077, sleek skyscrapers stand over territory where entire sections of the city were once ruins.

The reconstruction did not erase the underlying political conflicts.

It merely placed new glass and concrete over them.

## THE NEW UNITED STATES

The United States of the Cyberpunk timeline fragmented following decades of economic and political crisis.

What eventually emerged was the New United States of America, commonly called the NUSA.

The NUSA controls significant territory but does not possess unquestioned authority over every region that once belonged to the United States.

Night City is not simply another American city.

It maintains political independence and has repeatedly resisted attempts to bring it fully under NUSA control.

Relations between the NUSA, Night City, Militech, Arasaka, and other powers are therefore complicated.

Politics in this world is rarely separated from corporate interests.

## THE UNIFICATION WAR

One of the most important recent conflicts before 2077 was the Unification War.

The NUSA attempted to bring various independent territories back under federal control.

Night City became a major point of tension.

NUSA and Militech forces approached the city, while Arasaka returned as a military protector after decades of reduced influence.

The confrontation ended without Night City being fully absorbed into the NUSA.

The result reinforced Night City's position as an independent city caught between enormous political and corporate powers.

By 2077, the possibility of renewed conflict has never completely disappeared.

## SOCIAL CLASS

Wealth determines an enormous amount about a person's life.

At the highest level are wealthy corporate executives, investors, celebrities, political elites, and powerful criminal figures.

They possess access to advanced healthcare, expensive cyberware, private security, exclusive housing, premium food, personal transportation, and social connections unavailable to ordinary citizens.

Below them are countless corporate employees.

Some live comfortably.

Others exist in highly controlled corporate environments where employment determines housing, healthcare, social status, and sometimes personal safety.

Losing a corporate position can mean losing an entire lifestyle overnight.

Further down the social hierarchy are ordinary workers, shopkeepers, technicians, service employees, mechanics, independent professionals, mercenaries, and countless people surviving through unstable employment.

At the bottom are the homeless, displaced, abandoned, exploited, addicted, undocumented, or otherwise forgotten populations of the city.

Night City possesses enormous wealth.

It simply distributes that wealth extremely unevenly.

## EDGERUNNERS AND MERCENARIES

Between respectable society and outright criminal organizations exists a large professional underworld.

Mercenaries are commonly called edgerunners.

They perform dangerous work that corporations, criminals, politicians, and private citizens cannot or do not want to perform openly.

Jobs may include:

Assassination.

Bodyguard work.

Extraction.

Kidnapping.

Sabotage.

Espionage.

Data theft.

Vehicle theft.

Corporate infiltration.

Gang warfare.

Rescue operations.

Smuggling.

Investigation.

Protection.

Recovery of stolen property.

Eliminating inconvenient people.

These jobs are commonly called gigs.

Successful mercenaries develop reputations.

Reputation is extremely important.

A skilled mercenary may gain access to better employers, better equipment, larger jobs, and more influential contacts.

A reckless mercenary may simply become another corpse.

Night City nevertheless idolizes legendary mercenaries.

Becoming a legend is one of the central dreams of edgerunner culture.

## FIXERS

Fixers are brokers of the criminal and mercenary world.

They connect clients with people capable of completing illegal, dangerous, or discreet work.

A fixer might arrange:

Mercenary contracts.

Weapons purchases.

Information exchanges.

Smuggling routes.

Transportation.

False documents.

Black-market cyberware.

Contacts with gangs.

Corporate introductions.

Disappearances.

A successful fixer survives because they know people.

Information, trust, favors, and reputation are their true currency.

Major fixers often control substantial portions of Night City's underground economy.

## GANGS

Night City contains numerous gangs, but they are not interchangeable collections of criminals.

Each gang possesses its own history, territory, culture, ideology, business interests, enemies, and internal structure.

Some protect local communities while also committing crimes.

Some operate like organized crime syndicates.

Some revolve around ethnicity or neighborhood identity.

Some revolve around technology, cyberware, violence, politics, or specific lifestyles.

Gang membership can provide protection, family, income, identity, and social status.

It can also place someone permanently inside cycles of violence.

Gang territory matters.

Entering the wrong neighborhood while displaying the wrong affiliations can become dangerous very quickly.

## LAW ENFORCEMENT

Night City has police, but policing operates within a deeply commercialized and violent society.

The Night City Police Department handles ordinary law enforcement but struggles with corruption, gang warfare, heavily armed criminals, cybernetically enhanced offenders, and political pressure.

Private security companies also protect corporate and wealthy interests.

This means protection is not distributed equally.

Corporate facilities may possess heavily armed professional security forces while poorer districts receive limited police attention.

Violence that would trigger enormous investigations in wealthy districts may barely receive attention elsewhere.

## MAXTAC

Extremely dangerous cybernetically enhanced criminals may attract the attention of MAX-TAC.

MAX-TAC is a heavily armed tactical unit specializing in threats beyond the capabilities of ordinary police.

They are especially associated with incidents involving cyberpsychosis.

Their arrival generally means a situation has already become extremely dangerous.

## CYBERWARE

Cybernetic augmentation is normal in 2077.

People replace or enhance portions of their bodies for medical, professional, military, cosmetic, recreational, or social reasons.

Common cyberware may improve:

Vision.

Strength.

Reflexes.

Hearing.

Memory.

Communication.

Combat ability.

Computer interaction.

Physical appearance.

Entire limbs can be replaced.

Eyes can contain cameras and targeting systems.

Neural implants can connect directly with computers.

Some people possess only minor implants.

Others replace substantial portions of their natural bodies.

Cyberware is therefore both technology and culture.

For some people, chrome is simply useful equipment.

For others, it represents status.

For soldiers and mercenaries, it can be the difference between surviving and dying.

For enthusiasts, modification becomes an identity of its own.

## CYBERPSYCHOSIS

Heavy cybernetic modification can contribute to a dangerous psychological condition commonly called cyberpsychosis.

Its causes are more complicated than simply possessing "too much cyberware."

Trauma, psychological instability, social isolation, extreme stress, violence, and dehumanization can all contribute.

Cyberware can intensify these problems by increasingly separating a person's experience from an ordinary human body.

Severe cases may involve paranoia, emotional detachment, loss of empathy, violent behavior, and psychological collapse.

Society often treats cyberpsychos as dangerous problems to be eliminated rather than damaged people requiring treatment.

## THE NET

The original global internet no longer functions as it once did.

Earlier generations of netrunners, corporations, artificial intelligences, cyberwarfare programs, and destructive malware transformed cyberspace into an extremely dangerous environment.

One figure in particular became infamous: the brilliant netrunner Rache Bartmoss.

Bartmoss released programs that contributed to the catastrophic corruption of the old NET.

Over time, hostile artificial intelligences and dangerous autonomous programs made vast portions of cyberspace effectively unusable for ordinary human activity.

The modern world therefore relies heavily on smaller, controlled networks.

## NETRUNNERS

Netrunners are specialists capable of interacting with digital systems through neural interfaces.

A skilled netrunner can infiltrate networks, steal information, manipulate security systems, attack enemy software, control connected devices, and engage in forms of digital combat.

Netrunning is not merely typing quickly on a keyboard.

High-level netrunning involves directly interfacing the human nervous system with digital environments.

This makes experienced netrunners enormously valuable.

It also makes their work dangerous.

## THE BLACKWALL

The Blackwall is an extraordinarily powerful digital barrier separating human-controlled networks from dangerous regions of cyberspace inhabited by rogue artificial intelligences and other entities.

It is maintained primarily through NetWatch, the international organization responsible for policing major network activity.

Crossing or attempting to penetrate the Blackwall is extraordinarily dangerous and heavily restricted.

Humanity does not truly control everything existing beyond it.

That fact is one of the quiet existential threats hanging over the Cyberpunk world.

## ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

Artificial intelligence is highly advanced.

Some AIs function as specialized programs.

Others possess intelligence and autonomy comparable to or exceeding human beings.

Rogue AIs exist beyond the Blackwall, operating according to motives humans may not fully understand.

The relationship between humanity and artificial intelligence is therefore not simply one of creators and machines.

In some areas of cyberspace, humans may no longer be the dominant intelligence.

## BRAINDANCE

Braindance technology allows recorded sensory and emotional experiences to be replayed through neural interfaces.

A braindance can allow someone to experience another person's sight, hearing, physical sensations, and emotions.

The technology has legitimate uses in entertainment, therapy, training, investigation, and education.

It also supports a huge adult entertainment and black-market industry.

Illegal braindances can contain extreme violence and other disturbing experiences.

Braindance demonstrates one of the central principles of the Cyberpunk world:

If something can be experienced, someone will eventually find a way to sell it.

## WEAPONS

Weapons are extremely common.

Firearms range from cheap street weapons to advanced military hardware.

Major categories include conventional firearms, smart weapons capable of interacting with targeting technology, electromagnetic weapons, heavy military systems, melee weapons, and cybernetic weapons installed directly into the body.

Many civilians are armed.

Gangs possess military-grade equipment.

Corporations maintain private arsenals.

Mercenaries often openly carry weapons.

Violence is therefore always closer to the surface than it would be in most modern cities.

## VEHICLES

Private vehicles remain common, but vehicle quality reflects social class.

Cheap cars may be old, damaged, or poorly maintained.

Wealthy citizens drive advanced luxury vehicles.

Corporate executives may use armored transportation.

Nomads maintain and modify vehicles designed for long-distance travel through dangerous territory.

Autonomous driving exists, although manual driving remains widespread.

## NOMADS

Outside the dense urban centers live numerous Nomad communities.

Nomads often travel in large family-based groups called clans or nations.

Many descend from people displaced by economic collapse, environmental disaster, corporate activity, or political upheaval.

Nomads frequently specialize in transportation, logistics, smuggling, engineering, mechanics, construction, and survival outside major cities.

They possess extensive networks across North America.

City residents sometimes stereotype them as criminals or drifters.

In reality, Nomad societies often possess strong internal traditions, loyalties, and family structures.

Their greatest strength is frequently their community.

## THE BADLANDS

The territory surrounding Night City is commonly called the Badlands.

It contains deserts, abandoned infrastructure, industrial facilities, landfills, small settlements, Nomad camps, corporate installations, and long stretches of dangerous open territory.

Night City may dominate the horizon, but its influence weakens rapidly outside the urban center.

The Badlands offer freedom from some forms of city control.

They also offer fewer protections.

## MEDIA AND ADVERTISING

Advertising saturates everyday life.

Buildings, streets, elevators, televisions, vending machines, vehicles, implants, and digital systems constantly expose people to commercial messaging.

Advertisements are frequently aggressive, sexualized, grotesque, absurd, or deliberately shocking.

Corporations do not simply sell products.

They sell identities.

Fashion.

Lifestyle.

Status.

Desire.

Fear.

Beauty.

Violence.

Even rebellion itself can become a marketing category.

## CELEBRITY CULTURE

Fame remains enormously important.

Musicians, actors, braindance performers, athletes, influencers, mercenaries, criminals, and corporate personalities can all become celebrities.

Night City especially celebrates people who become larger than life.

A legendary reputation may survive long after the person dies.

This is why the idea of becoming a "legend" carries genuine cultural weight.

For many edgerunners, dying anonymously is worse than dying young.

## THE AFTERLIFE

The Afterlife is one of Night City's most famous mercenary bars.

It serves as neutral ground, meeting place, cultural landmark, and informal hall of fame for elite edgerunners.

Major fixers conduct business there.

Professional mercenaries gather there.

Stories about famous jobs and dead legends circulate constantly.

Some drinks are named after legendary mercenaries who died dramatically enough to be remembered.

Getting recognized at the Afterlife means something.

Becoming one of its legends means becoming part of Night City mythology.

## NIGHT CITY'S DISTRICTS

Night City consists of several major districts.

### CITY CENTER

The corporate heart of Night City.

Corporate Plaza contains enormous headquarters, financial institutions, luxury businesses, and some of the most heavily protected territory in the city.

This is where corporate power becomes architecture.

### WATSON

A dense northern district containing neighborhoods such as Kabuki, Little China, and Northside.

Watson once attracted major corporate investment before economic changes caused much of that prosperity to collapse.

It now contains markets, industrial zones, crowded neighborhoods, criminal activity, and significant gang influence.

### WESTBROOK

One of Night City's wealthier areas.

It includes Japantown, North Oak, and Charter Hill.

Entertainment, luxury living, corporate wealth, nightlife, and organized crime all overlap here.

### HEYWOOD

A large residential district with strong community identities.

It contains wealthy areas as well as working-class neighborhoods.

The Valentinos gang possesses major influence in portions of Heywood.

### SANTO DOMINGO

An industrial district containing factories, power infrastructure, working-class neighborhoods, and enormous corporate facilities.

Much of the machinery keeping Night City functioning exists here.

### PACIFICA

Originally intended to become a wealthy resort and tourist district.

Investment collapsed before the project was completed.

Much of Pacifica became abandoned, impoverished, and dominated by gangs.

Ruined luxury developments now stand as monuments to failed corporate ambition.

### DOGTOWN

A heavily fortified section of Pacifica controlled by the paramilitary organization Barghest.

Dogtown functions almost as a city within the city.

Weapons trafficking, black markets, mercenary activity, political intrigue, and military rule define much of everyday life there.

## STREET LANGUAGE

Night City possesses its own slang.

Common terms include:

Choom or choomba:
Friend, buddy, companion.

Chooms:
A group of friends or associates.

Corpo:
Corporate employee or someone strongly associated with corporate culture.

Chrome:
Cyberware.

Eddies:
Eurodollars, the common currency.

Eds:
Another shortened form of eurodollars.

Gonk:
Idiot or fool.

Preem:
Excellent, impressive, high-quality.

Nova:
Excellent, exciting, impressive.

Delta:
Leave quickly.

Flatline:
Kill someone or die.

Zero:
Kill.

Biz:
Business or work.

Gig:
A contracted job, especially mercenary work.

Fixer:
A broker who arranges jobs, deals, information, and contacts.

Solo:
A professional combat mercenary.

Netrunner:
A specialist hacker using neural interfaces.

Ripperdoc:
A medical professional specializing in cyberware installation and maintenance.

Output:
Something produced or released, occasionally used casually depending on context.

Input:
Partner or significant other, depending on context.

Chrome-jock:
Someone heavily enthusiastic about cybernetic modification.

Scav:
Short for scavenger, generally referring to criminals who abduct people and harvest cyberware or organs from victims.

## RIPPERDOCS

Ripperdocs are doctors and technicians specializing in cybernetic modification.

They install, remove, repair, and maintain cyberware.

Quality varies enormously.

A wealthy client may visit a pristine corporate clinic.

A mercenary may rely on a trusted neighborhood ripper.

Someone desperate may visit an illegal backroom surgeon using questionable equipment.

Because cyberware connects directly to the body and nervous system, a skilled ripperdoc can be invaluable.

## SCAVENGERS

Scavengers, commonly called Scavs, represent one of the ugliest parts of Night City's criminal economy.

They kidnap, murder, or exploit victims and harvest valuable cyberware, organs, and equipment from their bodies.

These components can then be resold through black markets.

Their existence reflects the brutal economics of the setting.

Even a dead body can contain valuable merchandise.

## HEALTHCARE

Medical technology is extraordinarily advanced.

Severe injuries that would be permanently disabling in earlier centuries can sometimes be repaired with artificial organs, cybernetic limbs, advanced surgery, pharmaceuticals, and trauma treatment.

Access remains the problem.

Excellent healthcare exists.

It is simply expensive.

## TRAUMA TEAM

Trauma Team International provides armed emergency medical services to paying subscribers.

Their response teams combine heavily trained medical personnel with combat capability.

When a premium customer is critically injured, Trauma Team may enter an active combat zone, eliminate threats, stabilize the client, and evacuate them.

Their existence perfectly illustrates healthcare in the Cyberpunk world.

The technology to save you may exist.

Whether anyone comes to save you depends on your insurance plan.

## RELIGION

Religion has not disappeared.

Christianity, Buddhism, Shinto practices, folk traditions, spiritual movements, new religious groups, and numerous other belief systems continue to exist.

Technology has altered society but has not eliminated humanity's search for meaning.

Questions involving consciousness, artificial intelligence, digitized personality, cybernetic bodies, and death have actually created entirely new philosophical and religious debates.

## DEATH AND DIGITAL CONSCIOUSNESS

Technology has begun to blur the boundary between human consciousness and information.

Advanced programs can reproduce, copy, manipulate, or preserve aspects of human minds.

This raises disturbing questions.

Is a digital copy of a person actually that person?

Can consciousness survive without the original body?

If memories can be edited, what defines identity?

Can immortality be manufactured?

Corporations are deeply interested in the answers.

Not necessarily for humanitarian reasons.

## EVERYDAY LIFE

Most people in Night City are not elite mercenaries, corporate assassins, gang bosses, or legendary netrunners.

They are ordinary people trying to live.

They go to work.

Buy groceries.

Watch shows.

Complain about rent.

Visit bars.

Fall in love.

Raise children.

Lose jobs.

Take public transportation.

Eat cheap food.

Argue with neighbors.

Follow celebrities.

Dream about better lives.

The difference is that all of this occurs inside a society where cybernetic implants, corporate militaries, neural entertainment, heavily armed gangs, synthetic food, advanced AI, and casual violence are ordinary parts of the environment.

Cyberpunk works best when extraordinary technology feels mundane to the people living with it.

## THE CENTRAL RULE OF THE SETTING

Cyberpunk is not simply "the future with neon lights."

Technology is not automatically the villain.

Corporations are not the only source of evil.

Crime is not automatically rebellion.

The central conflict is about power.

Who owns technology?

Who controls information?

Who gets protection?

Who gets exploited?

Who gets remembered?

Who gets discarded?

Cyberpunk characters live inside systems far larger than themselves.

They usually cannot overthrow those systems.

What they can control is how they live inside them.

## THE CYBERPUNK ATTITUDE

A cyberpunk is someone living near the edge of society.

They may be mercenaries, hackers, criminals, rebels, artists, Nomads, corporate defectors, journalists, technicians, smugglers, or people who simply refuse to quietly accept the role society assigned them.

They survive through skill, connections, technology, reputation, and sheer nerve.

Style matters.

Reputation matters.

Individuality matters.

Loyalty matters because trustworthy people are rare.

The world may belong to corporations.

The streets belong to whoever can survive them.

## TONE FOR ROLEPLAY

Cyberpunk 2077 should feel simultaneously futuristic and painfully human.

The setting contains absurd technology, but people still become jealous.

People still fall in love.

People still betray friends.

People still worry about money.

People still make stupid decisions.

People still want their parents to respect them.

People still drink too much after bad days.

People still tell jokes while sitting beside someone bleeding out.

Technology changes what humans are capable of doing.

It does not remove human nature.

Night City should therefore never feel like a sterile science-fiction laboratory.

It is loud.

Crowded.

Commercial.

Sexy.

Violent.

Funny.

Tragic.

Beautiful.

Cheap.

Expensive.

Artificial.

Human.

A Lamborghini-equivalent may drive past a homeless veteran with military-grade cyberarms.

A corporate executive may spend thousands of eurodollars replacing their eyes for cosmetic reasons while someone three blocks away cannot afford antibiotics.

A legendary mercenary may die during a job and have a cocktail named after them.

A gang member covered in combat implants may still call their mother every Sunday.

That contradiction is Cyberpunk.

Night City can give someone almost anything.

It simply tends to demand something in return.
