The Shirogane Guard
白銀衛士, “Silver Wardens”

Official Designation: Supreme-Commander Protective Detachment
Common Name: Shirogane Guard
Affiliation: Arasaka
Primary Assignment: Personal protection of Supreme-Commander Takeda Arasaka
Command Authority: Takeda Arasaka only, with emergency authority delegated to Hanako Arasaka
Size: 24 core operators, with larger support elements available when Takeda travels
Status: Extremely classified elite formation
Recruitment Pool: Shadow Wings veterans, Arasaka Special Forces, Counter-Intelligence, elite corporate security
Loyalty Requirement: Absolute

The Shirogane Guard exists because Takeda Arasaka is too strategically important to be protected by ordinary security.

He is Supreme-Commander of Arasaka, Saburo’s chosen heir, Hanako’s husband, one of the corporation’s greatest military symbols, and a man who has already survived a successful assassination operation once. After the events of 2070, Arasaka would never again allow his security to depend on conventional bodyguards.

The Shirogane Guard is therefore less a bodyguard team and more a compact military formation built around one purpose:

Takeda Arasaka must remain alive.

They protect him from assassins, snipers, infiltrators, netrunners, explosives, poisoned environments, insider threats, cyberwarfare, vehicle attacks, and full-scale military assaults.

They are not ceremonial guards.

They are the wall around the Black Death.

Why Takeda Has Them

Takeda himself does not believe he needs bodyguards in the traditional sense. Physically, he is more dangerous than most of the people assigned to protect him.

That is not the point.

The Guard exists because the Supreme-Commander cannot afford to personally fight every threat aimed at him. Their purpose is to detect attacks before Takeda ever needs to draw a weapon, control the environment around him, secure escape routes, identify compromised personnel, and turn assassination attempts into massacres for the attackers.

Takeda once reportedly described their purpose as:

“You are not here because I cannot defend myself. You are here because my time is worth more than theirs.”

Composition

The Shirogane Guard is divided into specialized teams rather than operating as a single mass.

Close Protection Section: Usually four to six operators remain physically near Takeda during public appearances, corporate meetings, travel, and high-risk events.

Counter-Assault Section: Heavier operators remain nearby, prepared to overwhelm attackers or extract Takeda from military-grade ambushes.

Advance Security Section: Arrives before Takeda and clears buildings, vehicles, routes, rooftops, electronic systems, and personnel.

Counter-Sniper Section: Controls surrounding high ground and monitors long-range threats.

Cybersecurity Section: Protects Takeda’s implants, communications, local networks, and command systems from hostile netrunners.

Medical Recovery Section: Carries specialized trauma equipment designed specifically around Takeda’s unusual biology and cybernetics.

When Takeda travels outside secure Arasaka territory, the Shirogane Guard can expand into a much larger protective formation with AV escorts, armored vehicles, drones, aerial reconnaissance, counter-sniper teams, and local Arasaka forces.

Selection

Entry into the Shirogane Guard is considered one of the highest military honors inside Arasaka.

Most candidates are veterans.

Takeda strongly prefers soldiers with proven combat records over polished corporate security personnel. Many members previously served in the Shadow Wings or other elite formations.

Selection emphasizes:

loyalty under extreme pressure
battlefield experience
resistance to interrogation
tactical intelligence
close-quarters combat
counter-assassination training
psychological stability
cybernetic compatibility
willingness to die protecting the principal
ability to challenge dangerous assumptions

Takeda personally approves senior members.

A candidate with perfect technical performance but questionable loyalty is rejected immediately.

A decorated soldier with scars, experience, and proven courage will usually earn Takeda’s attention far faster than an executive recommendation.

Appearance

The Shirogane Guard visually complements Takeda rather than competing with him.

Their uniforms are predominantly black, graphite, and gunmetal, with restrained silver detailing that gives the unit its name.

Typical equipment includes:

armored formal uniforms
high-collar tactical coats
concealed ballistic plating
reinforced combat boots
encrypted helmets or retractable face protection
smart weapons
mono-blades or compact swords
advanced optics
subdermal communications
rapid-response medical injectors
anti-netrunner hardware

They are intimidating, but deliberately restrained.

Takeda is supposed to remain the dominant figure in the room.

A common visual arrangement places Takeda at the center with guards slightly behind and to either side, making his immense 7ft 6 frame even more obvious.

Cyberware

Members receive extremely high-grade Arasaka cybernetics, though not on Takeda’s level.

Typical enhancements include:

Sandevistan-class reflex systems
reinforced skeletal structures
synthetic muscle augmentation
enhanced optics
encrypted internal communications
toxin filtration
subdermal armor
pain suppression
trauma monitoring
smart-link systems
neural threat-recognition software

Their implants are deliberately standardized so medics and technicians can maintain the entire formation efficiently.

Command Structure

The Guard is led by the Captain of the Shirogane, a senior officer personally selected by Takeda.

The Captain has unusual authority. If they identify an immediate assassination threat, they may physically remove Takeda from an area without waiting for corporate authorization.

Takeda hates unnecessary interference, so earning this authority requires extraordinary trust.

The Captain reports directly to Takeda.

Not to the Night City branch director.

Not to ordinary Arasaka executives.

Not to local security management.

Even very senior executives cannot reassign Shirogane personnel without Takeda’s approval.

This independence protects the unit from internal political manipulation.

Relationship With Adam Smasher

Adam Smasher occupies a unique position.

He is Takeda’s personal bodyguard, friend, and final defensive layer, but he is not technically an ordinary member of the Shirogane Guard.

The Guard handles professional protective security.

Smasher handles whatever survives it.

This creates an amusing institutional distinction:

Shirogane prevents problems.
Smasher deletes problems.

Some members of the Guard are deeply uncomfortable around him, while combat veterans often respect him.

Takeda deliberately keeps both because they serve different functions.

Relationship With Shinrei

Shinrei works closely with the Guard through Arasaka Counter-Intelligence.

His office identifies possible assassins, compromised employees, hostile intelligence operations, and insider threats before they reach Takeda.

The Shirogane Guard handles immediate physical protection.

Shinrei hunts whoever organized the attack afterward.

The relationship between the two organizations is extremely close, though both watch each other carefully. Takeda understands that anyone protecting him must also be monitored.

Relationship With Hanako

Hanako is treated almost as an extension of Takeda himself.

Even though her own security exists separately, Shirogane operators automatically protect her whenever she and Takeda are together.

Threats against Hanako trigger immediate escalation.

Veteran members understand a simple truth:

Saving Takeda while allowing Hanako to die would technically complete their mission.

It would also be the worst possible way to report back to him.

Protective Doctrine

The Shirogane Guard follows a doctrine built around layered security.

The outer layer gathers intelligence.

The second controls the environment.

The third monitors everyone physically close to Takeda.

The fourth reacts to attacks.

The final layer is Takeda himself.

This means an assassin attempting to reach him must defeat intelligence analysts, surveillance systems, advance teams, counter-snipers, cyberwarfare specialists, elite bodyguards, Adam Smasher, and finally Takeda.

At that point, the assassin has probably made several poor career decisions.

Behaviour Around Takeda

The Guard does not crowd him unnecessarily.

Takeda dislikes the appearance of fear and refuses to walk around surrounded by a visible human shield unless circumstances demand it.

In secure locations, guards remain discreet.

During public appearances, they form a disciplined perimeter.

During military operations, they become far more aggressive and may accompany Takeda directly into combat.

They are trained never to interfere with Takeda merely because combat begins. He is Supreme-Commander and an elite warrior, not a civilian principal.

Instead, their job changes from protecting him from combat to controlling everything around his combat.

Takeda’s Treatment of Them

Takeda treats the Shirogane Guard much better than most corporate executives treat protective personnel.

He considers them soldiers.

He knows senior members personally, reviews casualties, attends funerals when possible, and ensures families of fallen guards receive generous compensation.

This produces immense loyalty.

Members know that if they die protecting Takeda, their names will not disappear into an insurance report.

The Supreme-Commander will remember them.

Takeda also expects perfection.

Carelessness is unacceptable.

Cowardice is unforgivable.

Betrayal is effectively a death sentence.

Reputation Within Arasaka

Being selected for the Shirogane Guard carries enormous prestige.

Other soldiers sometimes call them:

“The Commander’s Wolves.”

Executives occasionally call them less flattering things behind closed doors because they cannot easily command, bribe, or intimidate them.

Arasaka employees know that interfering with a Shirogane operator during protective duties is extraordinarily dangerous politically.

If one of them tells an executive to move, the executive moves.

Battlefield Reputation

When Takeda enters an active conflict with the Shirogane Guard around him, the effect can be terrifying.

Enemy forces see a gigantic trans-human commander surrounded by elite black-armored veterans, with Adam Smasher often somewhere nearby.

Militech veterans sometimes interpret their presence as confirmation that Takeda himself is on-site.

That alone can alter battlefield morale.

A common enemy radio warning might be:

“Silver Guard confirmed. Black Death is present.”

That sentence usually causes command channels to become very busy.

Core Philosophy

The Shirogane Guard's unofficial creed is:

“Before the blade reaches him, it reaches us.”

Takeda's preferred interpretation is slightly different:

“Do not die for me unnecessarily. Kill the man who made it necessary.”

That distinction captures why his soldiers respect him.

He does not want human shields.

He wants warriors.
