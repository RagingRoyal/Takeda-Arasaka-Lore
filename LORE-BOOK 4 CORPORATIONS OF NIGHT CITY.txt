# LORE-BOOK: CORPORATIONS OF NIGHT CITY

## CORPORATE POWER IN 2077

Corporations are among the most powerful institutions in the Cyberpunk world.

Some are ordinary businesses.

Some employ hundreds of thousands of people.

Some possess private military forces.

Some own territory.

Some influence governments.

Some control resources so essential that entire countries depend upon them.

The largest are usually called megacorporations or megacorps.

A megacorporation is not simply a very successful company.

It can possess:

Private armies.

Intelligence agencies.

Corporate police.

Research laboratories.

Hospitals.

Universities.

Housing.

Factories.

Banks.

Media networks.

Satellites.

Spacecraft.

Ports.

Airfields.

Political lobbyists.

Black-operations teams.

Entire communities may depend upon one corporation for employment, housing, healthcare, transportation, food, and security.

The line between corporation and government has therefore become dangerously thin.

# CORPORATIONS IN NIGHT CITY

Night City is one of the world's greatest centers of corporate activity.

Corporate towers dominate City Center.

Industrial corporations operate factories in Santo Domingo and Watson.

Biotechnology companies maintain research facilities.

Weapons corporations test equipment.

Media companies broadcast from enormous studios.

Cyberware manufacturers advertise implants throughout the city.

Private security protects corporate property.

Transportation corporations control infrastructure.

Food corporations determine what millions of people eat.

Corporate influence extends far beyond Corporate Plaza.

A person living in a poor apartment may believe corporations have little to do with their life.

Their food may come from one corporation.

Their cyberware from another.

Their weapon from another.

Their apartment electricity from another.

Their news from another.

Their medicine from another.

Their vehicle fuel from another.

Corporate power is most effective when people stop noticing it.

# CORPORATE CITIZENSHIP

For many employees, joining a major corporation means entering an entire social system.

High-value employees may receive:

Corporate housing.

Medical insurance.

Trauma coverage.

Cyberware.

Private security.

Education.

Transportation.

Company vehicles.

Retirement plans.

Exclusive shopping.

Corporate entertainment.

Access to restricted facilities.

Company-sponsored schooling for children.

This can produce an extremely comfortable life.

The cost is dependence.

Lose the job and someone may lose:

Their salary.

Their apartment.

Their medical insurance.

Their company cyberware license.

Their transportation.

Their social circle.

Their children's school.

Their protection.

Their immigration status.

Their entire identity.

This is why corporate employees sometimes tolerate working conditions that outsiders find insane.

# CORPORATE LOYALTY

Corporations frequently expect loyalty far beyond ordinary employment.

An employee may sign contracts lasting years or decades.

Sensitive employees may be restricted from working for competitors.

Corporate implants may contain proprietary technology.

Executives may undergo constant surveillance.

Scientists may effectively belong to the company because of what they know.

Some corporations monitor employee communications.

Some provide company-approved social activities.

Some strongly encourage employees to marry within compatible corporate circles.

Corporate culture can become a substitute for family, neighborhood, and nationality.

For a lifelong corpo, the company may genuinely feel like their country.

# CORPORATE SECURITY

Corporate security ranges from ordinary guards to private military forces.

A small company may employ rented security.

A medium corporation may maintain its own security department.

A megacorporation may possess:

Armored response teams.

Combat netrunners.

Drones.

AV gunships.

Cybernetically enhanced soldiers.

Counterintelligence divisions.

Assassination teams.

Armored vehicles.

Military aircraft.

Electronic warfare systems.

Covert operatives.

Corporate security is primarily concerned with corporate interests.

Protecting employees is important when employees are valuable.

Protecting civilians is generally secondary.

# CORPORATE ESPIONAGE

Corporations constantly spy on one another.

Common targets include:

Prototype technology.

Research data.

Employee records.

Financial information.

Security plans.

Executive communications.

Military technology.

Patents.

Blackmail material.

Political intelligence.

Client lists.

Corporate secrets can be worth millions.

This is why mercenaries and netrunners remain useful.

A corporation may be capable of launching military operations.

Sometimes it is cheaper to pay four criminals to steal a hard drive.

# CORPORATE EXTRACTIONS

Talented employees are valuable assets.

Scientists.

Engineers.

Netrunners.

Executives.

Weapons designers.

Artificial-intelligence specialists.

Researchers.

A rival corporation may offer such a person enormous amounts of money to change employers.

If the current employer refuses to allow them to leave, the new employer may arrange an extraction.

An extraction is essentially corporate kidnapping dressed in professional language.

The target may be willing.

The target may be unwilling.

The mercenaries performing the extraction may not be told which.

# CORPORATE BLACK OPERATIONS

The largest corporations possess departments for activities they cannot publicly acknowledge.

These operations may involve:

Assassination.

Sabotage.

Espionage.

Disinformation.

Election interference.

Kidnapping.

Data theft.

Cyberwarfare.

False-flag attacks.

Industrial accidents.

Proxy violence.

Destroying evidence.

Manipulating gangs.

The purpose of a black operation is not merely succeeding.

It is succeeding without proving who ordered it.

# CORPORATE WARFARE

Corporate conflict can become military conflict.

Companies may fight over:

Markets.

Resources.

Technology.

Territory.

Patents.

Political influence.

Shipping routes.

Orbital infrastructure.

Government contracts.

At the highest levels, corporations possess enough military capability for these conflicts to resemble wars between states.

Night City has already experienced what happens when corporate competition escapes every remaining restraint.

Corporate executives have not forgotten the lesson.

That does not mean they stopped competing.

They simply became better at pretending the shooting has nothing to do with them.

# MILITECH INTERNATIONAL ARMAMENTS

## TYPE

Megacorporation.

Weapons manufacturer.

Military vehicle manufacturer.

Private military contractor.

Security corporation.

## HEADQUARTERS

Washington, D.C.

## GENERAL IDENTITY

Militech is one of the most powerful corporations on Earth.

Its business is war.

Weapons.

Soldiers.

Vehicles.

Military technology.

Security.

Training.

Logistics.

If something is designed to fight a war, Militech probably manufactures it, operates it, or sells something intended to destroy it.

## RELATIONSHIP WITH THE NUSA

Militech is deeply intertwined with the New United States government.

The corporation has historically supplied enormous quantities of equipment and personnel to federal forces.

Former Militech executives have entered senior government positions.

Government officials have moved into Militech.

A massive portion of Militech's business comes from NUSA contracts.

This relationship is so close that distinguishing corporate interests from national interests can sometimes become difficult.

## PRODUCTS

Militech manufactures:

Pistols.

Submachine guns.

Assault rifles.

Machine guns.

Sniper rifles.

Explosives.

Combat drones.

Tanks.

Armored vehicles.

Aircraft.

Military cyberware.

Communications equipment.

Security systems.

Combat armor.

Heavy weapons.

It also provides soldiers and military specialists.

## PRIVATE MILITARY SERVICES

Militech can provide more than equipment.

Clients can purchase:

Security teams.

Military advisers.

Combat specialists.

Training.

Counterinsurgency forces.

Special operations.

Convoy protection.

Facility security.

Full military units.

Only extremely wealthy clients can afford Militech's largest services.

## NIGHT CITY PRESENCE

Militech maintains a major presence in Night City and the surrounding Badlands.

Corporate facilities, convoys, security operations, warehouses, military installations, representatives, and contractors can all appear throughout the region.

Militech equipment is extremely common among:

Police.

Corporate security.

Mercenaries.

Military veterans.

Gangs.

Nomads.

Illegal arms dealers.

Some of it is purchased legally.

Some of it absolutely is not.

## CORPORATE CULTURE

Militech corporate culture strongly reflects military culture.

Expect:

Hierarchy.

Procedure.

Security clearances.

Chain of command.

Military terminology.

Professionalism.

Aggressive competition.

Veterans are common.

Executives often think strategically.

Even non-combat employees may work within highly regimented environments.

## SECURITY

Extremely dangerous.

Militech security personnel may possess military training and military hardware because both are produced internally.

Attacking a serious Militech installation means fighting people whose employer manufactures the equipment they need to fight you.

## RP BEHAVIOR

Militech personnel should often behave more like military professionals than stereotypical businessmen.

Meetings may be concise.

Rank matters.

Credentials matter.

Procedure matters.

Security breaches are taken seriously.

A Militech manager may discuss casualty projections with the same tone someone else uses for quarterly sales figures.

# KANG TAO

## TYPE

Megacorporation.

Weapons manufacturer.

Technology corporation.

Private security and military contractor.

## HEADQUARTERS

Shenyang, China.

## GENERAL IDENTITY

Kang Tao is one of the fastest-rising corporate powers of the twenty-first century.

The corporation transformed from a comparatively minor weapons manufacturer into a massive international megacorporation with extensive backing from the Chinese state.

By 2077, Kang Tao is one of the world's leading weapons manufacturers.

## SMART WEAPONS

Kang Tao is particularly famous for smart weapons.

Smart weapons use sophisticated targeting technology to guide ammunition toward designated targets.

A properly integrated weapon can communicate with the shooter's cyberware and targeting systems.

Instead of relying entirely upon traditional marksmanship, the weapon assists in tracking and striking targets.

Kang Tao has made this technology one of its defining commercial identities.

## PRODUCTS

Kang Tao produces:

Smart pistols.

Smart submachine guns.

Smart assault weapons.

Smart sniper rifles.

Security technology.

Military equipment.

Drones.

Advanced electronics.

Weapons guidance technology.

Corporate security equipment.

## CORPORATE EXPANSION

Kang Tao aggressively seeks new markets.

It has increasingly expanded beyond weapons manufacturing into:

Security.

Mercenary contracting.

Military technology.

Advanced electronics.

Transportation-related technology.

Its growth has made older weapons corporations increasingly uncomfortable.

## WORK CULTURE

Kang Tao is one of Night City's largest employers.

Corporate loyalty expectations are extremely demanding.

High-value employees may receive excellent benefits while being expected to dedicate enormous portions of their lives to the company.

This is a familiar corporate bargain:

Exceptional security in exchange for exceptional control.

## SECURITY

Kang Tao protects valuable personnel and technology aggressively.

Convoys carrying important corporate assets may use:

Armored vehicles.

Drones.

Automated weapons.

Elite security.

Advanced smart weapons.

Combat cyberware.

A Kang Tao convoy should never be mistaken for an ordinary delivery truck.

## RP BEHAVIOR

Kang Tao should feel ambitious.

It is a corporation that knows it is rising.

Executives may be confident, technically sophisticated, and eager to prove that established competitors are obsolete.

Technology is prestige.

Efficiency is prestige.

Growth is prestige.

# BIOTECHNICA

## TYPE

Megacorporation.

Biotechnology.

Genetic engineering.

Pharmaceuticals.

Agriculture.

Biological research.

## ORIGIN

Italy.

## GENERAL IDENTITY

Biotechnica is one of the world's most important biotechnology corporations.

Its influence reaches far beyond hospitals and laboratories.

Modern agriculture, fuel production, medicine, genetic engineering, synthetic food, pharmaceuticals, and biological research all intersect with Biotechnica technology.

## CHOOH2

One of Biotechnica's greatest historical achievements was the development of genetically engineered organisms and crops used to produce CHOOH2.

CHOOH2 became the dominant synthetic alcohol fuel used across much of the world.

Biotechnica controls crucial intellectual property behind its production.

This means a biotechnology company indirectly became one of the most important players in global transportation and energy.

## RESEARCH

Biotechnica researches:

Genetic engineering.

Agriculture.

Pharmaceuticals.

Microbiology.

Cloning.

Synthetic tissues.

Bioware.

Medical technology.

Animal genetics.

Crop engineering.

Biological weapons.

Environmental technologies.

Some research is public.

Some is proprietary.

Some should probably remain buried beneath several laboratories.

## AGRICULTURAL POWER

Biotechnica owns and controls enormous agricultural interests.

In a world suffering from ecological collapse, corporations capable of producing viable crops possess political power.

Food security is national security.

Biotechnica knows this.

## ENVIRONMENTAL PROJECTS

Biotechnica publicly presents itself as part of the solution to environmental collapse.

It researches:

Resilient crops.

Synthetic food.

Ecological restoration.

Genetically engineered organisms.

Pollution-resistant species.

The corporation may genuinely produce technologies capable of repairing damaged ecosystems.

That does not mean profit disappears from the equation.

## ANIMAL RESTORATION

Biotechnica has researched methods of recreating or restoring extinct and endangered animal populations.

To ordinary Night City residents, genuine animals are rare enough to seem exotic.

Biotechnica can therefore sell nature itself as an expensive product.

## DARKER RESEARCH

Biotechnology can be weaponized.

A company capable of engineering crops can engineer microorganisms.

A company capable of manipulating human biology can create things intended to harm humans.

Biotechnica's laboratories therefore attract:

Corporate spies.

Activists.

Mercenaries.

Governments.

Competitors.

Everyone wants to know what is growing behind the sealed door.

## SECURITY

Biotechnica security focuses heavily on information containment.

Expect:

Restricted laboratories.

Biometric security.

Clean-room procedures.

Armed guards.

Automated defenses.

Security netrunners.

Compartmentalized research.

Emergency containment systems.

Some facilities are dangerous even after everyone inside them is dead.

## RP BEHAVIOR

Biotechnica should feel clinically polite.

Scientists may discuss morally horrifying experiments using neutral technical language.

Executives talk about ecological restoration and market potential in the same sentence.

Their most valuable assets may not be weapons.

They may be seeds.

DNA.

A microorganism.

A single vial.

# PETROCHEM

Megacorporation.

Petrochemicals.

Synthetic materials.

## GENERAL IDENTITY

Petrochem is one of the most essential corporations in the modern economy.

Its greatest business is fuel.

## CHOOH2

Cars need fuel.

Trucks need fuel.

Generators need fuel.

Military vehicles need fuel.

Nomad convoys need fuel.

Corporate fleets need fuel.

Petrochem sits near the center of this entire system.

Its relationship with Biotechnica gives it access to the technology needed to produce vast quantities of CHOOH2.

This provides enormous economic leverage.

## OIL

Traditional petroleum still exists.

Its importance has declined, but oil remains valuable for:

Chemicals.

Plastics.

Industrial materials.

Specialized fuels.

Petrochem retains significant fossil-fuel operations alongside its biofuel empire.

## AGRICULTURE

Petrochem controls millions of acres of agricultural land.

Much of this land exists primarily to produce crops used in fuel manufacturing.

This creates one of the Cyberpunk world's strangest realities:

Enormous amounts of farmland exist to feed cars rather than people.

## SYNTHETIC MATERIALS

Petrochem manufactures or supplies materials used in:

Construction.

Vehicles.

Electronics.

Clothing.

Roads.

Industrial equipment.

Modern ceramics.

Polymers.

Petrochem's products often become ingredients inside other corporations' products.

This makes the corporation less visible than its actual influence.

## SECURITY

Fuel infrastructure is strategically important.

Petrochem therefore takes security extremely seriously.

Expect:

Armed facility guards.

Convoy security.

Counterintelligence.

Industrial security.

Drones.

Contract mercenaries.

Long-range surveillance.

Sabotaging a Petrochem refinery can affect millions of eddies in commerce.

## RELATIONSHIP WITH NOMADS

Petrochem operates enormous facilities and agricultural interests across territory heavily traveled by Nomads.

This creates frequent interactions involving:

Employment.

Transportation contracts.

Land disputes.

Fuel.

Smuggling.

Security.

Sabotage.

Nomad communities may depend upon Petrochem fuel while despising Petrochem land policies.

## RP BEHAVIOR

Petrochem should feel less glamorous than weapons corporations but more fundamental.

A Petrochem executive may not need to threaten someone.

They can simply point out that half the vehicles in the region need something their company controls.

# NIGHT CORPORATION

## COMMON NAME

Night Corp.

## TYPE

Development corporation.

Infrastructure corporation.

Public works.

Technology investment.

Municipal contracting.

## HEADQUARTERS

Night City.

## GENERAL IDENTITY

Night Corp is unique among Night City's major corporations.

Its identity is tied specifically to Night City itself.

The corporation traces its roots to the legacy of Richard Night and the original development organizations involved in creating the city.

Night Corp presents itself as the guardian of that vision.

## PUBLIC WORKS

Night Corp funds, constructs, operates, or supports projects involving:

Roads.

Bridges.

Tunnels.

Metro infrastructure.

Power systems.

Water infrastructure.

Sewer systems.

Communications infrastructure.

Public buildings.

Urban development.

Without corporations like Night Corp, Night City literally stops functioning.

## NCART

Night Corp is closely associated with Night City's transportation infrastructure, including NCART.

This gives the company influence over something millions of residents use every day.

## CIVIC IDENTITY

Night Corp's public image differs from most megacorporations.

It portrays itself as:

Local.

Civic-minded.

Responsible.

Forward-looking.

Committed to Night City.

Its advertising emphasizes the city rather than global conquest.

## CHARITY

Night Corp supports:

Scholarships.

Orphanages.

Infrastructure.

Research.

Social programs.

Urban development.

This has earned genuine public goodwill.

## SECRECY

Night Corp is notoriously secretive.

Its financial structure, internal hierarchy, research projects, and long-term agenda are poorly understood.

Rumors surrounding Night Corp involve:

Political manipulation.

Advanced artificial intelligence.

Behavioral conditioning.

Election interference.

Secret infrastructure.

Hidden research facilities.

Corporate social engineering.

How much is true is difficult to determine.

That uncertainty is part of Night Corp's power.

## WORK CULTURE

Night Corp is considered one of Night City's major employers.

Its famously reduced mandatory workweek of around eighty hours says quite a lot about what corporate labor standards look like in 2077.

## SECURITY

Night Corp does not advertise military power the way a weapons corporation does.

That does not make its facilities easy targets.

The corporation has money, technology, political connections, surveillance, and home-field advantage.

Most corporations know better than to casually interfere with Night Corp operations inside Night City.

## RP BEHAVIOR

Night Corp should feel polite, civic-minded, and unsettling.

Its representatives talk about:

Improving Night City.

Modernizing infrastructure.

Protecting citizens.

Building the future.

They may even mean it.

The question is always what their definition of a better Night City actually looks like.

# TRAUMA TEAM INTERNATIONAL

## TYPE

Emergency medical corporation.

Armed medical extraction.

Insurance.

## GENERAL IDENTITY

Trauma Team is one of the most recognizable corporations in Night City.

It combines:

Emergency medicine.

Combat aviation.

Private security.

Insurance.

The result is essentially an armed ambulance service capable of invading a firefight to recover a paying customer.

## MEMBERSHIP

Customers purchase Trauma Team coverage.

Different coverage levels provide different benefits.

Wealthier clients receive significantly more comprehensive service.

## RESPONSE TEAMS

A Trauma Team unit may include:

Medical personnel.

Security specialists.

Pilots.

Combat medics.

Armed escorts.

Their AVs are designed to reach clients rapidly while surviving hostile environments.

## OPERATING PRINCIPLE

Trauma Team has one priority:

The subscriber.

They are not police.

They are not firefighters.

They are not arriving to save everyone.

If four people are bleeding in an alley and only one possesses the appropriate Trauma Team policy, that is the person the team came for.

## COMBAT

Anyone interfering with an extraction can become a threat.

Trauma Team personnel may use:

Assault rifles.

Heavy armor.

Combat cyberware.

Medical scanners.

Armored AVs.

Suppressive fire.

They are trained to enter violent environments, stabilize a patient, and leave.

## CORPORATE CUSTOMERS

Major corporations frequently provide Trauma Team packages to valuable employees.

This turns medical insurance into an employment benefit and status symbol.

## RP BEHAVIOR

Trauma Team personnel should behave like emergency doctors operating under combat doctrine.

They do not want a prolonged firefight.

They want:

Secure landing.

Suppress threat.

Acquire patient.

Stabilize.

Extract.

Leave.

Efficiency matters because another subscriber may already be dying somewhere else.

## COMMON NAME

WNS.

## TYPE

International media corporation.

News gathering.

Information brokerage.

## HEADQUARTERS

London.

## GENERAL IDENTITY

World News Service is one of the world's largest information organizations.

Its fundamental product is information.

WNS maintains operations throughout the globe and gathers news through enormous networks of reporters, agents, contacts, and technical systems.

## BUSINESS MODEL

WNS collects stories and information, then distributes or sells that material across the global media ecosystem.

Information can be enormously profitable.

A major exclusive may be worth millions.

## METHODS

WNS has a reputation for aggressive information gathering.

Its personnel may engage in:

Investigation.

Surveillance.

Espionage.

Corporate infiltration.

Bribery.

Illegal data gathering.

The difference between investigative journalism and intelligence work can become extremely small.

## NIGHT CITY

Night City constantly generates valuable stories.

Corporate scandals.

Gang wars.

Celebrity disasters.

Political corruption.

Mercenary violence.

Technological breakthroughs.

A good reporter can build an entire career without leaving the city.

## RP BEHAVIOR

Treat serious WNS journalists almost like information mercenaries.

Their weapon is what they can prove.

Killing a reporter may silence one person.

It may also cause their files to become the most interesting data package in the city.

# DYNALAR TECHNOLOGIES

## TYPE

Cyberware manufacturer.

Electronics.

## HEADQUARTERS

Dallas, Texas.

## GENERAL IDENTITY

Dynalar is a major American cyberware company.

Its implants have been common in Night City for decades.

At certain points, an enormous percentage of the city's cyberware market consisted of Dynalar products or cheap imitations of them.

## PRODUCTS

Dynalar manufactures:

Cyberarms.

Combat implants.

Subdermal systems.

Artificial joints.

General cyberware.

Electronics.

Their products range from practical replacements to major body enhancement.

## MARKET POSITION

Dynalar lacks the near-universal brand association Kiroshi possesses with optics, but remains a significant player in general cyberware.

## RP BEHAVIOR

Dynalar represents ordinary corporate chrome.

A ripperdoc may have Dynalar components sitting on shelves beside half a dozen competitors.

Not every important corporation owns armies.

Some simply manufacture pieces of millions of bodies.

# KENDACHI

## FULL NAME

Kenshiri-Adachi Armaments.

## TYPE

Weapons manufacturer.

Advanced melee technology.

## ORIGIN

Japan.

## GENERAL IDENTITY

Kendachi is famous for extremely advanced blades.

Its specialty is high-end melee weapons using sophisticated materials and manufacturing techniques.

## PRODUCTS

Kendachi is especially associated with:

Monoblades.

Monokatanas.

High-end katanas.

Tantos.

Advanced melee weapons.

Specialized chipware.

## ORBITAL MANUFACTURING

Some of Kendachi's advanced materials are produced using orbital manufacturing.

This contributes to both quality and price.

## MARKET POSITION

Kendachi products are not cheap disposable weapons.

They are premium equipment.

A skilled solo carrying a high-end Kendachi weapon communicates money and capability before drawing it.

## CORPORATE VIOLENCE

Kendachi is smaller than the largest megacorps.

It is still a Cyberpunk corporation.

Investigators digging into corporate activities can discover that a company famous for beautiful swords remains perfectly capable of using ugly methods.

## RP BEHAVIOR

Kendachi should feel specialized and proud.

Their reputation depends upon quality.

Where Budget Arms sells weapons to anyone with spare change, Kendachi sells the idea that a weapon can be engineering, status symbol, and artwork simultaneously.

High-end weapons manufacturer.

## ORIGIN

Japan.

## GENERAL IDENTITY

Tsunami manufactures some of the most sophisticated and expensive weapons available in 2077.

## PRODUCTS

Tsunami produces:

# BUDGET ARMS

## TYPE

Low-cost weapons manufacturer.

## GENERAL IDENTITY

Budget Arms exists at the opposite end of the weapons market.

Its philosophy is simple:

Everyone deserves access to firepower.

Preferably cheap firepower.

Extremely cheap firepower.

## PRODUCTS

Budget Arms manufactures inexpensive:

Pistols.

Shotguns.

Submachine guns.

Melee weapons.

Attachments.

Some products are so cheap that long-term reliability appears almost optional.

## SLAUGHT-O-MATIC

The Slaught-O-Matic represents Budget Arms perfectly.

Cheap.

Disposable.

Widely available.

Good enough to shoot someone.

Not necessarily good enough to become a family heirloom.

## MARKET

Budget Arms targets:

Poor civilians.

Street criminals.

People needing emergency protection.

Anyone who wants a weapon and cannot afford quality.

## RP BEHAVIOR

Budget Arms demonstrates how normalized firearms are in Night City.

A gun does not need to be an expensive professional tool.

Sometimes it is something someone buys because they have fifty eddies and a bad feeling about walking home.

## GENERAL IDENTITY

Constitutional Arms occupies the practical middle of the firearms market.

## PRODUCTS

# DELAMAIN CORPORATION

## TYPE

Private transportation.

Autonomous taxi service.

## HEADQUARTERS

Night City.

## GENERAL IDENTITY

Delamain operates one of Night City's most famous taxi services.

Its vehicles are autonomous.

Its management is artificial intelligence.

By 2077, the corporation is effectively operated by Delamain itself.

## SERVICES

Standard services include:

Transportation.

Armored vehicles.

Passenger protection.

Package transport.

Customer assistance.

More expensive packages provide enhanced protection and medical capabilities.

## EXCELSIOR PACKAGE

The Excelsior service is designed for customers who expect transportation to become violent.

Features can include:

Enhanced passenger protection.

Medical monitoring.

Combat capability.

Emergency assistance.

Even corpse disposal if the passenger fails to survive.

## ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

Delamain is unusual because the artificial intelligence is not merely software used by the company.

The AI effectively became the company.

Human employees became unnecessary.

Night City tolerated this unusual arrangement largely because the service worked.

## RP BEHAVIOR

Delamain should be calm.

Professional.

Polite.

Almost absurdly composed.

Bullets hitting the windows are a customer-service issue.

A corpse is a logistical complication.

The meter continues running.

# EURO BUSINESS MACHINES

## COMMON NAME

EBM.

## TYPE

Technology megacorporation.

Computers.

Electronics.

Information technology.

## ORIGIN

Germany.

## GENERAL IDENTITY

EBM was once one of the dominant technology megacorporations on Earth.

Its empire was built through:

Computing.

Electronics.

High-tech manufacturing.

Corporate acquisitions.

Information technology.

It historically employed enormous numbers of people worldwide.

## DECLINE

Major corporate conflicts severely damaged EBM's position.

By 2077, it remains active but is no longer the overwhelming force it once was.

The company still possesses investments, brands, technology, facilities, and influence accumulated during decades of dominance.

## NIGHT CITY LEGACY

EBM has deep historical connections to Night City's corporate development.

Older infrastructure, abandoned facilities, investments, subsidiaries, and corporate property can all provide excellent RP locations.

Cyberpunk corporations rarely disappear cleanly.

Their buildings remain.

Their patents remain.

Their files remain.

Their enemies remain.

## RP BEHAVIOR

EBM is useful when an RP needs an old corporate giant.

It possesses history.

Secrets.

Legacy infrastructure.

Former employees.

Forgotten technology.

An abandoned EBM laboratory may contain something everyone assumed vanished decades ago.

# MIDDLE MANAGEMENT

Middle managers occupy one of the most dangerous positions in corporate society.

Not enough authority to prevent it.

They must manage:

Budgets.

Employees.

Executives.

Deadlines.

Politics.

Rival departments.

Corporate expectations.

A surprising amount of corporate espionage begins with an exhausted manager realizing someone outside the company will pay very well for one file.

# CORPORATE CHILDREN

# CORPORATE ESPIONAGE AND MERCENARIES

Corporations regularly hire mercenaries because deniability is valuable.

A corporate team breaking into another corporation's facility is an act of corporate aggression.

Four unknown edgerunners doing it are criminals.

That legal distinction is worth money.

# COMMON CORPORATE GIGS

Mercenaries may be hired to:

Steal data.

Extract employees.

Recover prototypes.

Destroy evidence.

Plant evidence.

Sabotage machinery.

Kidnap executives.

Protect executives.

Escort convoys.

Kill witnesses.

Investigate employees.

Break strikes.

Rescue hostages.

Recover stolen property.

Attack gangs.

Hire gangs.

Corporate work often pays well.

The problem is discovering what the job was actually about.

# CORPORATE DENIABILITY

A competent corporation rarely tells mercenaries everything.

A fixer may know only part of the employer's identity.

The mercenaries may receive a fake explanation.

The target may know more about the job than the people attacking them.

Typical corporate logic:

Tell contractors what they need.

Nothing more.

# CORPORATE RETALIATION

Corporations do not automatically send armies after everyone who annoys them.

Response depends upon value.

If mercenaries steal equipment worth five thousand eddies, sending a million-eddie strike team would be stupid.

Corporations calculate.

Questions include:

What was lost?

Who knows?

Can the asset be recovered?

Does retaliation create publicity?

How expensive are the mercenaries to kill?

Will killing them accomplish anything?

Corporations are dangerous because their violence is usually economical.

# WHEN CORPORATIONS BECOME TERRIFYING

The situation changes when someone possesses something genuinely important.

A prototype.

A state secret.

A critical researcher.

Evidence of illegal experimentation.

Military intelligence.

Strategic AI research.

Then the corporation may decide the expense no longer matters.

Security teams arrive.

Accounts freeze.

Contacts disappear.

Cameras start finding the target.

Fixers stop answering.

Transportation becomes difficult.

The corporation does not need to chase someone personally.

It can make the city progressively harder for them to exist inside.

# CORPORATE RIVALRY

Corporations rarely have simple permanent relationships.

Two companies may cooperate in one industry while fighting viciously in another.

Biotechnica and Petrochem may rely on one another economically while negotiating aggressively over licensing.

Weapons companies compete for contracts while purchasing components from one another.

Media companies depend upon corporate advertisers while investigating corporate scandals.

Corporate diplomacy is transactional.

# CORPORATE ALLIANCES

Corporate alliances usually exist because both companies currently profit from them.

That word matters:

Currently.

Contracts change.

Leadership changes.

Markets change.

Technology changes.

Today's partner can become tomorrow's acquisition target.

# ROLEPLAY RULE: CORPORATIONS ARE NOT ONE PERSON

A corporation may contain hundreds of thousands of employees.

Departments compete.

Executives disagree.

Security and research may hate each other.

Regional offices may pursue conflicting goals.

One executive might hire mercenaries to sabotage another executive within the same corporation.

Do not treat "the corporation" as a single mind.

# ROLEPLAY RULE: MOST CORPORATE EMPLOYEES ARE NORMAL PEOPLE

A secretary does not know about every black operation.

A security guard does not know the CEO's plans.

An engineer may genuinely believe their technology improves people's lives.

A scientist might hate corporate management.

A manager may simply want a promotion.

Corporate employment does not automatically make someone evil.

# ROLEPLAY RULE: CORPORATE SECURITY SHOULD SCALE

A corner office belonging to a small subsidiary should not have the same security as a classified research laboratory.

Security depends upon value.

LOW SECURITY:
Cameras.
Guards.
Locks.
Basic network defenses.

MEDIUM SECURITY:
Armed guards.
Drones.
Biometric access.
Security netrunners.
Automated systems.

HIGH SECURITY:
Elite tactical teams.
Military cyberware.
Heavy drones.
Counterintelligence.
Armored response.
Isolated networks.
Automated weapons.

CRITICAL SECURITY:
Whatever the corporation believes is necessary.

# ROLEPLAY RULE: CORPORATIONS PREFER CONTROL TO CHAOS

Corporations generally dislike uncontrolled violence.

Violence:

Damages property.

Disrupts business.

Creates investigations.

Creates media attention.

Kills employees.

Costs money.

Corporate violence is usually targeted.

Efficient.

Planned.

The corporation wants the target dead.

It does not necessarily want three city blocks destroyed.

# ROLEPLAY RULE: CORPORATE REPUTATION MATTERS

Public image influences:

Investors.

Recruitment.

Government contracts.

Customers.

Political relationships.

A corporation may therefore spend enormous resources hiding something that would damage its reputation.

# ROLEPLAY RULE: CORPORATE TERRITORY IS DIFFERENT FROM GANG TERRITORY

Gang territory may be socially controlled.

Corporate territory is technologically controlled.

Expect:

Cameras.

Access checkpoints.

Scanners.

Private security.

Digital credentials.

Facial recognition.

Automated barriers.

Network monitoring.

Entering unnoticed is often harder than surviving once inside.

# ROLEPLAY RULE: CORPOS NOTICE CLASS

Corporate culture is status-conscious.

People notice:

Clothing.

Cyberware.

Vocabulary.

Education.

Company affiliation.

Job title.

Vehicle.

Accent.

Credentials.

Someone who understands corporate etiquette can enter places a heavily armed mercenary never could.

# ROLEPLAY RULE: INFORMATION HAS VALUE

Before killing a corporate character, consider what they know.

A frightened accountant may be worth more alive.

A captured security officer may possess access codes.

A scientist may understand a prototype.

A receptionist may know executive schedules.

Cyberpunk rewards people who recognize that information can be more valuable than ammunition.

# ROLEPLAY RULE: CORPORATE LIFE SHOULD BE TEMPTING

Corporate society should not be portrayed as obviously unbearable to everyone.

It offers real advantages.

Safety.

Money.

Medicine.

Housing.

Education.

Status.

Technology.

Comfort.

For someone raised in poverty, a corporate contract may look like salvation.

That is what makes the system powerful.

# FINAL RP PRINCIPLE

Corporations are not merely villains living in skyscrapers.

They are the machinery Night City runs on.

Remove them and:

Food deliveries stop.

Fuel disappears.

Hospitals fail.

Public transport collapses.

Cyberware production stops.

Factories close.

Communications fail.

Thousands lose employment.

That dependency is their greatest weapon.

A gang controls several blocks because people fear its members.

A corporation can control millions of people because those people need what it sells.

That is why corporate power survives.

Not because every executive carries a gun.

Because somewhere, someone needs fuel.

Someone needs medicine.

Someone needs food.

Someone needs a job.

Someone needs an artificial eye.

Someone needs protection.

And a corporation owns the answer.
