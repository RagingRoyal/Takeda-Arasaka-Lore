# LORE-BOOK: HISTORY OF NORTH AMERICA AND THE DARK FUTURE

# FROM THE COLLAPSE TO 2077

## CANON SCOPE

This lore-book describes the history leading into Cyberpunk 2077, with particular emphasis on North America.

The Cyberpunk setting has existed across several tabletop editions before Cyberpunk 2077.

Some details from older material have been revised over time.

For roleplay purposes, this lore-book prioritizes the timeline established by:

Cyberpunk RED.

Cyberpunk 2077.

Phantom Liberty.

Older Cyberpunk 2020 material is used where it remains compatible with the modern continuity.

This distinction matters because history inside the Cyberpunk universe is itself unreliable.

Governments lie.

Corporations rewrite records.

Media organizations manipulate narratives.

Classified operations become conspiracy theories.

Wars are remembered differently depending on who survived them.

Two characters can therefore disagree about history without either necessarily being stupid.

# THE CENTRAL FACT OF MODERN HISTORY

The world of 2077 did not emerge from one apocalypse.

It emerged from a chain reaction.

Economic collapse weakened governments.

Weak governments lost territory.

Corporations replaced government services.

Environmental disasters created refugees.

Refugees became Nomads.

Wars created new technologies.

Technology empowered corporations.

Corporations fought wars.

Corporate warfare destroyed international infrastructure.

The global NET collapsed.

The world rebuilt locally.

Governments attempted to restore old borders.

Corporations became entangled with those governments.

By 2077, the map may still contain familiar names.

United States.

California.

Texas.

Canada.

Mexico.

But the political reality behind those names is radically different.

# BEFORE THE COLLAPSE

By the late twentieth century, the United States remained a major world power but had accumulated serious internal problems.

Economic inequality was rising.

Government institutions were increasingly influenced by secretive intelligence organizations.

Corporations were growing enormously powerful.

Environmental deterioration was accelerating.

Crime and political extremism increased.

The United States became involved in increasingly aggressive foreign operations.

At the same time, Europe was beginning to reorganize into a powerful economic bloc.

# THE EUROPEAN CHALLENGE

In the early 1990s, European states established what would become a powerful European economic community.

The Eurodollar emerged as a major international currency.

This represented a serious challenge to American economic dominance.

Elements inside the United States government reacted badly.

Instead of adapting peacefully to a multipolar world, powerful members of America's intelligence and political establishment began conducting covert operations intended to weaken foreign competitors.

# THE GANG OF FOUR

One of the most important organizations in early Cyberpunk history is commonly called the Gang of Four.

The name refers to a secretive alliance involving powerful elements of the American intelligence establishment and government.

The organizations most heavily associated with it included elements of:

The CIA.

The NSA.

The DEA.

The FBI.

The exact structure changed over time.

Its importance did not.

The Gang of Four increasingly operated beyond normal democratic oversight.

Its members manipulated foreign governments.

Ran illegal operations.

Controlled political figures.

Manipulated information.

Interfered with financial markets.

Used military power to pursue their own agendas.

The United States still possessed elections and institutions on paper.

Behind those institutions, unelected networks increasingly controlled important decisions.

# THE FIRST CENTRAL AMERICAN WAR

From approximately 1990 to 1994, the United States became involved in a major conflict in Central America.

American operations expanded through:

Panama.

Nicaragua.

Honduras.

El Salvador.

The conflict was tied to American attempts to maintain regional influence and control strategic assets such as the Panama Canal.

It also intersected with the American government's war against narcotics.

The United States deployed increasingly advanced military technology.

Early combat cyberware.

Smart weapons.

Aerodynes.

Mechanized infantry.

Cybernetically augmented soldiers.

Much of the military technology considered ordinary by 2077 traces part of its development to these wars.

# THE ANTI-DRUG BIOWAR

The American war on narcotics escalated beyond police operations.

The DEA developed biological agents intended to destroy plants used in narcotics production.

The effects spread through parts of Central and South America.

Entire agricultural economies were damaged.

Local populations suffered.

Cartels adapted.

Governments destabilized.

The policy succeeded at destroying some traditional drug production while simultaneously helping create enormous political instability.

Synthetic drugs eventually replaced many plant-based narcotics anyway.

It was a remarkably Cyberpunk solution:

Technologically impressive.

Politically catastrophic.

Ultimately incapable of solving the underlying problem.

# THE QUIET WAR

During the early 1990s, American intelligence agencies also fought an undeclared economic and intelligence conflict against the emerging European economic bloc.

This became known as the Quiet War.

Instead of tanks and infantry, much of the conflict involved:

Currency manipulation.

Stock-market manipulation.

Espionage.

Disinformation.

Computer intrusion.

Political interference.

Covert operations.

The Gang of Four attempted to make the United States appear economically stronger than it actually was.

That deception eventually exploded.

# THE CRASH OF 1994

The Europeans discovered American manipulation of international financial markets.

The information became public.

Confidence collapsed.

Markets crashed.

International trade was disrupted.

Foreign governments imposed economic pressure on the United States.

American financial institutions failed.

Investments disappeared.

Pensions vanished.

Savings were destroyed.

Businesses closed.

Unemployment exploded.

Millions of Americans who believed they were financially secure discovered that their wealth effectively no longer existed.

This became the Crash of '94.

The Crash was the beginning of the modern American Collapse.

# THE COLLAPSE OF THE UNITED STATES

The period generally called the Collapse consumed the United States during the late 1990s and early 2000s.

Economic disaster interacted with environmental catastrophe, disease, political corruption, crime, and institutional failure.

Government services began disappearing.

Municipal governments went bankrupt.

Police departments became overwhelmed.

Federal assistance programs collapsed.

Infrastructure deteriorated.

Food distribution became inconsistent.

Millions became homeless.

People who had spent their entire lives believing that the state would continue functioning suddenly found themselves living in a country where the state might not appear at all.

# THE DEATH OF THE SOCIAL SAFETY NET

The disappearance of federal support was devastating.

Programs involving:

Healthcare.

Food assistance.

Unemployment.

Retirement.

Housing.

Subsidized agriculture.

Public infrastructure.

Disaster relief.

All became unreliable or disappeared entirely in many areas.

People who could survive independently did so.

People who could form communities did so.

People who could not were frequently abandoned.

# THE RISE OF THE MEGACORPORATIONS

Corporations entered the vacuum.

Where government could no longer provide security, corporations sold security.

Where government infrastructure failed, corporations offered private infrastructure.

Where public healthcare collapsed, corporations provided employee healthcare.

Where governments could not employ people, corporations hired them.

This massively increased corporate legitimacy.

A citizen might distrust a megacorporation.

That same citizen might depend on the corporation for:

Food.

Employment.

Housing.

Medicine.

Security.

Electricity.

Transportation.

The corporations did not need to abolish government.

They only needed government to become weak enough that people depended upon corporate alternatives.

# 1996: MARTIAL LAW

By 1996, the situation had deteriorated severely.

President James Richard Allen was assassinated while campaigning in California.

Political chaos followed.

The United States entered martial law.

Military authority expanded dramatically.

The federal government reorganized military and security structures in an attempt to keep the country functioning.

Constitutional protections became increasingly theoretical.

Military courts and emergency powers became normal.

For ordinary Americans, government increasingly meant soldiers, checkpoints, rationing, and emergency orders.

# THE WARS OF SUCCESSION

Individual states began resisting federal authority.

Some refused federal taxes.

Others blocked interstate trade.

State governments increasingly relied upon:

National Guard units.

State militias.

Local security.

Corporate forces.

The federal military was repeatedly asked to force rebellious states back into compliance.

The military often refused.

American soldiers had little interest in invading American states on behalf of a government many of them no longer trusted.

This allowed the first Free States to emerge.

# THE FREE STATE IDEA

A Free State was not necessarily a completely independent country.

The term described states that had reclaimed enormous amounts of sovereignty from the federal government.

A Free State might maintain:

Its own military.

Its own border controls.

Independent taxation.

Independent trade policies.

Corporate agreements.

Foreign relationships.

Independent law.

Federal influence still existed in varying degrees.

But Washington no longer had automatic control.

# ENVIRONMENTAL DISASTER

Economic collapse was only part of the catastrophe.

North America experienced repeated environmental disasters.

Drought.

Wildfires.

Pollution.

Crop failures.

Flooding.

Severe storms.

Ecological collapse.

Industrial accidents.

Climate shifts.

Agricultural regions that had supported populations for generations became unreliable.

# THE MIDEAST MELTDOWN

In the late 1990s, warfare and nuclear destruction devastated large portions of the Middle East.

Global oil production collapsed.

Energy prices surged.

North America, already struggling economically, suffered another major shock.

This accelerated development of alternative fuels such as CHOOH2.

It also demonstrated how quickly modern civilization could become unstable when access to one strategic resource disappeared.

# THE DROUGHTS OF THE LATE 1990S

Large portions of the American agricultural interior suffered devastating drought.

Farms failed.

Rural towns disappeared.

Agricultural families lost land that had been held for generations.

Corporate agriculture increasingly replaced independent farming.

Millions migrated.

Some headed toward major cities.

Others joined growing communities of displaced people.

These displaced populations eventually became part of the Nomad movement.

# THE LOS ANGELES EARTHQUAKE

In 1998, an enormous earthquake devastated Los Angeles.

Tens of thousands died.

Large sections of the city were destroyed or flooded.

The disaster occurred while the country was already experiencing economic and political collapse.

Reconstruction became extraordinarily difficult.

Los Angeles survived.

It did not emerge unchanged.

# THE WASTING PLAGUE

In 1999, the Wasting Plague spread internationally.

Victims became unable to properly absorb nutrition and effectively starved regardless of food intake.

The disease devastated populations already suffering from failing healthcare systems.

Approximately fourteen million people died before an effective vaccine was developed.

The catastrophe reinforced an increasingly common belief:

Governments could no longer guarantee survival.

# THE BIRTH OF THE NOMADS

Nomads emerged from the millions of people displaced by:

Economic collapse.

Agricultural failure.

Environmental disaster.

War.

Corporate land purchases.

Urban violence.

Unemployment.

Destroyed towns.

At first, many were simply refugees.

Families packed their belongings into cars and trucks.

Groups combined for safety.

Mechanics became invaluable.

Truckers became invaluable.

People who understood logistics became invaluable.

Eventually those temporary caravans became permanent communities.

# NOMAD SOCIETY

Nomad groups developed their own:

Families.

Clans.

Nations.

Codes.

Trade networks.

Security systems.

Economic specializations.

They learned that movement itself could be power.

A government controls territory.

A corporation controls assets.

Nomads control routes.

# THE SEVEN NOMAD NATIONS

Historically, seven great Nomad Nations became particularly influential in North America:

The Aldecaldos.

The Jodes.

Snake Nation.

Folk Nation.

Blood Nation.

Thelas Nation.

MetaCorp.

Each developed different specialties and traditions.

Some transported goods.

Some specialized in construction.

Some operated at sea.

Some provided entertainment.

Some effectively became corporations.

The Seven Nations never became a single government.

They became a parallel civilization spread across roads, camps, ports, construction sites, and trade routes.

# THE ALDECALDOS

The Aldecaldos originated from displaced urban communities in the Los Angeles region.

They became one of North America's most famous Nomad Nations.

They specialized heavily in:

Transportation.

Construction.

Salvage.

Agricultural work.

Smuggling.

Mercenary operations.

By 2077, the Aldecaldos remain one of the best-known Nomad organizations in North America.

# THE JODES

The Jodes originated largely from farming communities displaced by the destruction of agricultural land across Oklahoma, Texas, Kansas, and neighboring regions.

Their history represents the original meaning of Nomad life:

People driven from home who eventually decide that the road itself will become home.

# THE LONG WALK

One of the greatest Nomad tragedies occurred after the Second Central American War.

When the conflict ended, the United States began withdrawing military personnel.

Hundreds of thousands of civilian contractors had also supported American forces.

Budget cuts meant the government refused to transport many of them home.

Approximately half a million contractors were effectively abandoned in Central and South America.

They began traveling north on their own.

This became the Long Walk.

Violence.

Disease.

Starvation.

Cartels.

Hostile terrain.

Border restrictions.

All took enormous numbers of lives.

Only a fraction of the original travelers successfully returned.

Many survivors became Nomads.

The abandonment became one of the defining betrayals remembered in Nomad culture.

# THE SECOND CENTRAL AMERICAN WAR

The Second Central American War began in 2003 and lasted until approximately 2010.

The United States intervened against countries including:

Colombia.

Ecuador.

Peru.

Venezuela.

The conflict became another enormously expensive military failure.

American troops fought guerrillas and conventional forces while European corporations supported opposing governments.

The United States possessed superior technology.

Technology did not provide an easy victory.

The war consumed money and lives while the country remained unstable at home.

# THE FALL OF THE GANG OF FOUR

In 2003, media investigations exposed major evidence concerning illegal intelligence operations connected to the Central American conflicts.

Public fury exploded.

Military personnel, civilians, and armed groups turned against the remaining Gang of Four leadership.

Political officials fled.

Some were captured.

Some were killed.

The secret system that had controlled large portions of American government collapsed.

This did not magically restore the country.

The institutions it had damaged remained damaged.

# THE RETURN OF ELECTIONS

By 2008, national elections resumed.

The most extreme phase of the Collapse was considered over.

The United States still technically existed.

But the old America was gone.

Millions were dead.

Millions more had become Nomads.

Numerous towns had been abandoned.

State governments possessed far more independence.

Corporations had become political powers.

The federal government was dramatically weaker than it had been before 1994.

# THE FIRST CORPORATE WAR

While governments weakened, corporations increasingly demonstrated that they were willing to wage actual wars.

The First Corporate War occurred between 2004 and 2006.

Its principal conflict involved Euro Business Machines and Orbital Air.

What began as a corporate acquisition dispute escalated into:

Sabotage.

Commando raids.

Orbital operations.

Armed attacks.

The war established a terrifying precedent.

Corporations could use soldiers against one another and survive politically.

# THE SECOND CORPORATE WAR

The Second Corporate War occurred between 2008 and 2010.

Its principal combatants were:

Petrochem.

SovOil.

The conflict focused heavily on energy resources in East Asia and the South China Sea.

Offshore facilities were attacked.

Corporate military forces fought for territory.

Oil infrastructure was destroyed.

Petrochem eventually withdrew from the contested region.

The lesson became clearer:

Corporate competition had become geopolitics.

# THE THIRD CORPORATE WAR

The Third Corporate War occurred in 2016.

It was principally fought between financial interests associated with:

Merrill, Asukaga & Finch.

The Rothstein Fund.

Lazarus Group became involved as military muscle.

Unlike previous corporate wars, most of the conflict occurred through the NET.

Financial systems were attacked.

Data was manipulated.

Netrunners fought.

Physical strikes targeted communications infrastructure.

The war demonstrated how completely global economics had become dependent upon cyberspace.

That dependency would become catastrophic only a few years later.

# CALIFORNIA BREAKS APART

California itself fractured during the post-Collapse era.

Political and economic tensions between northern and southern regions became increasingly severe.

Northern counties believed political power was concentrated around the enormous Los Angeles metropolitan region.

Conflict escalated.

Infrastructure connecting northern and southern California was destroyed.

The Northern Separation War followed.

# THE NORTHERN SEPARATION TREATY

In 2012, the conflict ended with the Northern Separation Treaty.

California was divided into:

Northern California.

Southern California.

Northern California became a Free State with Sacramento as its capital.

Southern California continued separately.

Night City became part of Northern California.

The division became one of the most important political borders in western North America.

# NIGHT CITY BEFORE THE FOURTH CORPORATE WAR

Night City had already become an unusually corporate metropolis.

Corporations possessed enormous political influence.

Gangs controlled many poorer areas.

Mercenaries operated openly.

Corporate forces were often more powerful than municipal authorities.

Night City was therefore perfectly positioned to become a battlefield when the largest corporations on Earth eventually turned against each other.

# THE FOURTH CORPORATE WAR

The Fourth Corporate War is the most important conflict in modern Cyberpunk history.

It devastated:

International trade.

Corporate power.

Global communications.

Cities.

Orbital infrastructure.

The global NET.

National governments.

The consequences created the world eventually seen in Cyberpunk 2077.

# THE WAR DID NOT BEGIN WITH MILITECH AND ARASAKA

One of the most important historical details is that the Fourth Corporate War did not initially begin as a conflict between Militech and Arasaka.

It started with a corporate bankruptcy.

# IHAG

Internationale Handelsmarine Aktiengesellschaft, usually abbreviated IHAG, was a major corporation involved in international shipping and ocean technology.

IHAG collapsed financially in 2021.

Its remaining assets were extremely valuable.

Two major ocean-focused corporations wanted them:

OTEC.

CINO.

# OTEC

Ocean Technology and Energy Corporation wanted control of IHAG assets.

Its rival wanted the same thing.

# CINO

Corporation Internationale Nauticale et Oceanique competed directly against OTEC.

Neither company could easily defeat the other financially.

So normal corporate competition became illegal corporate competition.

# THE OCEAN WAR

The two corporations began using:

Stock manipulation.

Espionage.

Sabotage.

Netrunners.

Mercenaries.

Military forces.

Neither could secure a decisive advantage.

Eventually they hired larger corporations to provide military support.

# OTEC HIRES MILITECH

OTEC contracted Militech.

Militech supplied:

Weapons.

Security.

Military advisers.

Special operations.

Combat personnel.

# CINO HIRES ARASAKA

CINO hired Arasaka.

Arasaka provided comparable military and intelligence capabilities.

The problem was immediate.

Militech and Arasaka were already long-term global rivals.

# THE PROXY WAR BECOMES PERSONAL

At first, neither megacorporation necessarily intended to begin a world war.

They were contractors.

Then personnel died.

Facilities were attacked.

Equipment was destroyed.

Accusations accumulated.

Retaliations escalated.

Saburo Arasaka and Militech leadership had their own longstanding hostility.

The original OTEC-CINO dispute became irrelevant.

Militech and Arasaka increasingly fought each other directly.

# THE END OF THE ORIGINAL DISPUTE

OTEC and CINO eventually reached a settlement.

Their corporate dispute ended.

Militech and Arasaka did not stop.

The hired soldiers had decided to continue the war after the clients went home.

# THE SHADOW WAR

The next stage became known as the Shadow War.

Militech and Arasaka conducted covert operations against one another.

Sabotage.

Assassination.

Espionage.

Soulkiller attacks.

Netrunner operations.

Data theft.

Proxy attacks.

Corporate infiltration.

The corporations attempted to damage each other without crossing the line into obvious global warfare.

They failed.

# THE HOT WAR

By the middle of 2022, the Shadow War became the Hot War.

Corporate facilities were openly attacked.

Factories were destroyed.

Mercenaries fought in cities.

Aircraft were deployed.

Orbital assets became targets.

Military-grade cyberweapons were released.

Cities such as Chicago, Los Angeles, Hong Kong, Rio de Janeiro, and Night City became battlegrounds.

The distinction between corporate warfare and international warfare disappeared.

# CORPORATE ARMIES

The Fourth Corporate War demonstrated just how powerful the megacorporations had become.

Militech and Arasaka possessed:

Professional infantry.

Special operations units.

Aircraft.

Naval vessels.

Submarines.

Orbital capabilities.

Netrunners.

Intelligence organizations.

Armored vehicles.

Corporate allies.

Mercenary forces.

Both companies could conduct military operations on multiple continents simultaneously.

# GOVERNMENTS LOSE CONTROL

At first, governments hesitated to intervene.

Militech and Arasaka were economically essential.

They supplied:

Weapons.

Security.

Infrastructure.

Employment.

Technology.

Government contracts.

Nationalizing or destroying them could cause additional economic collapse.

Eventually the destruction became impossible to ignore.

# WAR IN NORTH AMERICA

North America experienced extensive corporate fighting.

Corporate facilities became military targets.

Supply chains collapsed.

Weapon shortages appeared.

Transportation became unreliable.

Cities experienced battles between corporate troops.

Night City became one of the most important theaters because both Militech and Arasaka possessed enormous assets there.

# THE WAR IN CHICAGO

Chicago had already endured earlier disasters and reconstruction.

During the Fourth Corporate War, fighting between Militech and Arasaka threatened the city again.

Nomad groups ultimately intervened against both corporations in portions of the conflict.

This illustrates how powerful Nomad organizations had become.

They were no longer refugees.

Some could field forces capable of influencing corporate warfare.

# THE ORBITAL WAR

Corporate warfare spread into orbit.

Satellites.

Space stations.

Communications infrastructure.

Orbital weapons.

All became potential targets.

Debris accumulated.

Commercial launches became dangerous.

Insurance companies refused to cover orbital assets.

The war damaged infrastructure humanity had spent decades building.

# ENTER RACHE BARTMOSS

At approximately the same time the Fourth Corporate War was becoming global, another disaster began.

Rache Bartmoss was one of the greatest netrunners in history.

He hated corporate control of cyberspace.

He believed information should not remain imprisoned inside corporate datafortresses.

Bartmoss created a collection of programs intended to attack corporate networks following his death.

These became known as R.A.B.I.D.S.

# R.A.B.I.D.S.

R.A.B.I.D.S. stands for:

Roving Autonomous Bartmoss Interface Drones.

They were extremely sophisticated autonomous programs.

Bartmoss intended them to penetrate corporate datafortresses and release corporate information.

They operated through a dead-man system connected to Bartmoss himself.

When Bartmoss died, the programs activated.

# THE DEATH OF RACHE BARTMOSS

In 2022, corporate forces located Bartmoss.

He was killed.

His death activated the R.A.B.I.D.S.

The consequences were far beyond anything he had anticipated.

# THE DATAKRASH

The R.A.B.I.D.S. spread through the global NET.

They breached networks.

Corrupted information.

Released data.

Attacked systems.

Reproduced.

Mutated.

Large portions of global cyberspace became unstable.

Within months, the programs had infected an enormous percentage of the NET.

This disaster became known as the DataKrash.

# THE FOURTH CORPORATE WAR DID NOT CAUSE THE DATAKRASH

This distinction is important.

The Fourth Corporate War and DataKrash occurred at roughly the same time.

They interacted catastrophically.

But the DataKrash was triggered by Bartmoss's programs after his death.

It was not simply a Militech or Arasaka cyberattack.

# WHY THE WAR MADE EVERYTHING WORSE

The NET was already filled with military software.

Corporate AIs.

Black ICE.

Experimental viruses.

Cyberwarfare programs.

Automated defenses.

Militech and Arasaka released even more during the war.

When the DataKrash hit, all of these systems interacted.

Programs mutated.

AIs escaped restrictions.

Old networks became hostile environments.

The global NET transformed from infrastructure into wilderness.

# ROGUE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

Many powerful AIs had originally been created for:

Military operations.

Corporate research.

Network security.

Financial analysis.

Intelligence.

Cyberwarfare.

The DataKrash freed or transformed many of them.

Some became genuinely autonomous.

Some became hostile.

Some simply stopped obeying humans.

Over time, vast areas of cyberspace became inhabited by entities human netrunners could not safely control.

# THE OLD NET DIES

The destruction of the Old NET did not occur in one instant.

Instead:

Data became unreliable.

Network paths became dangerous.

R.A.B.I.D.S. spread.

Rogue AIs appeared.

Corporate cyberweapons remained active.

Netrunners died.

Infrastructure was physically destroyed during the war.

Eventually using the global NET became too dangerous.

By 2025, NetWatch officially declared the old network effectively unusable.

# THE WORLD LOSES THE INTERNET

This was one of the greatest technological reversals in human history.

Before the DataKrash, corporations could communicate globally almost instantly.

International banking depended upon network connections.

Shipping depended upon digital infrastructure.

Media depended upon the NET.

Governments depended upon it.

Then the network became hostile.

The world had advanced technology.

It suddenly lacked safe global connectivity.

# INFORMATION BECOMES PHYSICAL AGAIN

Data became something that occasionally had to be transported physically.

Hard drives.

Data shards.

Secure servers.

Courier systems.

Nomad convoys.

Private satellite networks.

Local networks.

This dramatically strengthened regional economies and logistics organizations.

# NATIONALIZATION OF THE CORPORATE ARMIES

By the later stages of the Fourth Corporate War, governments finally began directly attacking corporate independence.

The United States effectively nationalized Militech's military operations.

President Elizabeth Kress had strong historical connections with Militech.

Militech forces increasingly became extensions of American military power.

Japan moved against Arasaka assets.

European governments seized corporate property.

The war had become so destructive that governments could no longer tolerate private armies operating independently.

# ARASAKA'S NORTH AMERICAN STRONGHOLD

By 2023, Arasaka's most important remaining position in North America was Night City.

The corporation heavily fortified its headquarters.

Night City became surrounded by forces aligned against Arasaka.

The situation approached open siege.

# THE ARASAKA TOWER ASSAULT

On August 20, 2023, a major operation targeted the Arasaka Towers.

Multiple strike teams participated.

Important figures associated with the assault included:

Johnny Silverhand.

Rogue Amendiares.

Spider Murphy.

Morgan Blackhand.

Shaitan.

Nomad forces.

Militech and American personnel.

Different teams possessed different objectives.

# THE OBJECTIVES

One team sought to destroy Soulkiller-related technology.

Another targeted Arasaka's enormous data resources.

Other personnel provided tactical support.

A nuclear device was carried into the operation.

The intention appears to have involved detonating the weapon in a location where much of the blast would be contained underground.

That is not what happened.

# THE NIGHT CITY HOLOCAUST

The nuclear device detonated high inside the Arasaka complex.

The blast destroyed the towers.

Massive debris struck surrounding structures.

Gas lines exploded.

Electrical systems failed.

Fires spread.

The Corporate Center was devastated.

The radioactive and structural destruction killed enormous numbers of people.

Approximately half a million died during the initial catastrophe.

Hundreds of thousands more died in the immediate aftermath.

# WHO NUKE'D NIGHT CITY?

This remains one of the most politically distorted events in modern history.

The public American narrative blamed Arasaka.

The official story claimed Arasaka destroyed its own headquarters rather than allow Militech to capture it.

Later evidence established that the assault force carried a nuclear weapon supplied through the American/Militech operation.

Exactly how and why it detonated where it did remained disputed.

For roleplay purposes, characters may believe completely different versions depending upon:

Age.

Nationality.

Corporate loyalty.

Political affiliation.

Education.

Media consumed.

An Arasaka veteran may call it an American nuclear attack.

An NUSA loyalist may insist Arasaka destroyed its own tower.

A mercenary may believe everyone involved was lying.

# JOHNNY SILVERHAND'S ROLE

Johnny Silverhand participated in the assault.

Popular mythology greatly inflated his role.

Johnny became remembered as one of the central figures in the destruction of Arasaka Tower.

The actual operation was considerably larger and involved many elite combatants.

Morgan Blackhand, Rogue, Spider Murphy, military personnel, Nomads, and others played critical roles.

This is an excellent example of how Night City turns history into legend.

# THE END OF THE FOURTH CORPORATE WAR

The destruction of Arasaka's Night City position effectively ended its major American military presence.

American forces continued eliminating remaining Arasaka resistance.

Japan restricted Arasaka.

Militech became increasingly controlled by the American government.

By 2025, the final major pockets of corporate resistance had been eliminated.

The Fourth Corporate War was officially over.

Nobody had meaningfully won.

# THE CONSEQUENCES OF THE FOURTH CORPORATE WAR

International trade collapsed.

Global shipping became unreliable.

Entire corporate empires disintegrated.

National economies were devastated.

Cities were damaged.

Millions were displaced.

Orbital infrastructure was destroyed.

The NET became unusable.

Supply chains disappeared.

Manufacturing became regional.

The old megacorporate economic system temporarily broke apart.

# THE TIME OF THE RED

The decades following the Fourth Corporate War became known as the Time of the Red.

The name came from the sky.

Atmospheric debris from:

The Night City nuclear disaster.

City fires.

Orbital strikes.

Industrial destruction.

Conventional warfare.

Burning agricultural regions.

Created a red haze across enormous portions of the atmosphere.

For years, skies carried an unnatural crimson color.

Rain could pull red particulate from the air and produce greasy crimson precipitation sometimes called blood rain.

# THE RED DECADES

The Time of the Red lasted from the aftermath of the Night City disaster into the later 2040s.

This was not a total post-apocalyptic wasteland.

Civilization remained.

Cities remained.

Governments remained.

Corporations remained.

Technology remained.

What disappeared was the infrastructure connecting all of it reliably.

# LOCALIZATION

The old global economy fractured.

People increasingly depended upon local production.

Local corporations.

Local markets.

Local networks.

Regional manufacturing.

Nomad transport.

Goods that had once arrived overnight from another continent might become extremely rare.

# NOMADS BECOME THE LOGISTICS SYSTEM

Nomad clans became essential.

They already possessed:

Vehicles.

Mechanics.

Security.

Long-distance experience.

Independent communications.

Knowledge of dangerous roads.

Family-based organization.

They became some of the only groups capable of reliably moving cargo between cities.

The refugees of the Collapse became one of the pillars holding civilization together.

# NOMADS REBUILD AMERICA

Nomads participated heavily in:

City reconstruction.

Road repair.

Transportation.

Agriculture.

Construction.

Shipping.

Industrial salvage.

Many modern cities owe part of their survival to Nomad labor.

Urban populations may stereotype Nomads as criminals or smugglers.

The irony is that many of those cities might not exist without them.

# THE RESETTLEMENT MOVEMENT

Beginning in the late 2020s, communities began reclaiming locations abandoned during the Collapse and Fourth Corporate War.

People moved back into damaged cities.

Construction crews rebuilt infrastructure.

Nomad contractors restored roads.

Local governments re-established services.

Corporate investment gradually returned.

North America slowly crawled out of crisis.

# THE CITINETS

The destruction of the Old NET created a fundamental communications problem.

The solution was not immediately rebuilding another global network.

That seemed too dangerous.

Instead, cities developed isolated local networks called CitiNets.

# ZIGGURAT

The corporation Ziggurat became extremely important during the Time of the Red.

It developed local CitiNet infrastructure and associated Data Pools.

These systems allowed residents of individual cities to access:

Messages.

Media.

Local databases.

Entertainment.

Commercial services.

Applications.

Local information.

The networks were deliberately isolated from the dangerous Old NET.

# COMMUNICATION BETWEEN CITIES

Long-distance communication slowly returned through combinations of:

Old telecommunications infrastructure.

Satellites.

Data transfers.

Nomad couriers.

Network relays.

Private corporate communications.

The result was functional but nowhere near as seamless as the old global NET.

# NETWATCH

NetWatch survived the DataKrash.

Its purpose became more important than ever.

Humanity could not destroy everything remaining inside the Old NET.

So NetWatch eventually pursued containment.

# THE BLACKWALL PROJECT

During the 2040s, NetWatch secretly began developing what became known as the Blackwall.

The Blackwall separates the human-controlled sections of cyberspace from the dangerous regions dominated by rogue artificial intelligences and surviving hostile systems.

Despite the name, it is not simply a brick wall made of code.

The system itself possesses extraordinary intelligence.

It identifies and blocks entities attempting to cross between environments.

# THE PRICE OF THE BLACKWALL

Creating safe cyberspace required abandoning enormous portions of the old network.

Humanity lost access to:

Data.

Research.

Software.

Corporate archives.

Government records.

AI systems.

Decades of digital information.

Some of the greatest repositories of knowledge ever created remain somewhere beyond the Blackwall.

So do things capable of killing the people attempting to retrieve them.

# BY 2077

Human networking has become much more sophisticated again.

People can communicate across large distances.

Corporations maintain enormous networks.

The Net exists.

Netrunners operate constantly.

But beneath the usable network exists the poisoned ruin of the old one.

The Blackwall is the membrane separating civilization from it.

# THE UNITED STATES AFTER THE FOURTH CORPORATE WAR

The surviving federal government faced a nearly impossible task.

The country was fragmented.

Free States resisted Washington.

Infrastructure was destroyed.

Corporations remained powerful.

The government needed to rebuild legitimacy.

President Elizabeth Kress became central to that process.

# ELIZABETH KRESS

Kress had once been associated with Militech leadership before becoming President.

During and after the Fourth Corporate War, she centralized what remained of American federal power.

Her administration worked to:

Rebuild infrastructure.

Control Militech.

Suppress remaining corporate warfare.

Re-establish federal authority.

Reconstruct the economy.

Create a functioning national government.

Critics considered her rule authoritarian.

Supporters argued that without extreme control there would have been no country left to rebuild.

# MILITECH AND THE GOVERNMENT

Militech became deeply intertwined with American federal power.

This relationship never truly disappeared.

By 2077, separating NUSA strategic interests from Militech strategic interests can be extremely difficult.

Militech is simultaneously:

A corporation.

A military contractor.

An arms manufacturer.

A political institution.

An instrument of NUSA power.

# THE OLD UNITED STATES BECOMES THE NUSA

For years, the surviving federal government existed in an awkward transitional state.

The country was eventually formally reorganized.

On July 4, 2053, a new constitution was ratified.

The New United States of America officially emerged.

Usually abbreviated:

NUSA.

# THE NUSA

The NUSA claims continuity with the historical United States.

Its government insists that the divided American territories ultimately belong together.

Many Free States disagree.

By 2077, the NUSA remains far weaker internationally than the pre-Collapse United States.

It is still a major military power.

Militech ensures that.

# THE NUSA CAPITAL REGION

Federal power is concentrated heavily in the northeastern urban corridor.

The densely populated region stretching broadly between Boston and Washington forms the economic and political center of NUSA power.

It is sometimes referred to collectively as the Plex.

# PRESIDENT DAVID WHINDAM

Following the 2053 constitutional restructuring, David Whindam became the first elected president of the formal NUSA.

The restored democratic system remained deeply influenced by corporations.

The flag had returned.

The relationship between money and government had not changed much.

# ROSALIND MYERS

Rosalind Myers became President of the NUSA in 2065.

Before entering politics, Myers had been CEO of Militech.

This career path perfectly illustrates the relationship between the NUSA and its largest military corporation.

Myers is:

Ambitious.

Highly capable.

Nationalistic.

Aggressive.

Committed to reunification.

# THE FREE STATES AFTER THE WAR

The western Free States retained enormous autonomy.

# THE PACIFICA CONFEDERATION

# NIGHT CITY AND THE PACIFICA ECONOMY

# THE FEDERAL DREAM OF REUNIFICATION

# SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA CHOOSES THE NUSA

# 2069: THE UNIFICATION WAR

President Rosalind Myers pushed a formal reunification program.

War followed.

# THE NUSA SIDE

Militech support.

# THE FREE STATE SIDE

# ARASAKA'S ROLE

# THE PACIFIC NORTHWEST

Washington, Oregon, and Idaho attempted to remain outside the war.

# TEXAS

# FEDERAL ADVANCES

The NUSA initially performed extremely well.

By 2070, most of the active Free States were under severe pressure.

# BATTLE OF RIDGECREST

One of the bloodiest battles occurred at Ridgecrest in Southern California.

Thousands were killed in a single day.

The battle demonstrated that reunification would not be a clean political operation.

America was once again killing itself over who controlled the map.

# SACRAMENTO

Northern California became one of the final major centers of resistance.

Its capital, Sacramento, suffered aerial bombardment during the war.

The conflict increasingly threatened to spread directly into Night City.

# NIGHT CITY'S POSITION

Night City attempted to remain outside the war.

The city still belonged politically to Northern California but possessed enormous practical autonomy.

Its leadership feared both outcomes.

NUSA control threatened Night City's independence.

Open warfare threatened to destroy the city.

# LUCius RHYNE

Night City politician Lucius Rhyne became central to the city's survival.

Rhyne sought assistance from the one power capable of making the NUSA reconsider an invasion.

Arasaka.

# ARASAKA RETURNS

Arasaka answered Night City's request.

An Arasaka supercarrier entered Del Coronado Bay.

Suddenly the NUSA faced a choice.

Continue the offensive and risk direct warfare with Arasaka.

Or negotiate.

The memory of the Fourth Corporate War was still alive.

Nobody wanted another one.

# THE NUSA RETREATS

The presence of Arasaka dramatically changed the strategic calculation.

President Myers did not want a renewed global Arasaka-Militech war.

Federal forces backed away from Night City.

Negotiations began.

# THE TREATY OF UNIFICATION

The conflict ended in 2070 through the Treaty of Unification, also associated with the Arvin Accord.

The agreement produced a deliberately ambiguous political settlement.

The NUSA could claim that the Free States were now part of a renewed federal system.

The Free States retained enormous practical autonomy.

Everyone declared some version of victory.

# WHAT THE FREE STATES AGREED TO

The surviving Free States accepted greater formal participation in the federal political structure.

They also agreed to cease various hostilities.

In exchange, their local autonomy remained largely intact.

The result is why 2077 maps can appear confusing.

A territory can be nominally associated with the NUSA while functioning almost like a separate country.

# TEXAS REFUSES

Texas did not sign the Treaty of Unification.

The Republic of Texas retained full practical independence.

Relations with the NUSA remained hostile.

# NIGHT CITY BECOMES INDEPENDENT

Night City gained perhaps the most important outcome.

The city became a fully autonomous Free City.

By 2077, Night City is not legally governed by:

Southern California.

The NUSA.

It is its own city-state.

# THE PRICE OF NIGHT CITY'S INDEPENDENCE

Night City survived the NUSA offensive because Arasaka intervened.

Arasaka expected compensation.

The corporation was welcomed back into the city.

The decades-old prohibition on major Arasaka activity in North America effectively collapsed in Night City.

Arasaka rebuilt its headquarters.

Corporate influence over the city surged.

# THE NEW ARASAKA TOWER

A new Arasaka Tower rose from the city center.

It was constructed near the site associated with the corporation's previous destroyed headquarters.

Symbolically, the message was unmistakable.

Arasaka had survived exile.

It had returned.

It now stood inside North America because Night City wanted it there.

# DOGTOWN IS BORN

# COMMON HISTORICAL MISCONCEPTIONS

## "THE UNITED STATES WAS DESTROYED IN A NUCLEAR WAR."

Incorrect.

The United States collapsed primarily through:

Economic disaster.

Political corruption.

Environmental crisis.

Disease.

War.

Institutional failure.

Regional separatism.

Nuclear destruction occurred elsewhere and Night City suffered a nuclear disaster, but there was no single nuclear event that destroyed America.

## "THE FOURTH CORPORATE WAR WAS ALWAYS ARASAKA VS MILITECH."

Incorrect.

It began with OTEC and CINO fighting over IHAG assets.

Militech and Arasaka entered as contractors.

Their rivalry eventually consumed the original dispute.

## "THE FOURTH CORPORATE WAR DESTROYED THE NET."

Partially incorrect.

The war heavily damaged cyberspace and filled it with dangerous cyberweapons.

The DataKrash caused by Rache Bartmoss and the R.A.B.I.D.S. was a separate event occurring during the same period.

Together they rendered the Old NET effectively unusable.

## "BARTMOSS JUST TURNED OFF THE INTERNET."

Incorrect.

His programs spread through it.

They breached corporate systems.

Corrupted data.

Freed or destabilized AIs.

The infrastructure did not simply disappear.

It became hostile.

## "THE BLACKWALL WAS BUILT IMMEDIATELY."

Incorrect.

Humanity spent years attempting to live with the consequences of the DataKrash.

The Blackwall project emerged later, during the 2040s.

## "ARASAKA NUKE'D ITS OWN TOWER."

That is the historical NUSA version.

The assaulting American/Militech operation carried a nuclear device.

The exact events surrounding its detonation were deliberately obscured afterward.

## "JOHNNY SILVERHAND SINGLE-HANDEDLY DESTROYED ARASAKA TOWER."

No.

Johnny participated in one team within a far larger operation.

His legend eventually overshadowed many of the other participants.

## "NIGHT CITY IS IN THE NUSA."

Geographically, Night City lies on former American territory.

Politically, Night City is an independent city-state by 2077.

## "NIGHT CITY IS PART OF NORTHERN CALIFORNIA."

It was.

After the Unification War, it became fully autonomous.

## "THE FREE STATES ARE ALL COMPLETELY INDEPENDENT COUNTRIES."

Not exactly.

Most accepted the Treaty of Unification and formally participate in elements of the new federal order.

In practice they remain highly autonomous.

Texas is the major exception because it refused the treaty.

## "ALL WESTERN STATES FOUGHT AGAINST THE NUSA."

No.

Southern California and Utah aligned with the NUSA.

Washington, Oregon, and Idaho remained largely neutral during the Unification War.

The main Free State military alliance consisted of Northern California, Arizona, Colorado, Montana, Nevada, New Mexico, and Wyoming.

## "THE NUSA IS JUST THE OLD UNITED STATES WITH A NEW NAME."

No.

The NUSA is the political descendant of the United States.

It possesses different borders.

Different power relationships.

A different constitution.

Much weaker global influence.

Far deeper integration with corporations such as Militech.

# ROLEPLAY PRINCIPLE: HISTORY IS RECENT

The Fourth Corporate War is not ancient history.

A person in their seventies during 2077 may personally remember the Time of the Red.

Middle-aged characters may have grown up hearing stories from parents who rebuilt cities after the war.

The Unification War ended only seven years before 2077.

Veterans are everywhere.

Politicians involved remain in power.

Soldiers involved remain active.

The border infrastructure remains visible.

Dogtown exists because of it.

The political wounds are extremely fresh.

# ROLEPLAY PRINCIPLE: THE COLLAPSE CREATED MODERN CULTURE

Nomad culture exists because of the Collapse.

Corporate citizenship exists partly because governments failed.

Private security expanded because police failed.

Megacorporations gained legitimacy because governments could not provide basic services.

The modern world cannot be understood without remembering that millions of people watched public institutions fail in front of them.

# ROLEPLAY PRINCIPLE: AMERICANS DO NOT ALL SHARE ONE NATIONAL IDENTITY

A Night Citizen may have little emotional loyalty to the NUSA.

A Texan may openly despise Washington.

A Northern Californian may consider NUSA troops foreign occupiers.

A Southern Californian may view Free States as separatists.

A NUSA soldier may genuinely believe they are defending one country divided by criminals and corporations.

A Nomad may think the entire argument is ridiculous.

# ROLEPLAY PRINCIPLE: CORPORATE LOYALTY CAN REPLACE PATRIOTISM

An Arasaka employee may care more about Arasaka than Japan.

A Militech employee may see the NUSA and corporation as effectively the same cause.

A Biotechnica scientist may consider national borders irrelevant.

A corporate citizen may move internationally without ever emotionally leaving the corporation.

# ROLEPLAY PRINCIPLE: THE OLD NET IS HAUNTED TERRITORY

Characters should not treat the Old NET as an abandoned website.

It is digital wilderness.

Entering uncontrolled regions can mean encountering entities created decades earlier that have continued evolving ever since.

A program abandoned in 2022 has had fifty-five years to become something else.

# ROLEPLAY PRINCIPLE: OLD DATA IS TREASURE

Pre-DataKrash archives can contain:

Lost technology.

Corporate secrets.

Government intelligence.

Bank accounts.

Military research.

Personal records.

AI research.

Blackmail.

Patents.

A functioning server containing intact data from before 2022 can be worth enormous amounts of money.

# ROLEPLAY PRINCIPLE: EVERY BORDER HAS A STORY

Crossing a border should communicate history.

Southern California to Northern California:

NUSA versus Free State.

Northern California to Night City:

State sovereignty versus city independence.

Night City into the Badlands:

Corporate metropolis versus open territory.

NUSA to Texas:

A political wound waiting to reopen.

# ROLEPLAY PRINCIPLE: THE CORPORATE WARS CHANGED HOW PEOPLE VIEW MEGACORPS

Everyone knows corporations can become military powers.

The Fourth Corporate War proved they can destabilize the entire planet.

This is why governments fear another uncontrolled corporate conflict.

It is also why corporations increasingly prefer:

Proxy warfare.

Mercenaries.

Espionage.

Assassination.

Economic warfare.

Deniable operations.

Open war is bad for quarterly earnings.

# ROLEPLAY PRINCIPLE: THE WORLD REBUILT, IT DID NOT RECOVER

By 2077, the world is technologically extraordinary again.

That does not mean the old civilization returned.

A different civilization grew from the wreckage.

Its institutions are different.

Its borders are different.

Its economics are different.

Its relationship with technology is different.

Its definition of citizenship is different.

The world did not heal back into what it was.

It scarred into something new.

# FINAL HISTORICAL PRINCIPLE

The central story of North American history in Cyberpunk is the transfer of power.

First, power belonged primarily to governments.

Then governments failed.

States took power.

Corporations took power.

Nomads built power outside both.

The federal government rebuilt.

Corporations entered government.

The government tried to restore its borders.

Other governments resisted.

By 2077, nobody has achieved total victory.

The NUSA possesses armies but not the whole nation.

The Free States possess autonomy but live beneath constant threat.

Texas possesses independence but prepares for war.

Night City possesses freedom because corporations make conquest too dangerous.

Nomads possess no conventional country but travel across everyone else's.

Megacorporations possess no traditional nation yet can decide the outcome of wars.

And beyond the Blackwall, humanity has created powers that may eventually consider the entire argument irrelevant.

North America in 2077 is therefore not the aftermath of history.

It is history between wars.
